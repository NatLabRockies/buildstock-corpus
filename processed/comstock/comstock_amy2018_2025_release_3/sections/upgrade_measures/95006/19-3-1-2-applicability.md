## 3.1.2 Applicability

The HP-RTU upgrade is applicable to ComStock models with either gas furnace RTUs (PSZ-AC with gas coil) or electric resistance RTUs (PSZ-AC with electric coil). ComStock HVAC distributions are informed by the CBECS (2012 and 2018 data combined). The methodology for interpreting CBECS data to create HVAC probability distributions for ComStock is discussed in the ComStock documentation [2]. This methodology is especially impactful to aggregated results as it determines how many buildings are applicable to the upgrade and therefore how much stock potential the upgrade has. The upgrade does not apply to space types that directly serve kitchens, spaces that are unconditioned, or RTUs with outdoor air ratios above 55%. Overall, this upgrade applies to about 36% of the ComStock floor area (Figure 3).

Figure 3. Horizontal bar chart of the ComStock HVAC system type distribution, with two value columns per system type: Stock floor area from total [%] and Total stock site energy from total [%], both 0% to about 24%. Bars are colored by Applicability to this measure (blue = False, orange = True). PSZ-AC with gas coil (24.23% floor / 23.96% energy) and PSZ-AC with electric coil (11.99% / 10.16%) are the two applicable (orange) types the HP-RTU scenario applies to, together about 36% of stock floor area. Other (non-applicable) types include PVAV with gas boiler reheat (9.68%), PVAV with PFP boxes (9.08%), PVAV with gas heat with electric reheat (6.61%), and a long tail down to 'All other types (<1% prevalence)' at 2.42%.

Note: PTHP stands for packaged terminal heat pump, PVAV stands for packaged variable air volume, DOAS stands for dedicated outdoor air system, PSZ-AC stands for packaged single zone air conditioner, and PFP stands for parallel fan-power.
