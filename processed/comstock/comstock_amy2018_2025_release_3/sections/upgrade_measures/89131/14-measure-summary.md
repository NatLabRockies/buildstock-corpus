## Measure Summary

| Measure Title      | Packaged Water-to-Air Geothermal Heat Pump                                                                                           |
|--------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Measure Definition | This measure replaces existing packaged rooftop systems with packaged rooftop water-to-air heat pumps tied to a ground-coupled loop. |
| Applicability      | Models with gas-fired or electric resistance packaged rooftop unit (RTU) systems or packaged variable air volume (PVAV) systems.     |
| Not Applicable     | Models that do not contain gas-fired or electric resistance packaged RTUs or PVAVs.                                                  |
| Release            | 2024 Release 1: 2024/comstock_amy2018_release_1/                                                                                     |
