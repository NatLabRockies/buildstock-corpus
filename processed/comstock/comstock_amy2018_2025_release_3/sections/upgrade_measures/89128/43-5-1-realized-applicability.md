## 5.1 Realized Applicability

Figure 7 provides a breakdown of the applicability of the HP-RTU + ASHP Boiler + DCV + HR + Economizers package by individual measure. The individual component measures were applicable to between 29% and 78% of the stock floor area. Heat Recovery had the highest applicability at 78%. The ASHP-Boiler measure had the lowest applicability at 29% of the floor area, which is to be expected because of the prevalence of boilers in the baseline.

Figure 7: horizontal bar chart of the fraction of floor area applicable to each individual measure in the package, values printed on an axis running 0 to 1: HR 0.78, DCV 0.71, Economizer 0.57, HP-RTU 0.33 and HP-Boiler 0.29. Section 5.1 reads these as 29% to 78% applicability, with heat recovery highest and the ASHP boiler lowest.

The package was applicable to 85% of the stock floor area, meaning at least one measure was applicable. Approximately 15% of the stock was not applicable to any of the five measures. Figure 8 shows the measure combinations with the highest applicability by fraction of total floor area. The most common combination of measures was the HP-RTU retrofit + DCV + Heat Recovery + Economizers, which was appliable to 31% of the stock by floor area. There were 15 additional measure combinations observed in the results that are not shown, but each were applicable to under 2% of the stock by floor area.
