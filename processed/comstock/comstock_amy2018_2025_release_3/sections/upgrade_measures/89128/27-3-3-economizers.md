## 3.3 Economizers

This measure enables dry-bulb or enthalpy-based air-side economizing in air systems depending on climate zone if not already present. The measure cycles through air loops checking for eligibility for the upgrade. If an air loop does not have outdoor air or already has a DOAS system or existing economizer, it is not eligible for the upgrade. For air loops that are eligible for the upgrade, the model gets the climate zone and sets the economizer type (FixedDryBulb, FixedEnthalpy, or FixedDewPointAndDryBulb) based on ASHRAE 90.1 requirements by climate zone. The measure then adds economizers to eligible air loops. More details are described in subsequent sections.
