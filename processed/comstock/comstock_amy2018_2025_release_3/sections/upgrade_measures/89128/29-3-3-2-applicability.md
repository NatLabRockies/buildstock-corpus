## 3.3.2 Applicability

The applicability criterion of the economizer upgrade is simple: Economizers will be installed in buildings including air system with ventilation (i.e., AHUs and RTUs) that do not have economizers. Figure 4 shows the coverage of baseline buildings on floor area and highlights the economizer control types including floor area without economizers. The economizer upgrade will target the floor area without economizers shown in the figure for corresponding HVAC systems; however, the amount of savings will be determined by the weather, building's outdoor air requirement, heat gain level in the return air stream, and configuration of the economizer.
