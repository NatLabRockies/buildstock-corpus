## 4.1  Applicability

Figure 3 shows the heating type distribution among the ComStock buildings. This measure is applicable for buildings that use gas boiler as a heating source, which accounts for 33% of the ComStock baseline total floor area. The orange bar in the figure indicates portions for the applicable buildings. Out of the buildings with boiler, this measure is applicable for 94% of them; the rest are boilers used for supplemental heating for heat pump applications.
