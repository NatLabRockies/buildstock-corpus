## 6.6  Measure Impact by Building Type

Figure 18 shows the impact of the measure by building type. Hospitals, primary schools, and secondary schools are the three building types with the most site energy savings, whereas quickservice restaurants exhibit the least. The higher savings in these three buildings is attributed to their HVAC system type as well as their relatively higher heating loads. Only ~1% of the ComStock quick-service restaurant buildings use boiler and the rest use furnace, electric resistance and ASHPs, which resulted in lower savings from application of this measure. In contrast, 63% of hospitals, primary schools and secondary schools use boiler which attributed to the observed higher site energy savings.
