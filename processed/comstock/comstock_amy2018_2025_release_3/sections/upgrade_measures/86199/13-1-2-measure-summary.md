## 1.2  Measure Summary

| Measure Title      | Replace Boiler by Air-Source Heat Pump Boiler (replace_boiler_by_heatpump)                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
|--------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Measure Definition | This measure replaces a natural gas boiler by an air-source heat pump for space heating.                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Applicability      | Buildings that use natural gas boiler for HVAC system.                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Not Applicable     | Buildings that do not use natural gas boiler for space heating such as those with furnace, electric heaters, or district heat source. Boilers used for domestic hot water heating and supplemental heating in heat pump condenser loops will not be changed in this measure. |
| Release            | 2023 Release 1                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
