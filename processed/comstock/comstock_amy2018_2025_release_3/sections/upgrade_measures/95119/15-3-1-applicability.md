## 3.1  Applicability

The HP-RTU measure is applicable to ComStock models with either gas furnace RTUs ('PSZAC with gas coil') or electric resistance RTUs ('PSZ-AC with electric coil'). This accounts for about 36% of the ComStock floor area (Figure 1). ComStock HVAC distributions are informed by CBECS 2012. The methodology for interpreting CBECS data to create HVAC probability distributions for ComStock is discussed in the ComStock documentation [6]. The measure is not applicable to space types that directly serve kitchens, spaces that are unconditioned, or RTUs with outdoor air ratios above 55%.

Figure 1 is a three-panel horizontal bar chart sharing one set of row labels, one row per ComStock HVAC system type sorted by prevalence. The three panels are "% Stock Floor Area", "Stock Floor Area (ft2)", and "Stock Number of Buildings". A legend titled "upgrade_applicable" with False and True entries colors each bar by whether the HP-RTU measure applies. Rows top to bottom with percent of stock floor area: PSZ-AC with gas coil 24%; PSZ-AC with electric coil 12%; PVAV with gas boiler reheat 10%; PVAV with PFP boxes 9%; VAV chiller with gas boiler reheat 7%; VAV with gas heat with gas boiler reheat 5%; VAV air-cooled chiller with gas boiler reheat 4%; Residential AC with residential forced air furnace 4%; PTHP 3%; PTAC with gas coil 3%; PSZ-HP 3%; VAV chiller with PFP boxes 3%; VAV district chilled water with district hot water reheat 2%; PTAC with gas boiler 2%; then eight types at 1% each including DOAS variants, VAV chiller with district hot water reheat, PTAC with electric coil, and All Other Types (<1% Prevalence). The two PSZ-AC rows are shaded distinctly from the rest: per the surrounding text those are exactly the applicable types, and 24% + 12% = the 36% of stock floor area this measure reaches. The largest row also carries about 15.1 billion square feet and 1.89 million buildings; the remaining absolute values are dense and are not transcribed here.

This measure scenario is applicable to the 'PSZ-AC with gas coil' and 'PSZ-AC with electric coil' system types. PSZ-AC= packaged single zone air conditioner; PVAV = packaged variable air volume; PFP = parallel fan powered box; PTHP = packaged terminal heat pump; DOAS = dedicated outdoor air system
