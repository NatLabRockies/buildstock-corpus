## 5.1  Single Building Measure Tests

In this section, we describe the operation behavior of a small, two-story office building in Alamosa County Colorado, climate zone 6B, to demonstrate the measure scenario application on a single building. The baseline model uses packaged RTUs with direct expansion cooling and gas furnace heating. Outdoor ventilation air is provided directly through the RTUs. Three scenarios are compared: the baseline, as previously described, the standard performance HPRTU retrofit scenario, and the HP-RTU retrofit scenario with added roof insulation. The existing roof yields an R-value of 19.61, and the R-value provided by the roof insulation measure yields a final R-value of R-33, as recommended by the AEDG.

Figure 2 compares the heating load of the HP-RTU measure scenario, with and without the added roof insulation measure applied. The sample RTU plotted is on the top floor of the building where there is an exterior roof area. The additional roof insulation provides some reduction in heating load compared to the HP-RTU scenario without any change to the roof insulation.

Table 5 compares the site HVAC energy usage of the baseline (with gas RTUs), the HP-RTU measure scenario, and the HP-RTU measure scenario with added roof insulation. First and foremost, all gas heating use is removed in the two HP-RTU scenarios compared to the baseline. Both scenarios also show reductions in cooling and fan energy consumption as a result of replacing older, less-efficient RTUs with newer RTUs that have more efficient staged fan and cooling systems. Between the two HP-RTU scenarios, the addition of roof insulation provides slight reductions to all HVAC end uses shown. Note that roof insulation only impacts zone loads for the top floor of buildings with external roofs. The relative impact of added roof insulation will decrease for buildings with more floors, wherein the roof area is a smaller portion of the overall envelope. Alternatively, single-story buildings generally stand to gain the highest relative impact with roof insulation.
