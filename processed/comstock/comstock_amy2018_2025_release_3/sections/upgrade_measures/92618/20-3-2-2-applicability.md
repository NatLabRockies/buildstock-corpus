## 3.2.2 Applicability

The roof insulation upgrade applies to all roof assemblies with R-values below the target AEDG R-values, and for this measure package, to buildings that apply to the HP-RTU measure. For ComStock, only roofs that follow the ComStock 90.1-2013 energy code during the last roof replacement (or installation) have R-values that meet or exceed the AEDG suggestions.
