Figure A-1. Grouped bar chart of Natural Gas Total Energy Consumption (TBtu) by U.S. census division, y-axis 0 to about 380. Three bars per division (Baseline, HP RTU Std Perf, Std Perf w Roof) for New England, Middle Atlantic, East North Central, West North Central, South Atlantic, East South Central, West South Central, Mountain, and Pacific. East North Central is the largest (baseline about 380, falling to roughly 290 for the measure scenarios), followed by Middle Atlantic and Pacific. Both measure scenarios reduce natural gas below baseline in every division as gas heating is electrified, with the roof variant slightly lower than HP-RTU-only.

Note: tBtu = trillion British thermal units
