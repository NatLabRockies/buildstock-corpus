## 5.1 Realized Applicability

Figure 3 provides a breakdown of the applicability of the LED Lighting + HP-RTU Standard Performance + ASHP Boiler package by observed measure combinations. The most common observed measure combination was the LED Lighting upgrade only, followed by the LED Lighting measure combined with one of the other heat pump upgrades. At least one measure from the package was applicable to 90% of the stock floor area, leaving 10% of the stock not applicable to any upgrade.
