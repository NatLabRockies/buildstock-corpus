## 5.7  Building Type Impacts

Figure 10 and Figure 11 show the impacts of the LED Lighting + HP-RTU Standard Performance + ASHP Boiler upgrade package on natural gas and electricity consumption across the building types in the stock. The building types that showed the highest natural gas savings are buildings in which RTUs or boilers are common, including retail, offices, schools, hospitals, outpatient, and warehouses. These building types were more likely to receive the HP-RTU or ASHP Boiler upgrades because of the HVAC system distributions in the baseline. Therefore, these building types resulted in large natural gas savings from switching to electric heating.

Restaurants and hotels had the lowest natural gas savings. Restaurants have large natural gas loads from gas-fired cooking and water heating equipment, so a large portion of their natural gas load is not eliminated through this upgrade package. Hotels have a relatively higher portion of packaged terminal air-conditioner systems, which are not eligible for either the HP-RTU or ASHP Boiler measure. Therefore, many of the hotel models only received the LED Lighting measure, which has a minimal effect on natural gas consumption.

Package 3 is dramatically lower for every type (e.g. SecondarySchool falls to roughly 30) as space heating is fuel-switched away from natural gas.

When looking at electricity savings by building type, some buildings see a reduction in electricity while others see an increase. Schools, hospitals, and large offices showed an increase in electricity. These larger building types are more likely to begin with boiler systems and receive the ASHP Boiler upgrade. Because this upgrade only modifies the heating system, there are no cooling efficiency improvements to offset the added electric heating load. Buildings that received the HP-RTU measure, the LED Lighting measure, or both, see cooling and lighting savings, which can often cancel out the added electric heating load and result in net electricity savings.
