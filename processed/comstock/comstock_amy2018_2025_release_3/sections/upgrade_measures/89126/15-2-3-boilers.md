## 2.3 Boilers

The current version of boilers in ComStock is gas-fired, noncondensing boilers. The efficiencies of the boilers are assigned based on the U.S. Department of Energy's reference buildings templates and capacities; Table 3 summarizes the values [2]. As indicated in Table 3, three different performance curves were used to adjust the efficiency of the boiler based on part-load ratio. All ComStock boilers have a heating set point of 180°F with a capacity to modulate flow.
