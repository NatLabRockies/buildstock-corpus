## 5.3 Stock Greenhouse Gas Emissions Impact

ComStock simulation results show greenhouse gas emissions avoided across all three electricity grid scenarios and all on-site combustion fuel types (Figure 5). When combining all fuel types, Package 3 resulted in 48 to 50 million metric tons (MMT) of carbon dioxide equivalent (CO2e) avoided (11% -20% reduction) depending on the grid scenario. The emissions from natural gas are reduced by 60 MMT CO2e (61%) as a result of fuel switching much of the heating end use. The natural gas results remain consistent across all three grid scenarios, as these scenarios exclusively pertain to modifications within the electricity grid, without affecting natural gas outcomes.

Electricity greenhouse gas emissions avoided from the electricity grid vary depending on the scenario chosen; some scenarios show increased electricity emissions and others show reduced electricity emissions. This is because the scenarios project different levels of renewable energy penetration to the grid. For Package 3, the LRMER Low RE and High RE scenario show a 12 MMT CO2e and 11 MMT CO2e increase in electricity emissions, respectively. The eGRID scenario shows 12 MMT CO2e of avoided greenhouse gas emissions from the electricity grid.

Compared to Package 2, the higher performance HP-RTU package, Package 3, demonstrates the same reduction in natural gas emissions. However, the stock electricity emissions are 5 -10 MMT CO2e higher than Package 2, which is due to the lower cooling savings and higher electric heating penalty compared to Package 2.
