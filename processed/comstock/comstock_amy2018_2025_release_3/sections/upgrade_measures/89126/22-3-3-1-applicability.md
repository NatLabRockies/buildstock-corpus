## 3.3.1 Applicability

This upgrade is applicable to the ComStock OpenStudio ®  models with the following HVAC system types:

- DOAS with fan coil air-cooled chiller with boiler
- DOAS with fan coil chiller with boiler
- DOAS with fan coil district chilled water with boiler
- DOAS with water-source heat pump cooling tower with boiler
- PSZ-AC with gas boiler
- Packaged variable air volume with gas boiler
- Packaged variable air volume with gas heat with electric reheat
- Variable air volume air-cooled chiller with gas boiler reheat
- Variable air volume chiller with gas boiler reheat
- Variable air volume district chilled water with gas boiler reheat.

This upgrade is applicable to about 29% of the ComStock floor area.
