## 5.2 Stock Energy Impacts

The LED Lighting + HP-RTU Standard Performance + ASHP Boiler package demonstrates 17.8% total site energy savings (822 TBtu) for the U.S. commercial building stock modeled in ComStock. The savings contributions by end use and fuel type are summarized in Table 7.

Throughout the subsequent results sections, the results of the LED Lighting + HP-RTU Standard Performance + ASHP Boiler (referred to in figures as Package 3) will be compared to the baseline as well as the LED Lighting + HP-RTU + ASHP Boiler package (referred to in figures as Package 2). Package 2 and Package 3 differ in the performance levels of the HP-RTU measure; the other two measures in the package did not change. The savings contributions of the two packages by fuel type and end use are illustrated in Figure 4.

Compared to the baseline, the most notable savings from Package 3 are seen in interior lighting, which has been upgraded to LEDs, and natural gas heating, which has been eliminated in buildings with RTUs and boilers and replaced with a heat pump. As a result, stock-level electric heating increased by nearly 200% due to the added electric heat pump load. In addition, LEDs give off less heat to the space than fluorescent lights, so this also contributes to the added heating load. However, when all heating fuels are summed, the package still results in heating savings of 43%. Cooling and fans also saw 7% and 13% savings, respectively, as a result of buildings with RTUs switching to a higher efficiency heat pump compared to the existing systems.

Compared to Package 2, which uses a higher performance HP-RTU, Package 3 shows 2.0% (92 TBtu) lower total site energy savings. The main difference between the HP-RTU technologies modeled is the part-load performance, as the standard performance unit uses two-speed operation and the advanced performance unit uses variable-speed operation. More details about the comparison between the HP-RTU technologies modeled in Package 2 and 3 can be found in the HP-RTU Standard Performance measure documentation. By end use at the stock level, compared to Package 2, Package 3 demonstrated:

- No change in stock heating gas savings
- 12.6% higher stock heating electricity penalty (22.1 TBtu)
- 4.9% lower stock cooling electricity savings (34.3 TBtu)
- 6.3% lower stock fan electricity savings (35.2 TBtu).
