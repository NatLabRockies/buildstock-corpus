## 3.5 Limitations and Concerns

In general, real buildings may have limitations beyond what has been included in the upgrades' applicability criteria that could affect the upgrade results. The following subsection details the limitations and concerns for both heat pump upgrades. There are no major limitations or concerns associated with the LED Lighting upgrade.
