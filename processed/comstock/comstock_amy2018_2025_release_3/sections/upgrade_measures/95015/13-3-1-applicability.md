## 3.1  Applicability

Figure 4 shows the heating type distribution among the ComStock buildings. This measure is applicable to buildings that use gas boilers as a heating source, which accounts for 25% of the ComStock baseline floor area. In addition, there is one HVAC system that is classified as 'WSHP' as the heating type-'DOAS with water source heat pumps cooling tower with boiler'-which also uses a boiler for heating. This brings the total applicable floor area to approximately 26%.

Although buildings with boiler systems account for 26% of the floor area, they account for 56% of the natural gas heating consumption in ComStock. The breakdown of HVAC system types that are applicable to this measure and their respective percentages of floor area was shown in Table 1.

Figure 4 has two side-by-side horizontal bar panels sharing the row labels, under the heading "In.Hvac Heat Type". The left panel axis is "Total Weighted Square Footage (sqft)" as a percentage from 0% to about 40%, and the right panel axis is "Total Weighted Natural Gas Heating Energy (TBtu)" as a percentage from 0% to about 60%. Values are printed at the end of each bar. Rows top to bottom, given as floor area then natural gas heating energy: Furnace 35.2% and 43.8%; Electric Resistance 28.4% and 0.0%; Boiler 25.3% and 55.7%; ASHP (air-source heat pump) 6.2% and 0.0%; District 3.3% and 0.0%; GSHP (ground-source heat pump) 1.3% and 0.0%; WSHP (water-source heat pump) 0.3% and 0.5%. The point of the figure is the mismatch between the two panels: boilers serve only about a quarter of stock floor area but account for well over half of all natural gas heating energy, which is why this measure has outsized fuel impact relative to its applicability. The surrounding text notes that gas boilers cover 25% of baseline floor area, and adding the one WSHP system that also uses a boiler brings total applicability to roughly 26%.
