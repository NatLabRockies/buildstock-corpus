## 2.3  Envelope Baseline Assumptions

The EUL of the envelope components that this measure scenario pertains to (wall insulation, roof insulation, and windows) is very large: 200 years for walls and roofs and 70 years for windows (Table 2); therefore, the vast majority of buildings in ComStock are assumed to have the original walls, roof, and windows from when the building was built. This is often many code cycles behind what is required of new construction buildings in the state where that building is located.
