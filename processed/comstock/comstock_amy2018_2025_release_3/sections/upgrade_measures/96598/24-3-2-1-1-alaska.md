## 3.2.1.1  Alaska

Alaska has no statewide building energy code, so we need to assume an appropriate ASHRAE 90.1 version for this measure. According to BCAP, commercial buildings in Alaska follow the 2018 IECC, enhanced with amendments to cater to Alaska's harsh climate [15]; therefore, we assume 2018 IECC or ASHRAE 90.1-2016 for the state of Alaska.
