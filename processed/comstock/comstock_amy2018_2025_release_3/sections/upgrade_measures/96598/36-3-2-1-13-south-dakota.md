## 3.2.1.13 South Dakota

South Dakota has no statewide building energy code, so we need to assume an appropriate ASHRAE 90.1 version for this measure. According to ACEEE, most jurisdictions have adopted codes based on the 2015 IECC [21]; therefore, we assume ASHRAE 90.1-2013 for the state of South Dakota.
