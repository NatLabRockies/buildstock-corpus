## 3.2.1.5  Hawaii

According to BECP, Hawaii does not have a statewide energy code, but a review of codes in place in jurisdictions across the state indicates that more than 80% of the population is covered by codes at the 90.1-2016 level [4]. According to an ACEEE database, all four counties in the state have adopted IECC 2018 [21]. As such, we assume ASHRAE 90.1-2016 for the state of Hawaii.
