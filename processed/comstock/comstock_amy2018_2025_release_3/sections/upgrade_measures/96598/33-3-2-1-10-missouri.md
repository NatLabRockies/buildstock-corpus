## 3.2.1.10 Missouri

Missouri has no statewide building energy code, so we need to assume an appropriate ASHRAE 90.1 version for this measure. Although state-owned buildings are required to comply with the latest version of IECC, other commercial buildings do not have a mandatory code. According to ACEEE, approximately 50% of the state's population is covered by the 2009, 2012, 2015, or 2018 IECC or equivalent codes [21]. In the absence of other information, we conservatively assume ASHRAE 90.1-2007, for the state of Missouri.
