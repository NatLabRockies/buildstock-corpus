## 3.2.1  Generation of Standard Performance Data

This section describes the process of generating a new set of performance maps from available and public information that manufacturers provide. Note that manufacturer and model information are included based on what can be found from the internet. The National Renewable Energy Laboratory (NREL) does not endorse or support any particular brand, type, or model of equipment.
