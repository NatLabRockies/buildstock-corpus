Figure A-1: map of the United States shading counties by ASHRAE climate zone 1 through 8, divided by vertical lines into Marine (C), Dry (B) and Moist (A) columns with a Warm-Humid line across the Southeast. Insets note that Alaska is zone 7 except for eight listed zone 8 boroughs, and that zone 1 includes Hawaii, Guam, Puerto Rico and the Virgin Islands.
