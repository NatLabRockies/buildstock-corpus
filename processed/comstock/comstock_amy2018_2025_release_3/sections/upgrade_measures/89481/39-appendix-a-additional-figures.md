![Grouped bar chart of site annual natural gas consumption by census division for the ComStock baseline and the two heat pump RTU scenarios](89481_images/image_000016_e484e316d1a35911cf5c34b1f576d15d051be30ccb22fde1cd5c06c65bfaba32.png)

Figure A-1 plots site annual natural gas total energy consumption in TBtu, on a 0 to 350 axis, by census division, with three bars per division: Baseline (darkest), HP RTU E Backup, and HP RTU + ER (lightest). Divisions left to right: New England, Middle Atlantic, East North Central, West North Central, South Atlantic, East South Central, West South Central, Mountain, and Pacific. East North Central is the largest gas consumer at roughly 340 TBtu in the baseline, followed by Middle Atlantic near 215 TBtu, then Pacific and South Atlantic near 140 TBtu; East South Central and New England are smallest at roughly 50 TBtu. Every division shows a baseline-to-upgrade reduction from electrifying gas-heated RTUs, with the largest absolute drops in the cold, gas-heavy East North Central, Middle Atlantic, and West North Central divisions. The two upgrade bars are nearly equal in every division, consistent with Section 5.2: gas heating totals do not change between the HP-RTU and HP-RTU plus energy recovery scenarios, because the remaining gas use is in buildings not applicable to either scenario. Bar magnitudes are read from the axis; this chart carries no data labels.

![Grouped bar chart of site annual natural gas consumption by ComStock building type for the baseline and the two heat pump RTU scenarios](89481_images/image_000017_e8fe4d974da1c9a2a7a5c989061882465b4a28193bcec0a60e3fe72cd54f1848.png)

Figure A-2 plots site annual natural gas total energy consumption in TBtu, on an axis with gridlines up to 175, by ComStock building type, with three bars per type: Baseline (darkest), HP RTU E Backup, and HP RTU + ER (lightest). Types left to right: FullServiceRestaurant, QuickServiceRestaurant, RetailStripmall, RetailStandalone, SmallOffice, MediumOffice, LargeOffice, PrimarySchool, SecondarySchool, Outpatient, Hospital, SmallHotel, LargeHotel, and Warehouse. RetailStripmall has the largest baseline gas consumption, the only bar extending above the 175 TBtu gridline, followed by SecondarySchool and PrimarySchool near 160 to 170 TBtu, FullServiceRestaurant near 155 TBtu, and Warehouse near 140 TBtu; SmallHotel is smallest at roughly 15 TBtu. Reductions from baseline are largest for the retail and school types. The two restaurant types show comparatively small reductions, consistent with the measure excluding RTUs that serve kitchens and excluding heat/energy recovery for food service building types. The two upgrade bars are nearly identical for every building type. Bar magnitudes are read from the axis; this chart carries no data labels.

![Grouped bar chart of site annual electricity consumption by ComStock building type for the baseline and the two heat pump RTU scenarios](89481_images/image_000018_5be001aa46a371ae8d3eef647c63312c2750892d69ab9c5cdaf7a02f4881300d.png)

Figure A-3 plots site annual electricity total energy consumption in TBtu, on a 0 to 400 axis, by ComStock building type, with three bars per type: Baseline (darkest), HP RTU E Backup, and HP RTU + ER (lightest). Types left to right: FullServiceRestaurant, QuickServiceRestaurant, RetailStripmall, RetailStandalone, SmallOffice, MediumOffice, LargeOffice, PrimarySchool, SecondarySchool, Outpatient, Hospital, SmallHotel, LargeHotel, and Warehouse. RetailStripmall is the largest electricity consumer at roughly 395 TBtu in the baseline, with Warehouse near 360 TBtu, LargeOffice near 290 TBtu, and RetailStandalone near 280 TBtu; SmallHotel is smallest at roughly 40 TBtu. Scenario-to-scenario changes are proportionally far smaller than in the natural gas appendix figures, because the electricity results net two opposing effects: added electric heating from electrifying gas RTUs against savings from higher-efficiency variable-speed compressors and fans and from reduced ventilation loads. RetailStripmall, RetailStandalone, and SmallOffice show visible electricity reductions, while several types are nearly flat. Bar magnitudes are read from the axis; this chart carries no data labels.

![Grouped bar chart of site annual electricity consumption by census division for the ComStock baseline and the two heat pump RTU scenarios](89481_images/image_000019_9a2a8fe60476c71be278642d217f5999ef3aa425d12e979a48ae3e0786f75ecc.png)

Figure A-4 plots site annual electricity total energy consumption in TBtu, on an axis with gridlines up to 600, by census division, with three bars per division: Baseline (darkest), HP RTU E Backup, and HP RTU + ER (lightest). Divisions left to right: New England, Middle Atlantic, East North Central, West North Central, South Atlantic, East South Central, West South Central, Mountain, and Pacific. South Atlantic is by far the largest electricity consumer, the only bar reaching above the 600 TBtu gridline, followed by West South Central near 430 TBtu, East North Central and Pacific near 395 TBtu, and Middle Atlantic near 355 TBtu; New England is smallest at roughly 125 TBtu. Reductions from baseline are proportionally modest and are largest in the cooling-dominated South Atlantic, West South Central, and Pacific divisions, where cooling and fan electricity savings dominate; the colder East North Central, Middle Atlantic, and New England divisions show the smallest relative change, because added electric heating offsets those savings. Bar magnitudes are read from the axis; this chart carries no data labels.
