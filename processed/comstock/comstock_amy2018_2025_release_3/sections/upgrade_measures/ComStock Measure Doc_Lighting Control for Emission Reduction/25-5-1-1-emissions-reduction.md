Figure 5: three stacked time-series panels for the test small office over 7/16 to 7/20: building
load in kW (10 to 35), hourly emissions in kg CO2e with the grid emission factor in kg/MWh on
the right axis, and lighting schedule dimming level (40 to 100%). Baseline (dashed orange) and
lighting emission reduction (blue) differ only in the daily emission-peak windows, where
lighting dims to about 50%.

The measure realizes emission savings through reducing lighting electricity usage, but the impact of electric load reduction on emission is relatively small because the significant timing of emission (corresponding to high emission factors) is staggered to significant (peak) electric load. The absolute magnitude of load change is small when reducing load during peak emission time, likely in or close to nonworking hours as shown in Figure 5, while the resulting emission change would also be small even if more load could be reduced during peak load time due to smaller emission factors in the periods.
