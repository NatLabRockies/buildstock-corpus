## 5.2.1  Stock Greenhouse Gas Emissions Impacts

Figure 6 shows the annual stock-level impact of the measure with emission reduction objective on greenhouse gas emissions and presents approximately 0.3% -0.5% emission reductions for three grid electricity scenarios. Note that these results are at stock level, including non-applicable buildings. For applicable buildings, more granular results are shown in Figure 7 at the seasonal daily average level, for selected Cambium LRMER Mid Case 15-Year scenario. The savings performance increases to 1.6%-2.0% depending on the seasons.

The emission reductions are mostly attributed to the resulting reduced electricity consumption. The small impact on emission is mainly due to the inconsistent patterns of building electricity loads and emission factors, as well as the resulting emission profiles, as discussed in Section 5.1.1. In addition, the dispatched lighting load is small compared to total building load (less than 10% as shown in section 5.3), and thus the resulting emissions savings are small. The electricity emissions are dispatched daily following the proposed demand flexibility strategy, and the annual aggregate results would vary if other emissions reduction strategies were applied.
