## 5.4 Stock Emissions Impact

ComStock simulation results show avoided emissions across all three electricity grid scenarios and all on-site combustion fuel types (Figure 5) compared to the ComStock baseline. When combining all fuel types, the Comprehensive GHP + High Efficiency Envelope + LED Lighting package resulted in 81 to 127 million metric tons (MMT) of carbon emissions equivalent (CO2e) avoided (28% -33% reduction) depending on the grid scenario.

The emissions from natural gas are reduced by 59 MMT CO2e (58%) as a result of fuel switching much of the heating end use. The natural gas results remain consistent across all three grid scenarios, as these scenarios exclusively pertain to modifications within the electricity grid, without affecting natural gas outcomes.

Avoided electricity emissions from the electricity grid vary depending on the scenario chosen. The eGRID 2021 scenario represents the makeup of the grid as it was in 2021, while the LRMER scenarios project the future grid makeup by assuming different levels of renewable energy penetration. For the Comprehensive GHP + High Efficiency Envelope + LED Lighting package, the LRMER Low RE Cost and High RE Cost scenario show a 19 MMT CO2e and 42 MMT CO2e decrease in electricity emissions, respectively. The eGRID scenario shows 64 MMT CO2e of avoided emissions from the electricity grid.

Figure 5 has three side-by-side panels using different electricity emissions assumptions: "eGRID 2021", "LRMER High RE Cost 15", and "LRMER Low RE Cost 15" (LRMER = long-run marginal emissions rate, 15-year levelization; RE = renewable energy). Each panel stacks annual emissions in million metric tons of CO2 equivalent (MMT CO2e) by fuel -- electricity, natural gas, propane, fuel oil -- for the Baseline, Comprehensive GHP, Comprehensive GHP + High Efficiency Envelope, and Comprehensive GHP + High Efficiency Envelope + LED Lighting scenarios, with totals printed above each bar and percent change from baseline in parentheses. eGRID 2021: 452, then 365 (-19%), 340 (-25%), 325 (-28%). LRMER High RE Cost 15: 346, then 270 (-22%), 253 (-27%), 242 (-30%). LRMER Low RE Cost 15: 245, then 181 (-26%), 170 (-31%), 164 (-33%). Natural gas contributes about 102 MMT CO2e in every baseline and drops to roughly 43-44 MMT CO2e in all upgrade scenarios, since the GHP measures displace most on-site gas heating. Electricity emissions fall from about 345 to 281 MMT CO2e under eGRID and from about 139 to 120 MMT CO2e under the low-RE-cost LRMER case. Percent savings are largest under the cleanest grid assumption, because the remaining electricity load carries less carbon.

Three electricity grid scenarios are presented: eGRID 2021, Cambium Long-Run Marginal Emissions Rate (LRMER) High Renewable Energy (RE) Cost 15-Year, and Cambium LRMER Low RE Cost 15-Year. Note: MMT stands for million metric tons. .

Similar to the site energy results, we see that when the LED Lighting measure is added into the package, we see additional savings at the stock level for emissions. This is primarily due to the Lighting measure reducing electricity emissions. However, there are also cooling and fan savings that reduce electricity emissions. There are minimal changes to the emissions of the remaining fuels compared to the Comprehensive GHP + High Efficiency Envelope package.
