## 5.8.1  Ground Heat Exchanger Sizing

Figure 12 shows the borehole count, borehole depth, and total length of the ground heat exchanger for the three Comprehensive GHP packages. Because commercial buildings have such a wide range of size and load, the borehole count is normalized by building floor area (number of boreholes per thousand feet of floor area). The total ground heat exchanger loop length is calculated by multiplying the number of boreholes by the borehole depth, resulting in a rough approximation for the total length of drilling that would be required for the system.

When comparing the three packages, the borehole count, borehole depth, and total ground heat exchanger length get progressively smaller as more efficiency measures are added to the package. While the impact on the median building is rather minimum in all three cases, we see more substantial impacts on the more extreme ends of the box-and-whisker plot. In general, it appears that the envelope measures have a higher impact on the ground heat exchanger sizing than the lighting measure. This makes intuitive sense because the envelope measures primarily impact the HVAC end uses, while the lighting measure mainly impacts the lighting end use, with residual effects on HVAC end uses. Still, it is clear from the results of this package that combining GHPs with envelope and lighting improvements has a variety of positive impacts on the savings potential for a building, whether that be energy, emissions, utility bills, or installation and drilling costs.

Figure 12 is a set of three stacked box plot panels comparing ground heat exchanger sizing for the three package variants -- Comprehensive GHP, Comprehensive GHP + Envelope, and Comprehensive GHP + Envelope + Lighting -- each panel listing those three rows under "Upgrade Name". The top panel, "Normalized Borehole Count by Comprehensive GHP Upgrade Package", plots boreholes per thousand square feet of floor area on an x-axis from 0 to about 13; the GHP-only box reaches past 5 while both envelope packages shift left to boxes ending near 4, with whiskers to about 11. The middle panel, "Average Borehole Depth by Comprehensive GHP Upgrade Package", plots borehole depth in feet on a narrow axis from 360 to about 440; the GHP-only row centers slightly deeper (box roughly 415-428 feet) than the envelope packages (roughly 407-425 feet). The bottom panel, "Total Ground Heat Exchanger Length by Comprehensive GHP Upgrade Package", plots borehole depth times number of boreholes, in feet, from 0 to about 400 thousand feet; the GHP-only row extends furthest right. Note that borehole count is normalized by floor area while total length is not, so the top and bottom panels answer different questions: drilling intensity per unit of building versus absolute drilling required. Adding envelope measures reduces the ground loop that must be drilled; adding LED lighting on top changes it very little.
