## Comprehensive GHP + High Efficiency Envelope + LED Lighting Package

- Window Replacement: All windows with assembly U-values greater than those proposed in the Zero-Energy Small/Medium Office Advanced Energy Design Guide (AEDG), and all windows with SHGCs greater than those proposed in the AEDG. This measure is applicable to more than 99% of the ComStock floor area.
- Exterior Wall Insulation: Models with mass, steel-framed, or wood-framed walls. This measure is applicable to approximately 99% of the ComStock floor area.
- Roof Insulation: Models with roof insulation R-values that are below those specified in the AEDG for the respective climate zone. This measure is applicable to more than 99% of the ComStock floor area.
- LED Lighting: Models without LED lighting installed. This measure is applicable to approximately 64% of the ComStock floor area.

2025 Release 1: 2025/comstock\_amy2018\_release\_1/

National annual results for impacts to site energy, utility bills, and emissions, are summarized in Table ES-2, Table ES-3, and Table ES-4, respectively. In addition to these results, this report addresses the impacts on ground heat exchanger sizing and heat pump sizing when combining GHPs with envelope and lighting improvements. In summary, this package demonstrates that the number of boreholes is reduced by 24% and capacity of the geothermal heat pump is reduced by 21% (based on median) due to the addition of envelope and lighting measures. Based on the analysis described in this report, it was concluded that the envelope improvements have much more impact on the ground heat exchanger and heat pump sizing than the lighting improvements.
