## 1.4 Summary

The building envelope and internal loads of a building can impact the performance and required capacity of HVAC equipment in a building. For this reason, this package pairs the GHP upgrades with envelope and lighting upgrades to understand the combined impacts of these measures. By comparing the standalone GHP upgrades with the packages that include envelope and lighting upgrades, we can analyze how much improving the envelope and lighting of a building can impact the performance and size requirements of the GHP system.
