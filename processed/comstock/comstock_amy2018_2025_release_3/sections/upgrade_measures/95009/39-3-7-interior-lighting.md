## 3.7 Interior Lighting

The LED Lighting upgrade replaces model interior lighting with Generation 5 LED lighting, where applicable. The upgrade will first identify whether the upgrade is applicable to the model. If applicable, it will cycle through each of the spaces in the model, changing the lighting to the appropriate technology based on the space type's lighting category (the lighting categories are shown in Table 6).
