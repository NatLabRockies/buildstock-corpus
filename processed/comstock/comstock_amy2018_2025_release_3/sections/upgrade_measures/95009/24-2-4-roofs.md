## 2.4 Roofs

Roofs in ComStock are modeled as flat roofs. For buildings outside of California, we chose a single roof construction type for each building type. As shown in Table 5, most buildings are assumed to use insulation entirely above deck (IEAD) roofs, which is consistent with the assumption of flat roofs. For buildings in California, we used the construction types from the California Public Utilities Commission's Database of Energy Efficiency Resources (DEER) prototype buildings.
