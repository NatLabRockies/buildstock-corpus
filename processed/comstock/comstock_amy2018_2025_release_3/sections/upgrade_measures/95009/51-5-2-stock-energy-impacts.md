## 5.2 Stock Energy Impacts

Throughout the subsequent results sections, the results of the Comprehensive GHP + High Efficiency Envelope + LED Lighting package will be compared to the baseline, the Comprehensive GHP package, and the Comprehensive GHP + High Efficiency Envelope package. A summary of these three packages is as follows:

- -Comprehensive GHP: Packaged GHP + Hydronic GHP + Console GHP
- -Comprehensive GHP + High Efficiency Envelope: Packaged GHP + Hydronic GHP + Console GHP + New Windows + Wall Insulation + Roof Insulation
- -Comprehensive GHP + High Efficiency Envelope + LED Lighting: Packaged GHP + Hydronic GHP + Console GHP + New Windows + Wall Insulation + Roof Insulation + LED Lighting.

The assumptions and applicability of the GHP measures in both packages are the same. With the application of envelope and lighting measures, we can analyze the impacts of these upgrades on both the stock level impacts of the combined technologies, as well as the sizing and performance impacts on the GHPs. The energy savings trends of the Comprehensive GHP + High Efficiency Envelope + LED Lighting package are largely similar to those of the Comprehensive GHP + High Efficiency Envelope package. See the measure documentation (Central Hydronic GHP, Packaged GHP, Console GHP) for the Comprehensive GHP + High Efficiency Envelope package for a detailed discussion on the savings by end use.

Lighting energy is the main end use contributing to the additional savings in the Comprehensive GHP + High Efficiency Envelope + LED Lighting package. The reduced lighting load is also contributing to changes in HVAC end uses. The package with LED Lighting sees additional cooling and fan savings because the more efficient lights emit lower internal gains to the building during hotter months, hence reducing the cooling and fan load required. For the same reason, the lower internal gains from the LED Lighting are resulting in higher heating loads required during winter (when internal gains are advantageous). Altogether, the cooling and fan savings outweigh the heating penalty, indicating that LED Lighting does save HVAC energy at the stock level.
