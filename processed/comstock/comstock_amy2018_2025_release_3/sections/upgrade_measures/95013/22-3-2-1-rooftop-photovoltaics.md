## 3.2.1 Rooftop Photovoltaics

This measure leverages the PVWatts ®  EnergyPlus ®  objects. PVWatts is a National Renewable Energy Laboratory tool that estimates the energy production of grid-connected PV systems [5]. The workflow applies PVWatts objects with total power levels corresponding to 40% of the model's roof area. The panels are fixed with no tracking.
