## 3.1.3  HVAC System Type

Regardless of space and building type, any model with a DOAS will not have DCV applied. A DOAS is generally a damperless system and would require significant retrofit to adjust the outdoor air flow to accommodate DCV. Models with single-zone unitary systems (e.g., PTAC, PTHP) will not have DCV applied because they are entirely reliant on occupants to manually control the ventilation air and are unable to be controlled using occupancy or CO2 sensors.

Following ASHRAE 90.1 guidelines, models with energy recovery ventilators (ERVs) will also not have DCV applied.
