Appendix A grouped bar chart, dark blue Baseline against light blue DCV, no data labels. Y-axis: Natural Gas Total Energy Consumption in TBtu, 0 to about 320 on 50-unit gridlines. X-axis: Census Division Name, nine divisions. Bar heights measured off the axis, baseline then DCV, in TBtu: New England 53 to 49, Middle Atlantic 203 to 189, East North Central 317 to 301, West North Central 108 to 100, South Atlantic 140 to 134, East South Central 49 to 47, West South Central 92 to 88, Mountain 75 to 71, Pacific 228 to 210. Reads are plus or minus about 2 TBtu because one pixel is about 2 TBtu at this resolution. The baseline bars total roughly 1,265 TBtu, consistent with the natural gas bands of Figure 3, and the total reduction of roughly 77 TBtu is consistent with the 72.5 TBtu of gas heating savings quoted in Section 5.3. Every division shows a reduction, proportionally largest in Pacific, New England, Middle Atlantic and West North Central at roughly 7% to 8% and smallest in East South Central, South Atlantic and West South Central at roughly 4%.

Appendix A grouped bar chart, dark blue Baseline against light blue DCV, no data labels. Y-axis: Electricity Total Energy Consumption in TBtu, 0 to about 450 on 100-unit gridlines. X-axis: Comstock Building Type, the same 14 types as Figure A-2. Baseline bar heights measured off the axis, in TBtu: FullServiceRestaurant 196, QuickServiceRestaurant 90, RetailStripmall 449, RetailStandalone 288, SmallOffice 194, MediumOffice 185, LargeOffice 288, PrimarySchool 239, SecondarySchool 245, Outpatient 125, Hospital 171, SmallHotel 36, LargeHotel 191, Warehouse 443. They total about 3,140 TBtu, consistent with the electricity bands of Figure 3. One pixel is about 2.9 TBtu here, so the electricity reductions, which Section 5.3 puts at only 34 TBtu across fans, cooling and electric heating for the whole stock, are at or below the resolution of the plot: the visible drops are about 3 to 9 TBtu for the retail, school and office types and unresolvable for the rest. No per-type electricity delta should be read off this figure.

Appendix A grouped bar chart, dark blue Baseline against light blue DCV, no data labels. Y-axis: Electricity Total Energy Consumption in TBtu, 0 to 700 on 100-unit gridlines. X-axis: Census Division Name, nine divisions. Baseline bar heights measured off the axis, in TBtu: New England 120, Middle Atlantic 381, East North Central 420, West North Central 201, South Atlantic 668, East South Central 188, West South Central 437, Mountain 197, Pacific 522. They total about 3,134 TBtu, matching the total of Figure A-3 and the electricity bands of Figure 3. One pixel is about 4.3 TBtu at this resolution, so the per-division reductions cannot be resolved: every DCV bar is between one and three pixels shorter than its baseline, which brackets the stock-level electricity savings of about 34 TBtu from Section 5.3 but does not pin down any division. The largest visible drops are in South Atlantic and West South Central, the two cooling-dominated divisions with the highest consumption. No per-division electricity delta should be read off this figure.
