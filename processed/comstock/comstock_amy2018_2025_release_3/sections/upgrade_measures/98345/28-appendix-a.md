Figure A-1. Grouped vertical bar chart of site annual natural gas consumption in TBtu for the ComStock baseline and the Fan SP Reset measure scenario, by census division. Nine division groups run along the horizontal axis, each with a dark blue Baseline bar and a light blue Fan_SP_Reset_MZVAV bar per the Upgrade Name legend. The bars carry no printed value labels, so every number below is pixel-derived and approximate. Baseline values, in TBtu: New England about 47, Middle Atlantic 222, East North Central 386, West North Central 156, South Atlantic 169, East South Central 53, West South Central 117, Mountain 94 and Pacific 178. The measure bar is equal to or about 3 TBtu taller than the baseline bar in each division, the expected direction since the measure imposes a natural gas heating penalty. The nine baseline values sum to about 1,422 TBtu and the measured increases sum to about 17 TBtu against Table 5's -12.2 TBtu gas penalty; the difference is within the accumulated one-pixel error of nine separate bar-pair reads.
