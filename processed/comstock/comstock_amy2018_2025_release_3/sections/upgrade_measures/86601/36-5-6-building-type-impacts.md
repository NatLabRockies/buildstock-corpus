## 5.6 Building Type Impacts

Figure 11 shows the impacts of the Interior Lighting and Heat Pump upgrade package on natural gas consumption across the building types in the stock. Offices, schools, retail standalone, hospitals, and warehouses benefitted most from electrification of heating. This is a result of the breakdown of existing HVAC systems in these building types. The majority have PSZ-AC systems or boilers. The building types that still show substantial gas usage generally use gas for other end uses not impacted by this measure like water heating (food service, hotels), cooking (food service, strip malls with food service) and other gas process loads, or have a high prevalence of HVAC system types not applicable to this package (e.g., PTACs in hotels).
