Appendix chart of natural gas total site energy consumption in TBtu disaggregated by census division, with a Baseline column (dark blue) beside an Energy Recovery column (light blue) for each division. The legend is headed Upgrade Name and the x-axis is Census Division Name, with nine groups in the standard order: New England, Middle Atlantic, East North Central, West North Central, South Atlantic, East South Central, West South Central, Mountain, and Pacific. The y-axis, Natural Gas Total Energy Consumption (TBtu), runs to just over 300. East North Central is the largest gas consumer at roughly 310 TBtu in the baseline, followed by Pacific near 225 and Middle Atlantic near 200. New England and East South Central are the smallest, near 50. The Energy Recovery column is visibly shorter than the Baseline column in every division, so gas heating savings are geographically broad rather than concentrated, though the largest absolute reductions land in the heating-dominated East North Central and Middle Atlantic divisions. Bar magnitudes are read from the axis; no data labels are printed.

Appendix chart of natural gas total site energy consumption in TBtu disaggregated by building type, with a Baseline column (dark blue) beside an Energy Recovery column (light blue) for each type. The legend is headed Upgrade Name and the x-axis is Comstock Building Type, listing FullServiceRestaurant, QuickServiceRestaurant, RetailStripmall, RetailStandalone, SmallOffice, MediumOffice, LargeOffice, PrimarySchool, SecondarySchool, Outpatient, Hospital, SmallHotel, LargeHotel, and Warehouse. The y-axis, Natural Gas Total Energy Consumption (TBtu), runs to about 200. RetailStripmall is the largest gas consumer at roughly 210 TBtu in the baseline, with SecondarySchool and FullServiceRestaurant near 175 and PrimarySchool near 225. The two restaurant types stand out because their columns are effectively unchanged between baseline and upgrade, consistent with food service being excluded from applicability. Most other types show a visible reduction, largest for RetailStripmall, PrimarySchool, and SecondarySchool. SmallHotel has the smallest gas consumption. Bar magnitudes are read from the axis; no data labels are printed.

Appendix chart of electricity total site energy consumption in TBtu disaggregated by census division, with a Baseline column (dark blue) beside an Energy Recovery column (light blue) for each division. The legend is headed Upgrade Name and the x-axis is Census Division Name, with nine groups: New England, Middle Atlantic, East North Central, West North Central, South Atlantic, East South Central, West South Central, Mountain, and Pacific. The y-axis, Electricity Total Energy Consumption (TBtu), runs to 700. South Atlantic is by far the largest electricity consumer at roughly 675 TBtu, followed by Pacific near 520, West South Central near 440, East North Central near 420, and Middle Atlantic near 385. New England is smallest, near 125. Unlike the natural gas appendix charts, the paired columns here are nearly the same height in every division, with only a slight reduction visible: electricity savings from reduced cooling and electric heating are partly offset by the added heat recovery fan power, so the net electricity change is small relative to the total. Bar magnitudes are read from the axis; no data labels are printed.
