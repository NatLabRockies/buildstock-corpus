## 3.4  Utility Bills

Utility bills are not presented for this measure scenario since the Ideal Thermal Air Loads measure is HVAC system and fuel type agnostic. The public dataset still includes utility bill impacts for natural gas, electricity, fuel oil, and propane. However, ideal thermal loads impacts are metered as district energy, which ComStock does not currently estimate bills for. Therefore, the reported utility bills for this measure scenario in the dataset do not include HVAC impacts.
