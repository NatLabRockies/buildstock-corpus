Figure A-1. Grouped bar chart of site annual natural gas total energy consumption (TBtu) by U.S. census division, y-axis 0 to about 350. Two bars per division (Baseline, dark blue; Ideal Thermal Loads, light blue) for New England, Middle Atlantic, East North Central, West North Central, South Atlantic, East South Central, West South Central, Mountain, and Pacific. Baseline gas consumption exceeds the Ideal Thermal Loads scenario in every division; East North Central is the largest (baseline about 370), followed by Middle Atlantic and Pacific (each roughly 210-230 baseline). The Ideal scenario roughly halves natural gas in most divisions because baseline gas heating is re-metered as district heating.

Figure A-2. Grouped bar chart of site annual natural gas total energy consumption (TBtu) by ComStock building type, y-axis 0 to about 250. Baseline exceeds Ideal for every type; SecondarySchool is the largest (baseline about 245, Ideal about 25), with RetailStripmall, PrimarySchool, Hospital, and FullServiceRestaurant also high. Gas consumption falls sharply under the Ideal scenario as gas heating shifts to district heating.

Baseline electricity exceeds the Ideal scenario in every division; South Atlantic is the largest (baseline about 690, Ideal about 340).
