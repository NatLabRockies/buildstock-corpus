## 5.1 Realized Applicability

Figure 2 provides a breakdown of the applicability of the Comprehensive GHP package by individual measure. 56.1% of total floor area was applicable to Packaged GHP, while 13.5% received the Hydronic GHP upgrade and 10.8% received the Console GHP upgrade. In total, at least one measure from the package was applicable to 80% of the stock floor area, leaving 20% of floor area not applicable to any measures in the package.
