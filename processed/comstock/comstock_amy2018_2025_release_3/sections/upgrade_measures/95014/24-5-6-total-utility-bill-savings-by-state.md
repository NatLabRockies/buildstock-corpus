## 5.6  Total Utility Bill Savings by State

Figure 17 shows the total utility bill savings by state in millions of U.S. dollars. California shows the highest total bill savings of $136M, followed by New York ($81M) and Illinois ($76M). The condensing boiler shows strong potential for utility bill savings in the northern Midwest (Illinois, Ohio, Michigan, Indiana, Minnesota, and Wisconsin) as well as the northern mid-Atlantic (New York, Pennsylvania, and New Jersey). These states are heating dominated and have high natural gas prevalence in the existing building stock, allowing for efficiency improvements to these systems that can result in substantial cost savings for building owners.
