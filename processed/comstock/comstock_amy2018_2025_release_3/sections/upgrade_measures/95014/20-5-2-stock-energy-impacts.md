## 5.2  Stock Energy Impacts

The Condensing Boiler measure demonstrates 1.7% total site energy savings (83 TBtu) for the U.S. commercial building stock modeled in ComStock. The savings contributions by end use and fuel type are summarized in Table 9 and illustrated in Figure 10.

Table 9. Summary of Site Energy Savings From Upgrade Measure Application Versus the ComStock Baseline

| End Use/Fuel Type   | Percent Site Energy Savings (All Buildings)   | Percent Site Energy Savings (Applicable Buildings Only)   |   Absolute Site Energy Savings (TBtu) |
|---------------------|-----------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------|
| Total Natural Gas   | 5.6%                                          | 12.6%                                                     |                                  84.8 |
| Total Other Fuel    | 2.4%                                          | 15.7%                                                     |                                   1.0 |
| Total Heating       | 6.6%                                          | 15.6%                                                     |                                  85.8 |
| Total Site Energy   | 1.7%                                          | 5.9%                                                      |                                  83.4 |

As shown in Figure 10, most of the annual site energy savings for the Condensing Boiler measure are seen in natural gas heating. Swapping out traditional boilers with higher-efficiency condensing gas boilers reduces the energy used for natural gas space heating by 8.8% across the building stock. In addition, the baseline also contains a small fraction of fuel oil boilers, so we also see savings in 'other fuel heating.' There are very minor (&lt;0.2%) changes in cooling and fan energy. These are residual effects of swapping out the heating system that can impact zone temperatures and operation of the HVAC system. All other end uses are unaffected by the measure, as expected.
