## 3.1  Applicability

Figure 7 shows the heating type distribution among the ComStock buildings. This measure is applicable to buildings that use gas boilers as a heating source, which account for 25% of the ComStock baseline floor area. In addition, there is one HVAC system that is classified as 'WSHP' for the heating type-'DOAS with water-source heat pumps cooling tower with boiler'-which also uses a boiler for heating. This brings the total applicable floor area to approximately 26%.

Buildings with boiler systems account for 26% of the floor area and 56% of the natural gas heating consumption in ComStock. Table 1 shows a breakdown of HVAC system types that are applicable to this measure and their respective percentages of floor area.
