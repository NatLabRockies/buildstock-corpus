## 5.4  Stock Emissions Impact

ComStock simulation results show avoided emissions across three electricity grid scenarios (Figure 13). When combining all fuel types, the Condensing Boiler measure scenario shows 5-6 million metric tons (MMT) of carbon dioxide equivalent (CO2e) avoided annually (1%-2% reduction). These avoided emissions are attributable to natural gas space heating. Natural gas emissions are reduced by 6 MMT CO2e in all three scenarios. The natural gas results remain consistent across all three grid scenarios, as these scenarios exclusively pertain to modifications within the electricity grid, without affecting natural gas outcomes.

This measure does not substantially impact any of the electricity end uses. In the eGRID scenario, the electricity emissions increase by 1 MMT CO2e between the baseline and the upgrade, which is a result of minor increases in cooling and fan end uses. In both LRMER scenarios, the electricity emissions show no change between the baseline and the upgrade. Note that the values in this figure are rounded to the nearest integer, so there could be small changes in emissions not captured when rounding.
