## 2  ComStock Baseline Approach

Of the buildings represented in ComStock™, about 26% are served by boiler heating systems. However, these systems represent over 56% of the natural gas heating energy in the baseline stock. This measure will be applicable to the following heating, ventilating, and air conditioning (HVAC) system types, which are shown in Table 1 along with the respective percentages of the ComStock floor area and natural gas heating energy that they represent.

Boilers for space heating in the baseline of ComStock are gas-fired or fuel-oil-fired noncondensing boilers. Their efficiencies were determined using the U.S. Department of Energy's reference building templates and capacities and are generally rated around 80%. The values are summarized in Table 2 [5]. Three different cubic performance curves based on the part load ratio (PLR) are used to adjust the boiler efficiency, as shown in Table 3. Figure 3 shows the variations of boiler efficiency multipliers with PLR based on the three performance curves, a graphical representation of the curves' output [6]. All ComStock boilers have a heating set point of 180°F and can modulate flow based on heating load. Boiler models do not have any supply temperature reset based on outdoor air temperature in the baseline.

![Figure 3. Scatter plot of boiler efficiency multiplier against boiler part load ratio for the three ComStock cubic performance curves, with the constant curve flat at 1.0 and the two turndown curves rising from about 0.75 and 0.78 at zero load to about 1.27 and 1.17 at a PLR of 1.3](95014_images/image_000004_306e1366784900d7d9c921f7c82ea2486e18aa0f7ecf6f201c80ace7e54877b5.png)

Scatter plot with markers only and no connecting lines, reproducing the output of the three cubic performance curves listed in Table 3. X-axis: Boiler Part Load Ratio, 0 to 1.4. Y-axis: Boiler Efficiency Multiplier, 0 to 1.4. Three series are plotted at PLR steps of 0.1. Blue is the Boiler constant efficiency curve and sits flat at exactly 1.0 across the whole range, matching its coefficients of 1, 0, 0, 0. Grey is Boiler with minimum turndown (0.7791 + 1.4745*PLR - 2.5795*PLR^2 + 1.3467*PLR^3): it starts at about 0.78 at zero PLR, crosses 1.0 near PLR 0.3, stays within a few percent of 1.0 through the middle of the range, and rises to about 1.17 at PLR 1.3. Orange is Boiler with no minimum turndown (0.7463 + 1.3196*PLR - 2.2154*PLR^2 + 1.1674*PLR^3): it starts lower at about 0.75, tracks slightly below the grey series to PLR 0.5, then rises more steeply to about 1.27 at PLR 1.3. The point for Section 2 is that ComStock baseline boilers are penalized at very low part load and only the constant curve is load independent; which curve a model receives is assigned by template and capacity in Table 2.
