![Grouped bar chart of site annual natural gas consumption in TBtu by census division for the ComStock baseline and the shedfb measure scenario, with baseline and measure bars visually identical in all nine divisions](89340_images/image_000016_8aa2593f4dfc2aeaa63649667978079e25d1811917f39ab8d33903f976670230.png)

Figure A-1 of the ComStock thermostat-control-for-load-shedding measure documentation compares site annual natural gas consumption between the ComStock baseline and the measure scenario by census division. Paired bars, dark blue 'Baseline' and light blue 'shedfb', are plotted for New England, Middle Atlantic, East North Central, West North Central, South Atlantic, East South Central, West South Central, Mountain, and Pacific, on a y-axis of natural gas total energy consumption in TBtu from 0 to 350. Reading from the axis, since the chart carries no data labels, East North Central is by far the largest at roughly 335 TBtu, followed by Middle Atlantic near 215, Pacific near 147, South Atlantic near 137, West North Central near 115, West South Central near 90, Mountain near 78, and New England and East South Central near 53 to 55. In every division the baseline and measure bars are the same height to the resolution of the chart, consistent with total stock natural gas heating savings of only 0.2 TBtu, or 0.023%. The measure targets electric HVAC load shedding in large offices, so it has essentially no effect on regional gas consumption.

Figure A-2 of the ComStock thermostat-control-for-load-shedding measure documentation compares site annual natural gas consumption between the ComStock baseline and the measure scenario by building type. Paired bars, dark blue 'Baseline' and light blue 'shedfb', are plotted for FullServiceRestaurant, QuickServiceRestaurant, RetailStripmall, RetailStandalone, SmallOffice, MediumOffice, LargeOffice, PrimarySchool, SecondarySchool, Outpatient, Hospital, SmallHotel, LargeHotel, and Warehouse, on a y-axis of natural gas total energy consumption in TBtu running to about 190. Reading from the axis, since the chart carries no data labels, RetailStripmall is the largest at roughly 188 TBtu, followed by SecondarySchool near 165, FullServiceRestaurant and Warehouse near 155, PrimarySchool near 123, LargeHotel near 83, RetailStandalone near 78, Hospital near 70, SmallOffice near 53, LargeOffice near 45, QuickServiceRestaurant and MediumOffice near 40, Outpatient near 28, and SmallHotel near 10. Baseline and measure bars are indistinguishable everywhere, including the applicable LargeOffice column, consistent with stock natural gas heating savings of 0.2 TBtu, or 0.023%.

Figure A-3 of the ComStock thermostat-control-for-load-shedding measure documentation is captioned 'Site annual electricity consumption of the ComStock baseline and the measure scenario by building type', but the plotted graphic does not match that caption: its y-axis reads natural gas total energy consumption in TBtu and the bar heights reproduce Figure A-2. Paired dark blue 'Baseline' and light blue 'shedfb' bars appear for FullServiceRestaurant, QuickServiceRestaurant, RetailStripmall, RetailStandalone, SmallOffice, MediumOffice, LargeOffice, PrimarySchool, SecondarySchool, Outpatient, Hospital, SmallHotel, LargeHotel, and Warehouse, with RetailStripmall largest near 188 TBtu and SmallHotel smallest near 10 TBtu, and baseline and measure bars visually identical throughout. The intended electricity-by-building-type appendix figure appears to be missing from the source PDF, replaced by a second copy of the natural gas chart. Stock electricity impacts must instead be taken from Figure 9, which reports cooling electricity savings of 1.0 TBtu, heating electricity 0.6 TBtu, and fan electricity 0.9 TBtu.
