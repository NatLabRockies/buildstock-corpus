## 5.4  Stock Greenhouse Gas Emissions Impact

Figure 10 shows the annual stock level impact of the measure on greenhouse gas emissions and presents approximately 0% emission reductions for all the three grid electricity scenarios. This is as expected due to the small energy savings. Future analysis will pursue the objective of saving carbon emission that will result in more impact on carbon emission savings.

Figure 10 of the ComStock thermostat-control-for-load-shedding measure documentation compares annual stock greenhouse gas emissions between the baseline and the default load-shed scenario under three grid electricity scenarios. Each panel stacks Electricity, Natural Gas, Fuel Oil, and Propane in million metric tons CO2e for a 'Baseline' and a 'Load shed' bar. Under eGRID 2021 the total is 402 MMT CO2e at baseline and 402 with the measure, annotated '(-0%)', with Electricity 314 and Natural Gas 81 in both bars. Under LRMER High RE Cost 15 the total is 325 and 325, again '(-0%)', with Electricity 237 and Natural Gas 81. Under LRMER Low RE Cost 15 the total is 240 and 240, '(-0%)', with Electricity 152 and Natural Gas 81. In all three panels the on-site fossil components are identical between bars and the residual Fuel Oil plus Propane contribution is about 7 MMT CO2e, consistent across scenarios. The measure delivers approximately 0% emission reduction in every grid scenario, as expected given site energy savings of only 0.046%; its objective is daily demand flexibility rather than annual energy or carbon reduction.
