Figure 7: option-matrix diagram of the decoupled VRF-plus-DOAS configuration modeled in this upgrade. The VRF block resolves two choices (heat recovery rather than multi-split, two-pipe rather than three-pipe) and the DOAS block three (HRV in dry climates or ERV in humid climates, counterflow plate heat exchanger, sensible heat or enthalpy exchange). Selected options are bold, rejected options greyed. See Section 4.2.
