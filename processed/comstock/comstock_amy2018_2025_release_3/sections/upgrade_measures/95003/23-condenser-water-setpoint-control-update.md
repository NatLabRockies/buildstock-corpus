## Condenser water setpoint control update:

- Change condenser water system (including cooling tower) specifications based on ASHRAE 90.1-2019 section G3.1.3.11
- Calculating and applying condenser water design supply temperature based on section G3.1.3.11, which reflects local weather and wet-bulb temperature limits within 55°F (12.8°C) and 90°F (32.2°C)
- Also updating condenser water setpoint temperatures accordingly based on the calculated condenser water design supply temperature: maximum setpoint temperature, minimum setpoint temperature, and the offset temperature compared to the outdoor wet-bulb temperature.
