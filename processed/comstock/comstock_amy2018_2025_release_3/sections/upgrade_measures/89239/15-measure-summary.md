## Measure Summary

| Measure Title   | Central Hydronic Water-to-Water Geothermal Heat Pump                                                                                   |
|-----------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Measure         | This measure retrofits existing hydronic HVAC systems to implement water-source                                                        |
| Definition      | heat pumps for heating and cooling, with the heat pumps coupled to a loop conditioned by a ground heat exchanger.                      |
| Applicability   | This measure is applicable to buildings with central hydronic systems, accounting for about 13% of the floor area modeled in ComStock. |
| Not Applicable  | This measure is not applicable to buildings with hot water baseboards, or to buildings served by district thermal energy systems.      |
| Release         | 2024 Release 1: 2024/comstock_amy2018_release_1/                                                                                       |
