## 1.1  Literature Review

A literature review was performed to determine the rated input power and fractions of radiant, latent, and lost energy for gas and electric models for each of the six types of cooking equipment. In addition, this section outlines the sources used to determine the prevalence of gas and electric equipment for each of the six appliances modeled.
