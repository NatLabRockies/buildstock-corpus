Figure A-1 of the ComStock Variable-Speed Pumps measure documentation. Nine pairs of vertical bars plot 'Natural Gas Total Energy Consumption (tbtu)' against 'Census Division Name', with a legend headed Upgrade Name distinguishing Baseline in dark blue from Pmp in light blue. Bar heights measured against the tick-label calibration give baseline values of roughly 49 TBtu for New England, 222 for Middle Atlantic, 387 for East North Central, 154 for West North Central, 172 for South Atlantic, 56 for East South Central, 117 for West South Central, 96 for Mountain and 179 for Pacific, summing to roughly 1,430 TBtu. East North Central is by far the largest gas consumer. The upgrade bars are the same height as or one pixel taller than the baseline in every division, consistent with the small national natural gas penalty of -3.1 TBtu reported in Table 4 - the pump measure adds less waste heat to the water loop, so boilers work slightly harder. The per-division increase is below the resolution of the plot, so no individual delta is asserted here. No bar carries a data label.

![Figure A-2: grouped bar chart captioned as site annual natural gas consumption by building type, but whose axis and content are site annual electricity consumption in TBtu by ComStock building type - the same graphic as Figure A-4](98223_images/image_000019_c6e3d44ae9364fd305f91dd407c2a49c63c69c68a9a5af9e5b147e74816b988d.png)

The image published under the Figure A-2 caption in the ComStock Variable-Speed Pumps measure documentation. The caption reads 'Site annual natural gas consumption of the ComStock baseline and the measure scenario by building type', but the plotted y axis is 'Electricity Total Energy Consumption (tbtu)' and the graphic is pixel-for-pixel the same chart as Figure A-4 apart from image re-encoding noise. There is therefore no natural-gas-by-building-type figure in the document. What the image actually shows is fifteen pairs of vertical bars against 'Comstock Building Type', legend Upgrade Name with Baseline dark blue and Pmp light blue. Measured baseline values are roughly: FullServiceRestaurant 203, QuickServiceRestaurant 73, RetailStripmall 492, RetailStandalone 311, SmallOffice 232, MediumOffice 194, LargeOffice 238, PrimarySchool 279, SecondarySchool 254, Outpatient 137, Hospital 191, SmallHotel 35, LargeHotel 178, Warehouse 391 and Grocery 108 TBtu, summing to about 3,310 TBtu. Visible reductions occur in LargeOffice, Hospital and LargeHotel; the national electricity saving is 14.4 TBtu per Table 4, which is below one pixel for most bars. No bar carries a data label.

Figure A-3 of the ComStock Variable-Speed Pumps measure documentation. Nine pairs of vertical bars plot 'Electricity Total Energy Consumption (tbtu)' against 'Census Division Name', legend Upgrade Name with Baseline in dark blue and Pmp in light blue. Bar heights measured against the tick-label calibration give baseline values of roughly 97 TBtu for New England, 339 for Middle Atlantic, 436 for East North Central, 271 for West North Central, 756 for South Atlantic, 213 for East South Central, 504 for West South Central, 233 for Mountain and 465 for Pacific, summing to roughly 3,310 TBtu - the same national total as the by-building-type electricity figure, as expected for two disaggregations of one quantity. South Atlantic is the largest consumer by a wide margin, followed by West South Central and Pacific. The upgrade bars are visibly shorter than the baseline only in South Atlantic and Pacific; the national electricity saving of 14.4 TBtu reported in Table 4 is spread thinly enough that most per-division deltas fall below one pixel and none is asserted here. No bar carries a data label.

Figure A-4 of the ComStock Variable-Speed Pumps measure documentation. Fifteen pairs of vertical bars plot 'Electricity Total Energy Consumption (tbtu)' against 'Comstock Building Type', legend Upgrade Name with Baseline in dark blue and Pmp in light blue. Measured baseline values are roughly: FullServiceRestaurant 203, QuickServiceRestaurant 73, RetailStripmall 492, RetailStandalone 311, SmallOffice 232, MediumOffice 194, LargeOffice 238, PrimarySchool 279, SecondarySchool 254, Outpatient 137, Hospital 191, SmallHotel 35, LargeHotel 178, Warehouse 391 and Grocery 108 TBtu, summing to about 3,310 TBtu, which matches the census-division electricity total in Figure A-3. RetailStripmall and Warehouse are the largest electricity consumers in the stock, reflecting their large aggregate floor area rather than high intensity. Reductions are visible in LargeOffice, Hospital and LargeHotel - the building types Figure 2 shows are most heavily served by chillers and boilers. This same graphic also appears under the Figure A-2 caption, where the caption instead promises natural gas by building type. No bar carries a data label.
