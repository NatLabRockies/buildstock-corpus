## 3.2.1 Applicability

Based on the many examples readily identified through a cursory search, it appears that exterior insulation is readily applied to mass, wood-framed, and steel-framed walls. For metal buildings, interior insulation is a more practical and likely retrofit-this upgrade is therefore not applicable to metal buildings.

For modeling purposes, we calculate the thickness of XPS insulation necessary to bring the wall assembly up to the AEDG recommendation for each climate zone. When the existing walls already meet or exceed the AEDG recommendations

Table 8, this upgrade is not applicable. When the required insulation thickness is less than 0.5 in., this upgrade is not applicable. For required thicknesses greater than 0.5 in., the selected thickness is rounded to the nearest inch using standard rounding to reflect commonly available products.

This upgrade is applicable to 98% of the ComStock floor area.
