Figure A-2: grouped bar chart of annual site natural gas consumption (TBtu, y-axis 0 to about 215) by ComStock building type, baseline against Package 3. RetailStripmall is largest, falling from roughly 215 to 120 TBtu, while the school and office types drop by most of their baseline. FullServiceRestaurant and LargeHotel change least, reflecting non-heating gas loads. Figure A-1 gives the same total by census division.
