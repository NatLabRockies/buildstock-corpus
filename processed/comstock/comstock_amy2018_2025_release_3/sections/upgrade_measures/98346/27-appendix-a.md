Figure A-1. Site annual natural gas consumption of the ComStock baseline and the Thermostat Setbacks measure scenario by census division. The vertical axis is Natural Gas Total Energy Consumption in TBtu, 0 to 400, the horizontal axis is Census Division Name, and each division carries a dark Baseline bar beside a light Thermostat_Setbacks bar. Approximate pixel-derived pairs, baseline then measure: New England 47 and 47, Middle Atlantic 220 and 217, East North Central 387 and 375, West North Central 155 and 152, South Atlantic 170 and 167, East South Central 53 and 53, West South Central 117 and 114, Mountain 94 and 91, Pacific 179 and 144. East North Central is the largest gas consumer and Middle Atlantic the second. The columns sum to about 1,421 TBtu in the baseline against about 1,360 with the measure, a reduction consistent with the 68.6 TBtu and 4.8% whole-stock natural gas saving reported in Table ES-2. The Pacific division is a visible outlier, showing roughly a 20% gas reduction where every other division shows 1% to 3%.

Approximate pixel-derived pairs, baseline then measure: RetailStripmall 224 and 217, SecondarySchool 214 and 202, FullServiceRestaurant 169 and 166, PrimarySchool 160 and 144, LargeHotel 129 and 129, Hospital 126 and 126, Warehouse 104 and 101, RetailStandalone 73 and 63, SmallOffice 56 and 51, LargeOffice 44 and 39, QuickServiceRestaurant 43 and 42, MediumOffice 37 and 32, Outpatient 35 and 35, Grocery 19 and 17, SmallHotel 9 and 9. The columns sum to about 1,440 against about 1,372 TBtu, a difference of about 68 TBtu that reproduces Table ES-2's 68.6. Hospital, SmallHotel and LargeHotel show no change, consistent with their excluded space types.

The relative changes are much smaller than in the natural gas panels, and the same building types that are largely excluded from the measure show no change at all.

Figure A-4. Site annual electricity consumption of the ComStock baseline and the Thermostat Setbacks measure scenario by census division. The vertical axis is Electricity Total Energy Consumption in TBtu, 0 to about 750, the horizontal axis is Census Division Name, and each division carries a dark Baseline bar beside a light Thermostat_Setbacks bar. Approximate pixel-derived pairs, baseline then measure: New England 95 and 92, Middle Atlantic 341 and 338, East North Central 439 and 434, West North Central 275 and 271, South Atlantic 754 and 746, East South Central 213 and 211, West South Central 506 and 500, Mountain 234 and 231, Pacific 465 and 455. South Atlantic is much the largest electricity consumer, followed by West South Central and Pacific, which reverses the natural gas ordering of Figure A-1, where the cold northern divisions lead. The columns sum to about 3,322 against about 3,278 TBtu, consistent both with the building-type totals of Figure A-3 and with Table ES-2's 39.4 TBtu and 1.2% electricity saving. Pacific shows the largest relative electricity reduction, about 2%.
