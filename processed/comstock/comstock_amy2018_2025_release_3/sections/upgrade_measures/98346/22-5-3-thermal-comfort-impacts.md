## 5.3  Thermal Comfort Impacts

To ensure that the measure resulted in operation within the intended temperature bounds, distributions of hours in which cooling and/or heating setpoints are unmet in the underlying building energy models were examined. In general, temperature setbacks can potentially create problems with a system's ability to 'recover' to the occupied setpoint at the desired time. The optimum start sequence implemented is intended to help mitigate that. Results for 'unmet hours' are shown in Figure 8. In heating, the median number of unmet hours increases from around 1 hour in the baseline to 8 hours in the measure case. The values also increase at the 75 th percentile but are not extreme. In both heating and cooling, the median values in the baseline are not visible because they are very close to 0, the 25 th percentile value. The results in cooling are similar. This confirms that the setback strategy implemented does not compromise thermal comfort by notably increasing unmet hours.

Figure 8. Boxplots of the number of hours with the heating setpoint unmet, for the baseline and the Thermostat Setbacks scenarios. The panel is titled Heating Unmet Hours and the horizontal axis is Unmet Hours Count on a logarithmic scale running from 1 to about 10,000. Both boxes begin at the left edge of the plotted range, because the first quartile of each distribution is at or below one hour. The baseline box extends to a third quartile of roughly 35 hours with a whisker to roughly 85 hours; the measure box extends further, to a third quartile of roughly 110 hours with a whisker to roughly 280 hours, and both distributions have dense outlier strings running out toward 10,000 hours. Section 5.3 gives the median as about 1 hour in the baseline and 8 hours with the measure, notes that the baseline median is not visible because it coincides with the first quartile near zero, and characterizes the rise in the 75th percentile as not extreme. Note that the caption promises heating or cooling and Section 5.3 states that the cooling results are similar, but only a heating panel appears in the extracted figure.
