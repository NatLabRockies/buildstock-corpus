## 3.2.5.1  Scenario

Cambium 2022 Mid-Case Scenario data are selected for generalizability of the data and alignment with existing ComStock data sources. The Cambium Mid-Case Scenario refers to the scenario where the input estimates such as technology costs, fuel prices, and demand growth are central (at mean or median values). More detailed definition of the scenario could be found in the Cambium Documentation [29]. The projection year of 2035 (among 2025, 2030, 2035, 2040, 2045, and 2050) for the Mid-Case Scenario is selected to balance between the long-term impacts and the short-term accuracy/certainty.
