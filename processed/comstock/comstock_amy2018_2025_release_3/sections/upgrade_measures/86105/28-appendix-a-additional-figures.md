![Grouped bar chart of site annual natural gas consumption in TBtu by census division for the ComStock baseline and the economizer scenario, with East North Central highest at about 336 TBtu and the two scenarios almost identical.](86105_images/image_000024_033df9a76c56b0d00ba51c0a23a17445fad928e0a65ee437ba7d712233b933f6.png)

Figure A-1 (Appendix A) compares site annual natural gas consumption between the ComStock baseline and the economizer scenario by census division. The y-axis is "Natural Gas Total Energy Consumption (tbtu)" from 0 to 350 and the "Upgrade Name" legend pairs a dark Baseline bar with a light Economizer bar for each of the nine divisions. Reading against the gridlines, the baseline values are approximately East North Central 336, Middle Atlantic 213, Pacific 147, South Atlantic 137, West North Central 114, West South Central 87, Mountain 72, New England 54 and East South Central 50, summing to roughly 1,210 TBtu. That total reconciles with the gas segments printed in Figure 10 - heating 854.9, interior equipment 212.1, plus the water systems gas segment - and with Figure A-2's building-type breakdown, which sums to about the same figure. The economizer bars are visually indistinguishable from the baseline in every division, with the small increases the report describes just detectable in West North Central and Pacific. No values are printed on the chart, so these readings are approximate; the figure's purpose is to show that gas consumption is essentially unaffected by the measure.

Figure A-2 (Appendix A) compares site annual natural gas consumption between the ComStock baseline and the economizer scenario by building type. The y-axis is "Natural Gas Total Energy Consumption (tbtu)" with gridlines every 25 up to 175, and the "Upgrade Name" legend pairs a dark Baseline bar with a light Economizer bar for each of the fourteen types. Reading against the gridlines, the baseline values are approximately RetailStripmall 188, SecondarySchool 164, FullServiceRestaurant 154, Warehouse 142, PrimarySchool 122, LargeHotel 82, RetailStandalone 77, Hospital 71, SmallOffice 54, LargeOffice 44, QuickServiceRestaurant 39, MediumOffice 36, Outpatient 26 and SmallHotel 8.5, summing to roughly 1,208 TBtu. That matches Figure A-1's census-division total of about 1,210 TBtu, which cross-corroborates both reads. The economizer bars are visually indistinguishable from the baseline for every type, consistent with the report's finding that stock gas consumption rises only 0.7 TBtu overall. No values are printed on the chart. The x-axis title is misprinted "Comstock Building Type" with a lowercase s.

Figure A-3 (Appendix A) compares site annual electricity consumption between the ComStock baseline and the economizer scenario by census division. The y-axis is "Electricity Total Energy Consumption (tbtu)" running to about 650 and the "Upgrade Name" legend pairs a dark Baseline bar with a light Economizer bar for each of the nine divisions. Reading against the gridlines, the baseline values are approximately South Atlantic 640, West South Central 414, Pacific 398, East North Central 394, Middle Atlantic 361, West North Central 196, Mountain 194, East South Central 178 and New England 120, summing to roughly 2,895 TBtu - consistent with the electricity segments printed in Figure 10 and with Figure A-4's building-type total. Unlike the gas figures, the economizer bars here are visibly shorter than the baseline in the larger divisions: the reductions are about 4 TBtu in South Atlantic and Pacific and about 3 TBtu in West South Central and Middle Atlantic, while New England, East South Central, West North Central and Mountain show no perceptible change. No values are printed on the chart, so these readings are approximate.

Figure A-4 (Appendix A) compares site annual electricity consumption between the ComStock baseline and the economizer scenario by building type. The y-axis is "Electricity Total Energy Consumption (tbtu)" from 0 to 400 and the "Upgrade Name" legend pairs a dark Baseline bar with a light Economizer bar for each of the fourteen types. Reading against the gridlines, the baseline values are approximately RetailStripmall 397, Warehouse 363, LargeOffice 294, RetailStandalone 279, SecondarySchool 226, PrimarySchool 221, SmallOffice 193, LargeHotel 192, MediumOffice 182, FullServiceRestaurant 168, Hospital 160, Outpatient 126, QuickServiceRestaurant 64 and SmallHotel 35, summing to roughly 2,900 TBtu. That matches Figure A-3's census-division total of about 2,895 TBtu, which cross-corroborates both reads. Small reductions are visible in the upgrade bars for RetailStripmall, LargeOffice, RetailStandalone and Warehouse, each on the order of 1 to 3 TBtu, while the smaller types show no perceptible change. No values are printed on the chart. The x-axis title is misprinted "Comstock Building Type" with a lowercase s.
