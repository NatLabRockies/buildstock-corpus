## 5.3  Stock Greenhouse Gas Emissions Impact

ComStock simulation results show greenhouse gas emissions avoided across all electricity grid scenarios and on-site combustion fuel types (Figure 11). Greenhouse gas emissions avoided from the electricity grid with the economizer upgrade are 0.2% -0.4% depending on three grid scenarios.

Figure 12. Greenhouse gas emissions comparison of the ComStock baseline and the upgrade scenario. Three electricity grid scenarios are presented: Cambium Long-Run Marginal Emissions Rate (LRMER) High Renewable Energy (RE) Cost 15-Year, Cambium LRMER Low RE Cost 15-Year, and eGrid. MMT stands for million metric tons.

Figure 12 (Section 5.3) compares annual greenhouse gas emissions between the ComStock baseline and the economizer upgrade under three electricity grid scenarios. Each of the three panels holds a Baseline and an Economizer bar on a shared "Annual GHG Emissions (MMT CO2e)" axis from 0 to about 410, stacked by fuel with a legend for Electricity, Natural Gas, Fuel Oil and Propane. All totals and the two large segments are printed. Under eGRID 2021 the total falls from 402 to 401, with electricity 314 to 313 and natural gas flat at 81. Under LRMER High RE Cost 15 it falls from 325 to 324, with electricity 237 to 236. Under LRMER Low RE Cost 15 it falls from 240 to 239, with electricity 152 to 151. Fuel oil and propane are thin slivers at the top of every bar. The natural gas contribution is identical across all three panels because only the grid electricity factor changes between scenarios. Each upgrade bar is labelled "(-0%)" because the figure rounds to whole percentages; the one million metric tons avoided corresponds to 0.25%, 0.31% and 0.42% respectively, which is the 0.2% to 0.4% range the body text reports.
