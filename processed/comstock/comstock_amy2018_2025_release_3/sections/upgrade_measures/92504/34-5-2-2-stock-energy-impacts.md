## 5.2.2  Stock Energy Impacts

The grid peak objective demonstrates 0.73% total site energy savings (35.6 TBtu) for the U.S. commercial building stock modeled in ComStock, with 1.55% savings for applicable buildings only. The savings contributions by end use and fuel type are summarized in Table 7 and illustrated in Figure 11.

Table 7. Summary of Site Energy Savings From Upgrade Measure Application, With Grid Peak Reduction Objective vs. the ComStock Baseline

| End Use/Fuel Type   | Percent Site Energy Savings (All Buildings)   | Percent Site Energy Savings (Applicable Buildings Only)   |   Absolute Site Energy Savings (TBtu) |
|---------------------|-----------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------|
| Total Energy        | 0.73%                                         | 1.55%                                                     |                                  35.6 |
| Total Electricity   | 1.23%                                         | 3.40%                                                     |                                  39.0 |
| Total Natural Gas   | -0.22%                                        | -0.52%                                                    |                                  -3.4 |
| Natural Gas Heating | -0.35%                                        | -0.57%                                                    |                                  -3.4 |
| Electric Heating    | 0.96%                                         | 1.69%                                                     |                                   2.5 |
| Electric Cooling    | 2.06%                                         | 4.21%                                                     |                                  15.0 |
| Electric Fans       | 0.58%                                         | 1.24%                                                     |                                   3.3 |
| Interior Lighting   | 4.09%                                         | 8.04%                                                     |                                  18.0 |

Figure 11. Comparison of annual site energy consumption between the ComStock baseline and the upgrade package scenario, with grid peak reduction objective, for the whole stock (left) and applicable buildings only (right). Energy consumption is categorized both by fuel type and end use.

Figure 11 is a two-panel stacked bar chart of annual site energy consumption in TBtu, comparing the baseline against Package_7 (the grid peak reduction objective). The left panel covers the whole ComStock stock and totals 4865 TBtu at baseline against 4830 TBtu with the package, a 35 TBtu reduction. The right panel covers applicable buildings only and runs 2293 to 2257 TBtu. Legible whole-stock segments, baseline then package: Interior Equipment Electricity 740.2 to 740.2; Fans Electricity 568.5 to 565.2; Cooling Electricity 727.0 to 712.0; Interior Lighting Electricity 440.4 to 422.4; Heating Electricity 260.1 to 257.6; Exterior Lighting Electricity 193.4 to 193.4. Applicable-only segments: Interior Equipment 381.3 to 381.3; Fans 266.5 to 263.2; Cooling Electricity 356.1 to 341.1; Interior Lighting 223.9 to 205.9; Heating Electricity 147.6 to 145.1; Heating Natural Gas 592.7 to 596.1; Exterior Lighting 116.7 to 116.7. Every delta reproduces Table 7 exactly: fans 3.3, cooling 15.0, interior lighting 18.0 and electric heating 2.5 TBtu saved against a 3.4 TBtu natural gas heating penalty, summing to 39.0 TBtu of electricity savings and 35.6 TBtu overall. Savings are consistently smaller than Package_6's because the dispatch window is set by the grid peak rather than each building's own peak.

There are considerable savings for interior lighting, electric heating, and electric cooling energy (8.04%, 1.69%, and 4.21%, respectively for applicable buildings) due to setback controls during the 4-hour peak windows every workday, and the fan energy is reduced accordingly. However, the savings potentials with grid peak objective are lower than the ones with individual peak objective for all the end uses.
