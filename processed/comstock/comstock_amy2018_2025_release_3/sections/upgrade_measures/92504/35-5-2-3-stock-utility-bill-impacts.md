Figure 12 is a three-panel stacked bar chart with the y axis labeled 'Annual Utility Bill (Billion USD, 2022)' running from 0 to 160, comparing the baseline against Package_7, the grid peak reduction objective. Each panel holds a baseline bar and a package bar and the panels differ only in the electricity rate applied: maximum, mean and minimum. Segments are Electricity, Natural Gas, Propane and Fuel Oil, with natural gas flat at 17 billion dollars in all six bars. Maximum rate: total 144 to 143 billion dollars, a 1% reduction, electricity 125 to 124. Mean rate: total 127 to 126, 1%, electricity 108 to 107. Minimum rate: total 112 to 111, 1%, electricity 93 to 92. The percentage change is printed above each pair. Bill savings are about half those of Package_6 in Figure 5, which shows 2% at every rate scenario.

Three sets of bill costs are presented: maximum electricity rate, mean electricity rate and minimum electricity rate.

Figure 13 is a horizontal box plot on an x axis labeled 'Electricity Utility Bill Cost Savings [%]' with ticks from -4 to 8, for Package_7, the grid peak reduction objective. Six rows are grouped into two blocks of three: buildings where the thermostat measure applies to electric cooling only, and buildings where it applies to electric heating and cooling. Within each block the rows are 'With Max Bill', 'With Mean Bill' and 'With Min Bill'. No values are printed; boxes were measured against the tick labels (zero at 222 px, 30.57 px per percent). Electric cooling only, giving Q1, median, Q3 and whisker range: Max Bill 0.95/1.77/2.75, -1.77 to 5.50; Mean Bill 0.98/1.80/2.71, -1.64 to 5.43; Min Bill 0.88/1.73/2.78, -1.96 to 5.66. Electric heating and cooling: Max Bill 0.75/1.73/2.71, -2.26 to 5.76; Mean Bill 0.79/1.73/2.71, -2.19 to 5.72; Min Bill 0.82/1.86/2.85, -2.29 to 5.95. Medians cluster near 1.8%, less than half the 3.7% to 4.3% seen for Package_6 in Figure 6, and unlike Package_6 the two applicability blocks are indistinguishable. The caption calls the three rows the maximum, mean and median bills, but the plotted third row is labeled minimum.
