## Accessing Results

This documentation covers the upgrade methodology for variable refrigerant flow (VRF) with 25% upsizing allowance for heating and briefly discusses key results. Results can be accessed on the ComStock data lake at 'end-use-load-profiles-for-us-building-stock' or via the Data Viewer at comstock.nrel.gov.
