Figure A-1: grouped bar chart of site annual natural gas total energy consumption in TBtu by census division, three bars per division for the baseline, VRF original sizing and VRF 25pct upsizing. East North Central is largest, falling from about 335 to 230 TBtu, and Middle Atlantic from 215 to 150; the two upgrade scenarios are indistinguishable. Appendix A collects these figures.
