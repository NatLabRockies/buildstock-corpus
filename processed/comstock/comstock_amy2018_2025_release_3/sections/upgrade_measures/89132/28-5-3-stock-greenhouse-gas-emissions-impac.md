## 5.3  Stock Greenhouse Gas Emissions Impact

ComStock simulation results show greenhouse gas emissions avoided across all electricity grid scenarios for all on-site combustion fuel types (Figure 6). Across all fuels, greenhouse gas emissions avoided are 1 to 2 million tons of CO2 equivalent (0.4-0.5%), depending on the grid scenario chosen. Because this upgrade is only applicable to 11% of the stock floor area, stocklevel emissions impacts are rather minimal. In addition, cooling and outdoor air were added to some buildings via the Console GHP upgrade, so energy savings potential can be muted by the inclusion of these feature upgrades. Electricity greenhouse gas emissions increased by 2 MMT (0.6% to 1.3%) across the three scenarios. Because this upgrade involves electrifying the space heating load in applicable buildings, an increase in electricity emissions is to be expected.

Natural gas emissions avoided by this upgrade equate to 3 million tons of CO2, or 3.7%. This is driven by transitioning natural gas heating systems in applicable buildings. The natural gas results remain consistent across all three grid scenarios, as these scenarios exclusively pertain to modifications within the electricity grid without affecting natural gas outcomes.

Greenhouse gas emissions comparison in million metric tons of CO2 equivalent (MMT CO2e), shown as three side-by-side panels, one per electricity grid scenario, each holding a Baseline and a Console GHP stacked column. Fuels are stacked and color-coded: Electricity (blue), Natural Gas (yellow), Fuel Oil (green), Propane (orange). Column totals are printed above each bar with the percent change on the upgrade column. eGRID 2021: 402 baseline versus 400 (-1%), with electricity rising 314 to 316 and natural gas falling 81 to 78. LRMER High RE Cost 15: 325 versus 323 (-1%), electricity 237 to 239, natural gas 81 to 78. LRMER Low RE Cost 15: 240 versus 239 (-0%), electricity 152 to 154, natural gas 81 to 78. The pattern is identical across scenarios: electricity emissions increase by about 2 MMT (0.6% to 1.3%) because space heating is electrified, natural gas emissions fall by 3 MMT (3.7%), and net emissions avoided are 1 to 2 MMT (0.4% to 0.6%). Natural gas, fuel oil, and propane values are unchanged across the three panels because the scenarios differ only in the electricity grid. Absolute totals shrink from eGRID to Low RE Cost as the assumed grid gets cleaner.

Three electricity grid scenarios are presented: Cambium Long-Run Marginal Emissions Rate (LRMER) High Renewable Energy (RE) Cost 15-Year, Cambium LRMER Low RE Cost 15-Year, and eGRID. MMT stands for million metric tons, and GHG stands for greenhouse gas.
