## 3.1  Applicability

This measure combines two lighting controls techniques: daylighting controls and occupancy sensors. Each type of lighting control will have its own applicability criteria, but when combined, this measure is applicable to 100% of the stock. We deem a building model to be applicable to this measure if at least one space in the model receives either daylighting controls, occupancy sensors, or both. In a vast majority of buildings, there are many spaces that will end up receiving one or both lighting controls.
