## 1  Technology Summary

The lighting control for load shedding measure applies lighting dimming control to reduce the lighting load during the peak window every weekday. The measure takes daily peak load schedule inputs generated by the method 'Dispatch Schedule Generation' described in the 'Supplemental Documentation: Dispatch Schedule Generation for Demand Flexibility Measures' to determine the start and end times of the predicted daily peak window, and then adjusts the lighting level by a percentage reduction from the original schedules during the peak window to reduce the peak demand.
