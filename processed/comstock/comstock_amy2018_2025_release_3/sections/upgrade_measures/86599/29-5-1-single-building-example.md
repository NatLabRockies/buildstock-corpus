## 5.1 Single Building Example

A 37,500-ft 2 quick service restaurant in Los Angeles with pre-1980 components was selected as a test example. All three measures within the package are applicable to this model. Table 9 shows the initial and final conditions for the three measures. The thermal properties of the model's windows, walls, and roof were all increased, indicating the package was applied correctly.

Table 9. Initial and Final Conditions for Measures Included in the High-Efficiency Envelope Package

|                   | Window Replacement                                                                                                                              | Exterior Wall Insulation                                                                                                                    | Roof Insulation                                                                                                                                      |
|-------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Initial Condition | Existing window simple glazing double-no LowE- tinted/reflective- aluminum has U-4.25 W/m 2 -K (0.75 Btu/hr ft 2 F), 0.484 SHGC, and 0.411 VLT. | The building has 1,348 ft 2 of exterior walls that need and can have exterior insulation applied.                                           | The building had one roof construction: DEER wood-framed roof R-14.18 (R-14).                                                                        |
| Final Condition   | New window CEC T24- CEC9 AEDG window has U-2.27 W/m 2 -K (0.4 Btu/hr ft 2 F), 0.24 SHGC, and 0.26 VLT.                                          | For DEER insulated steel-framed exterior wall R-5.67, add XPS-2 in. to achieve a total assembly R-Value of 15.4, close to the target of 16. | The insulation for roofs was set to R-26-this was applied to 1,000 ft 2 across one roof construction: DEER wood-framed roof R- 14.18 with added roof |
