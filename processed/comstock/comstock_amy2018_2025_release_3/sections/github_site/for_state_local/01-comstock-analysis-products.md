## ComStock Analysis Products
Explore analysis products from the ComStock team that provide regional and local insights. These also provide examples demonstrating how the data can be used to support decision-making.

### Building Segmentation Analysis
The U.S. Department of Energy has developed a series of reports that provide commercial and multifamily building characteristic and energy data for 88 local geographies, with the intention of helping policymakers at the city, county and state levels better understand commercial and multifamily building energy use. [Learn more](docs/for_state_local/segmentation.md).
