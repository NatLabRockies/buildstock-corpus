## 3.3  GHP Borehole Length and Heating Capacity

The total borehole length of the ground heat exchanger and GHP capacity are shown in Table 1 for applicable dwelling units only. The total borehole length is the borehole count of the geothermal loop multiplied by the length of one borehole.

Table 1. GHP Borehole Length and Heating Capacity for Applicable Dwelling Units Only

| Percentile   |   Total Borehole Length [ft] |   Heating Capacity [kBtu/h] |
|--------------|------------------------------|-----------------------------|
| 10           |                          243 |                        16.9 |
| 25           |                          456 |                        32.2 |
| 50           |                          780 |                        52.4 |
| Mean         |                          888 |                        58.0 |
| 75           |                        1,190 |                        76.6 |
| 90           |                        1,664 |                       104.3 |

The mean is larger than the median in the total borehole length and the heating capacity, indicating the upper outliers are causing the data to trend upwards.

Results for the two-stage GHP only can be viewed in the ResStock Measure Documentation: Residential Two-Stage Geothermal Heat Pump document (Fontanini, et al. 2026). To compare the impact of the light envelope improvement versus the advanced envelope improvement, review the ResStock Measure Documentation: Residential Two-Stage Geothermal Heat Pump (4.0 COP, 20.5 EER) With Advanced Envelope Improvements document (Maguire, Fontanini, et al. 2026).
