## Measure Summary

| Measure Title      | Residential Two-Stage Geothermal Heat Pump (4.0 COP, 20.5 EER) With Light Envelope Improvements                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
|--------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Measure Definition | This upgrade measure replaces the HVAC system in any existing dwelling unit in a building less than 8 stories with ducted heating or cooling with a two-stage compressor, water-to-air vertical ground heat exchanger GHP with a variable-speed fan. The measure also adds improvements to the envelope, including attic floor insulation, light air sealing, and duct sealing plus insulation. |
