## 2.4  Two-Stage Water-to-Air Vertical Ground Heat Exchanger GHP Upgrade Details

This section about the GHP is covered in the two-stage GHP document, and is restated here (Fontanini, et al. 2026).

Details specific to the two-stage GHP that may interact with the light envelope improvements are included below. In many cases, the approach used here is the same as when two-stage GHP equipment is installed on its own, but relevant portions of that approach are reiterated here. Since envelope improvements can change the loads and size of equipment installed, this document focuses on the approach to sizing equipment.
