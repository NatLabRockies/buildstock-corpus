## 5.1  Annual Stock Energy Consumption

Figure 4 presents the distributions of annual ResStock energy usage for the baseline and the measure at the national level. With broad adoption of efficient EVs and L2 chargers (87.9% of the housing stock), the national site energy usage increases by 6.3%, which is entirely driven by increases in EV charging electricity demand. Figure 5 shows the annual stock energy consumption by Building America climate zone. Stock energy consumption expectedly increases with the 'Efficient EV Adoption With L2 Charging' measure package in all climate zones. Although EV charging loads are influenced by outdoor temperatures, the effect is much smaller compared to heating, ventilating, and air conditioning (HVAC) loads. As a result, the measure's percentage of change in total energy usage is mostly driven by the proportion of baseline HVAC energy usage, where milder climates will exhibit a higher percentage energy increase from EV adoption. Increases in total consumption range from 4.7% in the 'Very Cold' climate zone to 9.2% in the 'Hot-Dry' climate zone.
