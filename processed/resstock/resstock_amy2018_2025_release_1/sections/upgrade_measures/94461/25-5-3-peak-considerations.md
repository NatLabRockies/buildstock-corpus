## 5.3  Peak Considerations

Figure 7 shows the electricity demand for the state of California over 3 days in the winter (left) and 3 days in the summer (right). The dashed line is the demand from the baseline scenario. This measure results in new electricity peaks coinciding with the average time an occupant returns home and aligns closely with the timing of the baseline daily peaks in both seasons. Note that this measure is not meant to reflect a realistic scenario on its own. Rather, it is designed to offer the users flexibility when combined with the baseline or other measures. As such, results should be interpreted with caution and not assumed to reflect real-world outcomes.
