## Measure Summary

| Measure Title      | Efficient EV Adoption With L2 Charging                                                                                                                                     |
|--------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Measure Definition | This measure adds one EV to each occupied dwelling unit. With each EV, an L2 charger is added, and the EV battery efficiency is increased by 15% compared to the baseline. |
| Applicability      | This measure applies to all occupied dwelling units.                                                                                                                       |
| Not Applicable     | This measure does not apply to vacant units.                                                                                                                               |
