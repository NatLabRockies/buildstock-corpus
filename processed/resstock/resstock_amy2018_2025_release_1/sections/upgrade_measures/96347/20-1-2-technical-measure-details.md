## 1.2  Technical Measure Details

To maximize energy efficiency and based on building science principles, the correct order for implementing this upgrade package is: (1) advanced air sealing, (2) duct sealing, and (3) attic floor insulation. Reducing air leakage is the foundation of energy efficiency. It helps control indoor temperatures and moisture and improves the effectiveness of insulation and HVAC systems. Sealing leaks before adding insulation ensures the insulation performs as intended. It also makes leaks easier to identify and reduces the burden on field staff, since they are not working around additional insulation. Once air sealing targets are met, it is essential to address the HVAC system by sealing any leaky ducts to prevent the loss of conditioned air, particularly in unconditioned spaces like attics or crawlspaces. Attic floor insulation should be installed last. While highly effective, it must follow air and duct sealing to avoid covering up critical areas that require sealing and to ensure ducts running through the attic are properly sealed and insulated beforehand.

Therefore, we assume that upgrades will be implemented in the following sequence: Advanced air sealing is applied first to applicable dwelling units, with the goal of achieving 5 ACH50. While some regions require tighter envelopes for new construction, 5 ACH50 reflects the average infiltration rate achieved in empirical studies across representative climate zones and ensures that selected homes do not already comply with 2024 IECC requirements (Phoung, Lou, &amp; White, 2025a).

After air sealing, HVAC ducts in applicable homes are sealed and insulated to achieve the following:

- A maximum of 5% duct air leakage to the outside
- Duct insulation level of R-8.

Attic floor insulation is the upgraded in applicable dwelling units to the following levels:

- R-49 in climate zone 1
- R-60 in climate zones 2 through 8.

More details about the modeling assumptions for these three measures can be found in the accompanying documentation (Phoung, Lou, &amp; White, 2025a) (Phoung, Lou, &amp; White, 2025b) (Lou &amp; Liu, 2025).
