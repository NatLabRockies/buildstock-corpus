## 3  Modeling Approach

The modeling approach of the three individual measures can be found in the accompanying documentation (Phoung, Lou, &amp; White, 2025a) (Phoung, Lou, &amp; White, 2025b) (Lou &amp; Liu, 2025).

Electrical panel constraint modeling was added to ResStock via OpenStudio-HPXML measures in 2025 to assess whether the existing panel has enough capacity or breaker space to accommodate new loads from an energy retrofit. For details on panel constraint modeling, refer to the ResStock Technical Reference Guide (Reyna, et al., 2025). This measures does not introduce new electrical loads or circuits, so it has no impact on the electrical panel. However, electrical panel metrics are included as a standard output for this and all other envelope measures. Any panel constraints appearing in the upgrade results reflect pre-existing conditions, as this measure does not worsen them.
