## 4.3  Stock Utility Bill Impacts

Figure 12 shows a comparison of utility bills with this measure applied. Stock-wide total bill savings are 16%, with an 11% reduction in electricity bills and a 26% reduction in natural gas bills. This is similar to the magnitude of overall stock-level site energy savings.
