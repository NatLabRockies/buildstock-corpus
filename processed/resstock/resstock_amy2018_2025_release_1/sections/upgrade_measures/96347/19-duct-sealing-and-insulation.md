## Duct Sealing and Insulation

Ducts distribute heated or cooled air throughout the house. In many homes, especially older ones, these ducts have leaks that allow conditioned air to escape into unconditioned or semi-conditioned spaces like attics or crawlspaces. As a result, the system must work harder and use more energy to maintain the desired temperature. Duct sealing addresses these leaks using mastic, metal tape, or an aerosol-based sealant, depending on the situation. Sealing ducts improves system efficiency, maintains more consistent room temperatures, and enhances indoor air quality by preventing dirty attic or crawlspace air from entering the ducts. Duct sealing can also reduce unintentional pressure imbalances, which may cause indoor air quality or combustion safety concerns. See the duct sealing documentation for more information on methods and materials (Lou &amp; Liu, 2025).
