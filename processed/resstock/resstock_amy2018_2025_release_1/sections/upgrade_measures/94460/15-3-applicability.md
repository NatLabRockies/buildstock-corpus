## 3  Applicability

The 'EV Adoption With Level 2 Charging' measure applies to all occupied dwelling units without an L2 charger in the baseline scenario. This logic corresponds to 87.9% of the existing housing stock. Units with existing EVs are upgraded to an L2 charger, if applicable, but all driving behavior remains the same. Only a single EV is adopted per dwelling unit, as modeling multiple vehicles is not currently supported. This measure design allows flexibility when analyzing scenarios of adoption. For example, a user can select a percentage of a specific segment to adopt this measure while using the results from the baseline simulation for the remaining models. Units in multi-family buildings are included in the measure. Although multi-family households may not own the charging infrastructure or see its impact on their utility bills in practice, we model them consistently with other housing types, treating EV energy as a direct household energy cost. We also assume that multi-family chargers are always available, which may not be true in practice, and therefore the charging event durations may not reflect actual events.
