## Accessing Results

This documentation covers the 'duct sealing and insulation' measure upgrade methodology and briefly discusses key results. All results are available on the ResStock™ Open Energy Data Initiative 'End-Use Load Profiles for the U.S. Building Stock' data lake and on the data viewer at resstock.nlr.gov.
