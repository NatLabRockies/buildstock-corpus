## Measure Summary

| Measure Title                 | Duct Sealing and Insulation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
|-------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Measure Definition            | This upgrade measure involves sealing HVAC ducts and adding insulation in applicable dwelling units. After the upgrade, the units will achieve 5% duct leakage to the outside and an R-8 duct insulation level.                                                                                                                       |
| Applicability                 | This measure applies to all dwellings with ductwork located in unconditioned space that have one or both of the following characteristics: • More than 6% duct leakage to the outside • Less than R-6 duct insulation. With these applicability constraints, the measure upgrade applies to about 46% of the existing dwelling units. |
| Not Applicable                | This measure does not apply to dwelling units with any of the following characteristics: • 6% (or less) duct leakage to the outside and R-6 (or greater) duct insulation • 0% leakage to the outside. These constraints exclude 54% of the dwelling units from receiving this measure upgrade.                                        |
