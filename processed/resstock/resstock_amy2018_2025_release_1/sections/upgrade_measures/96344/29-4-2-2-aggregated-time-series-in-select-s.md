## 4.2.2 Aggregated Time Series in Select State During the Peak Day

Colorado is selected as a case study to analyze the aggregated time-series data. Of all national dwelling units, 1.7% are in Colorado. Figure 8 shows the aggregated average daily baseline energy consumption in Colorado, with February 20 identified as the day with the highest energy use. To further examine this peak, Figure 9 presents the aggregated average power on February 20 in Colorado, comparing conditions before and after the duct sealing and insulation upgrade.

Figure 8. Aggregated daily average energy consumption of baseline in Colorado

![Image](96344_images/image_000009_e64a67a1db219ee638f2ab8ec7a68c5933bf86fd09d444004b6cff66b8c068d1.png)

Figure 9 shows that the duct sealing and insulation scenario consistently results in lower power demand throughout the day compared to the baseline, particularly during peak heating hours. The peak demand decreases from 12.9 kW per unit to 11.4 kW per unit, representing a 12% reduction.

Figure 9. Aggregated average power data in Colorado on peak day
