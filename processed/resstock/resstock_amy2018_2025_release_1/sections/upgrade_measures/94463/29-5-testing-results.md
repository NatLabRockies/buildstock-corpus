## 5  Testing Results

This section reports results from the national-scale ResStock run, demonstrating the functionality of the measure and verifying its correct operation. The analysis is presented to illustrate the measure's performance and ensure confidence in its implementation, not to reflect a realistic scenario on its own. The measure is designed to offer the users flexibility when combined with the baseline or other measures. As such, results presented here should be interpreted with caution.

We simulated this measure and two other scenarios as points of comparison, as summarized in Table 5. The baseline represents the ResStock baseline, which includes a low saturation of EVs, whereas the 'EV with L2' charging scenario has the same set of EV characteristics as this measure except for the application of DF. Although this section presents the energy and unmet driving hour impacts of the measure, the full set of outputs available in the dataset includes annual and timeseries charging energy, charging emissions, utility bills, and EV schedules.

Table 5. Summary of the Three Scenarios Modeled To Analyze the Results of This Measure

| Scenario                    | EV Assigned to All   | Charger Assigned to All   | DF Enabled   |
|-----------------------------|----------------------|---------------------------|--------------|
| Baseline                    | No                   | No                        | No           |
| EV + L2                     | Yes                  | L2                        | No           |
| EV + L2 + DF (this measure) | Yes                  | L2                        | Yes          |
