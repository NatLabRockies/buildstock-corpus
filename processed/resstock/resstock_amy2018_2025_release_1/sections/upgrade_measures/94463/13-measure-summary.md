## Measure Summary

| Measure Title      | EV Adoption With L2 Charging and DF                                                                                                                                                                                        |
|--------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Measure Definition | This measure adds one EV to each occupied dwelling unit. With each EV comes an L2 charger. Existing level 1 (L1) chargers are upgraded to L2 chargers. The chargers provide DF by suspending charging during peak periods. |
| Applicability      | This measure applies to all occupied dwelling units in the baseline-87.9% of dwelling units.                                                                                                                               |
| Not Applicable     | This measure does not apply to vacant dwelling units-12.1% of dwelling units.                                                                                                                                              |
