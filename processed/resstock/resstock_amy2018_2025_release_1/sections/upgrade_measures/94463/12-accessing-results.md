## Accessing Results

This documentation covers the 'Electric Vehicle (EV) Adoption With Level 2 (L2) Charging and Demand Flexibility (DF)' measure package methodology and briefly discusses key results. All results can be accessed on the ResStock TM  Open Energy Data Initiative End-Use Load Profiles for the U.S. Building Stock Data Lake and on the data viewer at resstock.nlr.gov.
