## 2 Applicability

This upgrade applies only to dwellings with existing ducted systems. The choice to apply this upgrade to homes without current access to natural gas was made to evaluate the theoretical impacts of switching to this fuel. Whether or not installing new gas distribution to supply existing homes is financially or technically feasible is outside the scope of this document.

However, this upgrade could be used as part of an analysis into the financial viability of installing new gas distribution and the benefits-costs to households. The upgrade replaces the existing heating system with a 95% AFUE natural gas furnace. If a dwelling unit that has a heating system that also provides air conditioning, like a heat pump, the air conditioning is removed as part of the upgrade. No changes to infiltration are assumed as part of the flue removal or installation process.

Approximately 74.3% of the residential building stock meets the conditions for this upgrade. Details on the application logic are in Table 1.
