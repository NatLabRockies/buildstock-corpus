Natural gas is common across each census division. Electricity is most prevalent in the South Atlantic, accounting for nearly 14.5% of the U.S. housing stock. Fuel oil is more prevalent in New England and the Middle Atlantic census divisions, which have historically used fuel oil in older and more rural buildings. Propane is used less frequently but is evenly distributed across the U.S. housing st ock, as it is primarily used in more rural areas that don't have access to natural gas service.
