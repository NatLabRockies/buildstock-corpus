**Upgrade Summary, part 1 of 2**

| Measure Title | Upgrade Description |
|---|---|
| Measure Definition | Replaces existing space-heating equipment with a high-efficiency natural gas furnace (95% AFUE). The upgrade also converts heating fuel to natural gas (if not already) and sets the heating type and fuel to 'Natural Gas Fuel Furnace . ' In cases where the space-heating equipment also provides cooling or air conditioning, like a heat pump, the cooling is removed. |
| Applicability | This upgrade applies to dwellings that have existing ducted systems. No restrictions are placed on prior heating fuel or access to natural gas infrastructure. Applies to dwellings that: • Have ducts, and • Do not use wood, 'other fuel,' or have no primary heating fuel, and • Has stand-alone HVAC (no part of the HVAC is shared with other dwelling units) No restriction is placed on prior heating fuel - homes are converted to natural gas within the upgrade logic. With these applicability constraints, the upgrade applies to 74.3% of the existing dwelling units. |

**Upgrade Summary, part 2 of 2**

| Measure Title | Upgrade Description |
|---|---|
| Not Applicable | This upgrade does not apply to: • Dwellings without ducts • Dwellings with primary heating fuel of wood , other fuel , or none • Dwellings served by shared HVAC systems , including shared heating only, shared cooling only, or both These constraints exclude 25.7% of the dwelling units from receiving this upgrade. |
