## Measure Summary

| Measure Title                 | HVAC Load Flexibility                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
|-------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Measure Definition            | This upgrade lowers the heating set point and increases the cooling set point during predefined on-peak hours. It can also preheat or precool the dwelling units for 1 to 4 hours, depending on the measure settings. |
| Applicability                 | This measure applies to all dwellings with either a heating system or a cooling system that is approximately 99.7% of the building stock.                                                                             |
| Not Applicable                | This measure does not apply to the dwelling units that have neither a heating system nor a cooling system - approximately 0.3% of the building stock.                                                                 |
