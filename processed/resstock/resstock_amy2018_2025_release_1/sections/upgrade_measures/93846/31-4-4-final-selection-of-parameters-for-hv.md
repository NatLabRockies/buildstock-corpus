## 4.4  Final Selection of Parameters for HVAC Load Flexibility Measure

After further reviewing the existing demand response programs and considering the limitations of the likely downstream modeling tools, we eliminated scenarios where load shifting and load shedding were applied at the same time. Table 8 shows the final selection of the load flexibility options that we believe cover the likely range of demand flexibility programs.

Table 8. Final Selection of Demand Flexibility Options to Cover the Likely Program Space

|                                                            | Pre-Peak   | Pre-Peak    | On-Peak    | On-Peak     |
|------------------------------------------------------------|------------|-------------|------------|-------------|
| Upgrade                                                    | Duration   | Offset (°F) | Duration   | Offset (°F) |
| 1 (on-peak load shedding, 4 hours, 2°F offset) No pre-peak |            | N/A         | 4 hours    | 2           |
| 2 (pre-peak load shifting, 4 hours, 2°F offset) 4 hours    |            | 2           | No on-peak | N/A         |
| 3 (on-peak load shedding, 4 hours, 4°F offset) No pre-peak |            | N/A         | 4 hours    | 4           |
| 4 (pre-peak load shifting, 4 hours, 4°F offset) 4          | hours      | 4           | No on-peak | N/A         |
| 5 (pre-peak load shifting, 1 hour, 2°F offset)             | 1 hour     | 2           | No on-peak | N/A         |
