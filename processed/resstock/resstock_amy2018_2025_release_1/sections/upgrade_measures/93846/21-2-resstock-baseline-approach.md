## 2 ResStock Baseline Approach

The baseline assumption for thermostats in ResStock is a range of heating and cooling set points and setbacks or setups derived from the Residential Energy Consumption Survey (RECS) 2020, which vary based on climate zone, building type, HVAC type, and tenure (U.S. Energy Information Administration, 2023). These set points are assumed to apply year-round. Approximately one-third of dwelling units are assumed to have heating and/or cooling thermostat setbacks or setups, where the set point is changed by the homeowner during part of each day. The distribution of set points in the building stock is shown in Figure 1. ResStock makes no explicit assumption about whether these setbacks or setups are achieved using manual adjustment, a programmable thermostat, or a smart thermostat. In practice, demand response programs typically incentivize or directly pay for customers to install smart thermostats as a condition of program participation.
