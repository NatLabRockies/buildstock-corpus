## 1.2  Technical Measure Details

Demand flexibility is commonly categorized into four groups: load shaping, load shifting, load shedding, and load shimmying (Peter Alstone, 2017). In this analysis, we consider load shifting and load shedding. In the following discussion, the peak period, or 'on -peak window,' is the window of time when the peak is to be reduced. The pre-peak window is the window of time immediately preceding the peak period.
