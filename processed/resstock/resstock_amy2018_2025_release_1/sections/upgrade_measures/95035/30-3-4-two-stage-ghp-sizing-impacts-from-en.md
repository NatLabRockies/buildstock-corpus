## 3.4 Two-Stage GHP Sizing Impacts from Envelope Improvements

Figure 9 shows the average HVAC capacity for two-stage GHPs with and without envelope improvements being included. As with the ground heat exchanger length, the size of HVAC equipment is very influenced by the loads in the building, and envelope improvements can substantially reduce the required capacity. This also presents substantial cost savings. While the ground heat exchanger installation costs are generally much larger than the cost of the heat pump, this can also represent some cost savings when installing a GHP system where the costs of installing both envelope improvements and a GHP might end up having a lower overall first cost as the lower GHP installation costs more than offset the cost of envelope improvements.
