## Measure Summary

Table 1. Upgrade Measure Overview

| Measure Title                                  | Residential Two-Stage Geothermal Heat Pump (4.0 COP, 20.5 EER) with Envelope Improvements and Advanced Air Sealing                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
|------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Measure Definition                             | This upgrade measure replaces the HVAC system in any existing dwelling unit in a building less than 8 stories with ducted heating or cooling with a two-stage compressor, water-to-air vertical ground heat exchanger geothermal heat pump (GHP) with a variable speed fan. The measure also adds some improvements to the envelope, including increasing attic insulation, general air sealing of the living space, and duct sealing plus insulation.                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Applicability                                  | This measure applies to all dwellings with: - Non-shared (in-unit) HVAC and any of the following heating fuels: electricity, natural gas, fuel oil, or propane - Shared (across multiple units) HVAC and a building height of < 8 stories and any of the following heating fuels: electricity, natural gas, fuel oil, or propane - Dwelling units with air ducts - Dwelling units with soil thermal conductivity greater than 0.8 Btu/h-ft-°F With these applicability constraints, the measure upgrade applies to 71% of the existing residential building stock. 6.4% of the residential units that receive the GHP upgrade lack existing space cooling systems in the baseline. |
| Not Applicable                                 | This measure does not apply to dwelling units: - Shared HVAC (across multiple units) in high-rise ( ≥ 8 floors) buildings. - Dwelling units without air ducts These constraints exclude 25% of the building stock from receiving this measure upgrade. An additional 4% of the dwelling units do not receive this upgrade because of their heating fuel type.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
