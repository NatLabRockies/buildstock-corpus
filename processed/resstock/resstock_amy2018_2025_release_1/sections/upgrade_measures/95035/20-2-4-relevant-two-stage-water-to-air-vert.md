## 2.4 Relevant Two-Stage Water-to-Air Vertical Ground Heat Exchanger GHP Upgrade Details

Details specific to the two-stage GHP that may interact with the envelope improvements are included below. In many cases, the approach used here is the same as when two-stage GHP equipment is installed on its own, but relevant portions of that approach are reiterated here to remind readers of the specifics.
