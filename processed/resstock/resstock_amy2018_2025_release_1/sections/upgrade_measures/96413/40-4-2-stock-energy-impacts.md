## 4.2  Stock Energy Impacts

Over the entire stock (including dwellings that are not applicable), this measure upgrade yields a total modeled energy consumption decrease of 30.0% as seen in Figure 7. Figure 8 shows total energy consumption for only dwellings that are applicable to the upgrade. When only these dwellings are considered, total savings from baseline are 36.1%. These savings in Figure 8 are driven by a 64.5% decrease in heating energy from baseline to the upgrade scenario. A slight decrease of 11.1% in cooling energy also contributes.

The total falls from 9,788 to 6,253 TBtu. Fuel heating segments shrink sharply and electric heating grows; cooling falls slightly.

Figure 9 shows the same total consumption as Figure 8 but sorted by baseline heating fuel. Most savings occur in dwellings with an original heating fuel of natural gas where total annual energy decreases by 40.5%. Dwellings with a baseline fuel of fuel oil also see a large decrease in percent energy consumed, with the upgrade scenario lowering total energy by 49.6%. Total energy is decreased by 10.3% and 44.0% for electricity and propane baseline heating fuels, respectively.

Figure 9: four-panel stacked bar chart of annual energy consumption (TBtu, 0 to about 6,000) for applicable dwellings, one panel per baseline heating fuel: electricity, fuel oil, natural gas and propane. Each panel has a Baseline and a Typical Cold Climate HP bar stacked by end use and fuel. Natural gas homes are the largest group and fall from 6,069 to 3,606 TBtu; electricity homes fall from 2,521 to 2,009.

Figure 10 sorts the annual total energy consumption by Building America climate zone. The largest magnitude of savings occurs in the Cold climate zone where 1,930 TBtu (45.2%) are saved with the application of the cold climate ASHP. Percent savings decrease as climate zones get warmer because less heating energy is necessary for homes. For example, the Hot-Humid climate zone only sees savings of 18.1%, driven by heating energy savings of 59.7% and cooling energy savings of 12.4%.
