## 1.2.6 Cooling Levels

Dwelling units that are not 100% cooled by their existing AC system (e.g., room AC providing cooling for just one room of a home) will be 100% cooled by this measure upgrade. This will increase cooling energy consumption of those dwelling units that were not already 100% cooled. The cooling level increase for all applicable dwelling units is implemented because ASHPs in this measure upgrade will be sized as large as the duct system allows to attempt to cool the entire dwelling unit. Although this increases energy use associated with cooling, it also increases health and comfort of the home.
