## 4.3  Stock Energy Utility Bill Impacts

Figure 11 shows a average of energy utility costs per dwelling of households that received the cold climate ASHP upgrade. On average, electricity bill costs are slightly higher in the upgrade because of the increase in electricity usage. There is a decrease in the average cost of natural gas, fuel oil, and propane utility bills, and the bill weighed average of total utility costs in the upgrade is less than the baseline average.

Figure 11. Average energy utility bill costs per dwelling of applied households

Figure 12 shows the average energy utility bill cost grouped by Building America climate zone. All climate zones see a decrease in the total utility bill cost. The significant increase in electric bill cost for most climate zones is due to the increased electricity usage.
