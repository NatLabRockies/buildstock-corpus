## 4.1  Single Dwelling Testing

To confirm that the cold climate upgrade measure model behaves as expected, two dwelling test models are analyzed in this section. Some pertinent characteristics are outlined in Table 6. Both dwellings are single-family detached households-the most common building type under this upgrade's applicability logic. Both dwellings have natural gas furnaces and central AC units. Dwelling 1 is in the colder climate zone of 6A and Dwelling 2 is in the hotter climate of 2A.

Table 6. Characteristics of Two Individual Dwelling Test Models

| Dwelling ID           | 1                              | 2                              |
|-----------------------|--------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Building Type         | Single-Family Detached         | Single-Family Detached         |
| IECC Climate Zone     | 6A                             | 2A                             |
| State                 | WI                             | TX                             |
| Baseline Heating Type | Natural Gas Furnace (80% AFUE) | Natural Gas Furnace (80% AFUE) |
| Baseline Cooling Type | Central AC, SEER 13            | Central AC, SEER 15            |
