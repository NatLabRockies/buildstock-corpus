## 1.2  Technical Measure Details

This ASHP option reflects an installation of a ducted ASHP into a building that already has existing ductwork for either heating or cooling. This entails the ASHP indoor coil and blower being installed with the dwelling's existing air distribution system and an outdoor unit coil, fan, and compressor unit being installed outside.
