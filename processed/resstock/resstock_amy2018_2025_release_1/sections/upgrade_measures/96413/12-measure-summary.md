## Measure Summary

| Measure Title                 | Typical Cold Climate Air-Source Heat Pump                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
|-------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Measure Definition            | This upgrade measure replaces any existing non-shared ducted heating and air conditioning system with a variable-speed ducted air-source heat pump (ASHP) with electric resistance backup. If the unit does not have air conditioning, the unit will receive air conditioning through the ASHP. The technology applied in this measure upgrade package is denoted in the ResStock options_lookup.tsv as the following: • Typical Cold Climate Heat Pump, HSPF2 8.5, SEER2 17.5. |
| Applicability                 | This measure applies to all dwellings with: • Ducted non-shared (in-unit) HVAC with any of the following heating fuels: electricity, natural gas, fuel oil, or propane. With these applicability constraints, the measure upgrade applies to 74.3% of the existing dwelling units.                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Not Applicable                | This measure does not apply to dwelling units with the following: • Shared HVAC • Non-ducted non-shared HVAC. These constraints exclude 25.7% of the dwelling units from receiving this measure upgrade                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
