## 1.2.1  Compressor Speed

This ASHP option has a variable-speed compressor, because virtually all cold climate ASHPs on the market include variable-speed compressors. Variable-speed compressors allow the system to optimize energy consumption by ramping the compressor speed up or down depending on the indoor and outdoor conditions. Compared to a single-stage or two-stage options, this ASHP can more efficiently operate at extreme conditions because of the multiple speeds available to the compressor.
