## 4.1.1 Single Dwelling Annual Results

Table 7 outlines the modeled savings in annual energy consumption for various key outputs for both individual dwelling test models. Both dwellings show savings in an expected manner. The N/A values for 'Electricity-Heating (incl. backup)' stem from the lack of any electric heating in the dwellings in the baseline. The nonzero electric heating in the upgrade reflects an infinite increase in this category's energy consumption for both dwellings. For Dwelling 1, the increase in electric heating can be seen reflected in the 'Utility Bills-Electricity' category. However, since Dwelling 2 does not require nearly as many heating hours as Dwelling 1, its savings for 'Utility Bills-Electricity' remains positive with savings from the cooling mode of the ASHP outweighing the energy consumption of the ASHP's heating mode.
