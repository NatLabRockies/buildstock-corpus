## Accessing Results

This documentation covers the 'typical cold climate air-source heat pump' measure upgrade methodology and briefly discusses key results. All results can be accessed on the ResStock™ Open Energy Data Initiative 'End-Use Load Profiles for the U.S. Building Stock' data lake and on the data viewer at resstock.nlr.gov/datasets.
