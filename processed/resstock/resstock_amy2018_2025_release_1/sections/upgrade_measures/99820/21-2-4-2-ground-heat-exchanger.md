## 2.4.2 Ground Heat Exchanger

The variable-speed GHP uses the same type of ground heat exchanger as the stand-alone variable-speed GHP measure. Both use thermally enhanced grout and pipe materials to improve heat transfer, and the loop is installed in a vertical, rectangular configuration.
