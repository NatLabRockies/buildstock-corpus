## 3.2  Annual Energy Bill Impact for Applicable Dwelling Units Only

Energy bills for applicable dwelling units are shown in this section. The average energy bill for the variable-speed GHP with light envelope improvements is shown in Figure 6. On average, total energy bills decreased by 30% (over $600 per year) for the variable-speed GHP with light envelope improvements compared to baseline dwelling units. All fuel costs decreased on average, with 13% savings on electricity bills, 66% savings on natural gas bills, 85% savings on propane bills, and 93% savings on fuel oil bills.

Figure 6: horizontal grouped bar chart. Rows are electricity, natural gas, fuel oil, propane and all fuels; x-axis is bills in US dollars. Series are Baseline and 'Variable Speed GHP + Envelope'. The all-fuels bill falls from about $2,800 to about $1,950, the natural gas bill from about $570 to about $190, and the electricity bill from about $2,000 to about $1,750; the fuel oil and propane bills, each about $110 at baseline, fall to near zero.

Figure 7 shows average energy bills for applicable dwelling units only broken out by Building America climate zone. The variable-speed GHP with light envelope improvements results in a lower average energy bill across all climate zones, with bills in the Subarctic region showing the largest magnitude savings (over $2,000 per year).
