## 1  Technology Summary

This measure combines two separate measures into a single package. The first measure is a water-to-air GHP with a variable-speed compressor rated at a COP of 4.4 and an EER of 30.9. More details on GHPs and how they are modeled in ResStock 2025 Release 1, including the details of modeling the ground heat exchanger, are included in the single-stage GHP measure document (Maguire, et al., 2026). The details of how variable-speed GHPs with this rated efficiency are modeled are included in the variable-speed GHP measure document (Maguire, et al., 2026). The details of the light envelope improvement measure package are included in the light air sealing, attic insulation, and duct sealing improvement measure document (Brossman &amp; White, 2026).
