## Accessing Results

This documentation covers the 'Residential Variable-Speed Geothermal Heat Pump (4.4 COP, 30.9 EER) With Light Envelope Improvements' measure upgrade methodology and briefly discusses key results. All results can be accessed on the ResStock™ Open Energy Data Initiative 'End-Use Load Profiles for the U.S. Building Stock' data lake and on the Data Viewer at resstock.nlr.gov.
