## 2.4  Variable-Speed Water-to-Air Vertical Ground Heat Exchanger GHP Upgrade Details

Details specific to the variable-speed GHP that may interact with the light envelope improvements are included below. In many cases, the approach used here is the same as when the variable-speed GHP equipment is installed on its own, but relevant portions of that approach are reiterated here. Since envelope improvements can change the loads and size of equipment installed, this document focuses on the approach to sizing equipment
