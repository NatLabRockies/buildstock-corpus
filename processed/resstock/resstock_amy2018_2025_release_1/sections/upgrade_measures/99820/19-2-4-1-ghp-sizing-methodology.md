## 2.4.1  GHP Sizing Methodology

The variable-speed GHP uses the same sizing methodology as the single- and two-stage GHPs (Maguire, et al., 2026) (Fontanini, et al., 2026). The design heating and cooling loads are calculated in accordance with Air Conditioning Contractors of America (ACCA) Manuals J (ACCA, 2016), and the equipment size is selected based on ACCA Manual S (ACCA, 2014) using a routine included in OpenStudio-HPXML. Note that because the GHP sizing routine includes accounting of the peak heating and cooling building loads, equipment sizes are decreased when envelope improvements are included. This means that for the combined package the equipment capacity, ground heat exchanger size, and associated cost for installing a GHP may be reduced compared to just installing a variable-speed GHP.
