## Measure Summary

| Measure Title      | Residential Variable-Speed Geothermal Heat Pump (4.4 COP, 30.9 EER) With Light Envelope Improvements                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
|--------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Measure Definition | This upgrade measure replaces the HVAC system in any existing dwelling unit in a building less than 8 stories with ducted heating or cooling with a variable-speed, water-to-air vertical ground heat exchanger GHP with a variable-speed fan. The measure also adds improvements to the envelope, including attic floor insulation, light air sealing, and duct sealing plus insulation. |

Applicable This GHP measure in this package applies to all dwellings with:

- Non-shared (in-unit) HVAC and any of the following heating fuels: electricity, natural gas, fuel oil, or propane
- Shared (across multiple units) HVAC and a building height of &lt;8 stories and any of the following heating fuels: electricity, natural gas, fuel oil, or propane
- Dwelling units with air ducts
- Dwelling units with soil thermal conductivity greater than 0.8 Btu/hr-ft-°F.

The light envelope measures in this package apply according to the following criteria:

- Light air sealing measure applies to dwellings with:
- o Infiltration rate of 10 ACH50 or more
- Attic insulation measure applies to dwellings with:
- o Unfinished attic &amp; less than R-49 attic floor insulation &amp; in climate zone 1
- o Unfinished attic &amp; less than R-60 attic floor insulation &amp; in climate zones 2 through 8
- Duct sealing and insulation measure applies to dwellings with:
- o More than 6% duct leakage to outside
- o Less than R-6 duct insulation.

The GHP must be applicable in order for the dwelling unit to also be eligible for the light envelope measures.

With these applicability constraints, the measure upgrade applies to 71% of the existing residential building stock. 6.4% of the residential units that receive the GHP upgrade lack existing space cooling systems in the baseline and therefore receive a central cooling system via the GHP.
