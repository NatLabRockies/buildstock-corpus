## Measure Summary

| Measure Title                 | Residential Geothermal Heat Pump (4.0 COP, 20.5 EER)-Two-Stage Compressor, Water-to-Air Geothermal Heat Pump (GHP) With Vertical Ground Heat Exchanger                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
|-------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Measure Definition            | This upgrade measure replaces the HVAC system in any existing dwelling unit in a building less than 8 stories with either ducted heating or ducted cooling to a two-stage compressor and variable-speed fan, water-to-air heat pump with vertical ground heat exchanger.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Applicability                 | This measure applies to all dwellings with: • Non-shared (in-unit) HVAC and any of the following heating fuels: electricity, natural gas, fuel oil, or propane • Shared (across multiple units) HVAC and a building height of <8 stories and any of the following heating fuels: electricity, natural gas, fuel oil, or propane • Dwelling units with air ducts • Dwelling units with soil thermal conductivity greater than 0.8 Btu/hr-ft-°F. With these applicability constraints, the measure upgrade applies to 71% of the existing residential building stock. 6.4% of the residential units that receive the GHP upgrade lack existing space cooling systems in the baseline. |
| Not Applicable                | This measure does not apply to dwelling units with: • Shared HVAC (across multiple units) in high-rise ( ≥ 8 floors) buildings (e.g. multifamily residential buildings) • Dwelling units without air ducts. These constraints exclude 25% of the dwelling units from receiving this measure upgrade. An additional 4% of the dwelling units do not receive this upgrade because of their heating fuel type.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
