## 3.3  Annual Energy Bill Impact for Applicable Dwelling Units Only

Energy bills for applicable dwelling units only are shown in the following figures. On average, energy bills decreased 11% for the two-stage GHP. This is a greater percent savings than the single-stage GHP, which was a 3% energy bill decrease on average. The average energy bill for the two-stage GHP is shown in Figure 32. Electricity bills increased slightly, while natural gas, fuel oil, and propane bills all decreased. Electric bills increasing on average is to be expected because of switching from non-electric heating to electric heating, and then some units receiving cooling if they did not have air conditioning before the two-stage GHP.

When comparing the average single-stage GHP energy bill to the average two-stage GHP energy bills, the two-stage GHP energy bill is about 12% less than the single-stage energy bill. This is seen in Figure 33.
