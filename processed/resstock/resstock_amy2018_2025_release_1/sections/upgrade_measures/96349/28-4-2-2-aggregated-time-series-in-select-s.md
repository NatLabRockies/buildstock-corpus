## 4.2.2 Aggregated Time Series in Select State During the Peak Day

Colorado is selected as a case study to analyze the aggregated time-series data. Of all national dwelling units, 1.7% are in Colorado. Figure 9 shows the aggregated average daily baseline energy consumption in Colorado, with February 20 identified as the day with the highest energy use. To further examine this peak, Figure 10 presents the aggregated average power on February 20 in Colorado, comparing conditions before and after the envelope package upgrade.

Figure 9. Aggregated daily average energy consumption of baseline in Colorado

![Image](96349_images/image_000010_228491704945e3f11f4e1c4c3ca360ea4915d79705a4eb2e236c061b5e7878b5.png)

Figure 10 shows that the envelope package scenario consistently results in the power demand throughout the day compared to the baseline. The peak demand decreases from 12.9 kW per unit to 8.6 kW per unit, representing a 33% reduction.

Figure 10. Aggregated average power data in Colorado on peak day
