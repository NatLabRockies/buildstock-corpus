## 1.2  Technical Measure Details

To maximize energy efficiency and based on building science principles, the correct order for implementing this upgrade package is: (1) air sealing, (2) duct sealing, (3) attic floor insulation, and (4) drill-and-fill wall insulation. Reducing air leakage is the foundation of energy efficiency. It helps control indoor temperatures and moisture, and improves the effectiveness of insulation and HVAC systems. Sealing leaks before adding insulation ensures the insulation performs as intended. It also makes leaks easier to identify and reduces the burden on field staff, since they are not working around additional insulation. Once air sealing targets are met, it is essential to address the HVAC system by sealing any leaky ducts to prevent the loss of conditioned air, particularly in unconditioned spaces like attics or crawlspaces. With both air leaks and ducts sealed, attic floor insulation should be installed. Finally, insulation can be added to uninsulated wall cavities to reduce conductive heat loss.

Therefore, we assume that upgrades will be implemented in the following sequence: Advanced air sealing is applied first to applicable dwelling units, with the goal of achieving 5 ACH50. While some regions require tighter envelopes for new constructions, 5 ACH50 reflects the average infiltration rate achieved in empirical studies across representative climate zones and ensures that selected homes do not already comply with 2024 IECC requirements (Phoung, Lou, &amp; White, 2025a).

After air sealing, HVAC ducts in applicable homes are sealed and insulated to achieve:

- A maximum of 5% duct air leakage to the outside
- Duct insulation level of R-8.

Attic floor insulation is then upgraded in applicable dwelling units to the following levels:

- R-49 in climate zone 1
- R-60 in climate zones 2 through 8.

Finally, uninsulated wood stud walls are upgraded with cellulose drill-and-fill insulation to reach an R-13 level.

More details about the modeling assumption for these three measures can be found in the accompanying documentation (Phoung, Lou, &amp; White, 2025a) (Phoung, Lou, &amp; White, 2025b) (Lou &amp; Liu, 2025) (Brossman &amp; Lou, 2025).
