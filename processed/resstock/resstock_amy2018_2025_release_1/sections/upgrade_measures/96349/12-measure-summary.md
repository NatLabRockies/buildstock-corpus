**Measure Summary, part 1 of 2**

| Measure Title | Advanced Air Sealing, Attic Floor Insulation, Duct Sealing, and Drill-and-Fill Wall Insulation |
|---|---|
| Measure Definition | This upgrade measure package applies advanced air sealing, attic floor insulation, duct sealing and insulation, and drill-and-fill wall insulation for applicable dwelling units. A dwelling unit may receive none, some, or all four measures, depending on its housing characteristics. |
| Applicability | This measure applies to all dwellings with at least one of the following housing characteristics: • Infiltration rate of 6 ACH50 or higher • Unfinished attic & less than R-49 attic floor insulation & in climate zone 1 • Unfinished attic & less than R-60 attic floor insulation & in climate zones 2 through 8 • Duct leakage to outside greater than 6% • Duct insulation less than R-6 • Uninsulated above-grade exterior wood stud walls. With these applicability constraints, 98% of existing dwelling units receive at least one of the measures. |

**Measure Summary, part 2 of 2**

| Measure Title | Advanced Air Sealing, Attic Floor Insulation, Duct Sealing, and Drill-and-Fill Wall Insulation |
|---|---|
| Not Applicable | This measure does not apply to dwelling units that have none of the following housing characteristics: • Equal or more than 6 ACH50 infiltration rate • Unfinished attic & less than R-49 attic floor insulation & in climate zone 1 • Unfinished attic & less than R-60 attic floor insulation & in climate zones 2 through 8 • More than 6% duct leakage to outside • Less than R-6 duct insulation • Uninsulated above-grade exterior wood stud walls. These constraints exclude 2% of dwelling units from receiving this measure upgrade. |
