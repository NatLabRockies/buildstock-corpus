## 2 Applicability

To identify which dwelling units qualify for advanced air sealing upgrades, the 2024 IECC air leakage thresholds for new construction (Table 2) are used to exclude units that already meet or exceed current standards. If an existing home's infiltration rate is below the IECC threshold, additional sealing using conventional techniques is unlikely t o yield significant improvements. Empirical results from

Table 3 are provided as case studies by climate zone. For example, data from a California study informs climate zone 3, while a Minnesota study informs zone 6. Because IECC zones reflect shared weather patterns and building practices, results from one state are assumed to represent conditions across the entire zone. Where no study exists for a specific zone, data from a nearby or similar zone are used. For example, Florida (zone 2) data is applied to zones 1 and 2. This zone-binning approach is consistent with IECC 2024's approach to climate zone binning.
