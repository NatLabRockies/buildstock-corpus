## 4.5  Aggregated Building Time Series

Colorado is selected as a case study to analyze the aggregated time-series data. Out of all national dwelling units, 1.7% are located in Colorado. Figure 21 presents the aggregated average daily baseline energy consumption in Colorado, with February 20 identified as the day with the highest energy use. To further explore this peak, Figure 22 examines the aggregated average power on February 20 in Colorado, comparing conditions before and after the advanced air sealing upgrade.

Figure 21. Modeled aggregate daily average site energy consumption of baseline in Colorado

![Image](96738_images/image_000023_87897ce8a8b14208b336198ccfc903c962fb4b00c2655d167b8cba7874c6a5f8.png)

Figure 22 shows that the advanced air sealing upgrade scenario consistently results in lower power demand throughout the day compared to the baseline, particularly during peak heating hours. The peak demand decreases from 12.9 kW per unit to 10.9 kW per unit, representing a 16% reduction.
