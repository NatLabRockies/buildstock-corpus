## 4.4  Stock Utility Bill Impacts

Figure 19 shows a comparison of modeled utility bills with the advanced air sealing upgrade applied. For the applicable units, the average energy bill savings are 279 USD/unit/year (10.7%), with a reduction of 117 USD/unit/year (6.4%) in electricity bills and 100 USD/unit/year (19.3%) in natural gas bills. This is similar to the magnitude in overall stock-level site energy savings.
