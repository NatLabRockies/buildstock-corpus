## Measure Summary

| Measure Title                  | Advanced Air Sealing                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
|--------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Measure Definition             | This measure reduces infiltration rates in existing buildings with baseline infiltration levels of 6 ACH50 (air changes per hour at 50 pascals) or higher.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Applicabili ty                 | Buildings with infiltration rates ≥6 ACH50 are eligible for an upgrade to 5 ACH50. While some regions require tighter envelopes for new constructions, 5 ACH50 reflects the average infiltration rate achieved in empirical studies across representative climate zones and ensures that selected homes do not already comply with 2024 IECC requirements. Table 4 provides additional details. With these applicability constraints, the measure upgrade applies to 92.2% of the existing dwelling units. |
| Not Applicable                 | This measure does not apply to new construction nor to existing units with infiltration rates below 6 ACH50. These constraints exclude 7.8% of the dwelling units from receiving this measure upgrade.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
