## 2. Table 3.

Identify the minimum observed infiltration reduction across studies,

3. Table 3 (found 20%).
4. Apply this minimum improvement to the 5 ACH50 target (from empirical data) to establish a qualifying threshold for existing buildings.

The existing building 's infiltration, when reduced by 20%, should be 5 ACH50. This yields a minimum pre-upgrade value of 6.25 ACH50, but since ResStock models infiltration in whole numbers, 6 ACH50 is used instead. This reflects a 17% reduction. This is still supported by empirical studies that show achievable reductions from 6 to 4.8 ACH50 [13] and from 6.1 to 3.2 ACH50 [14]. Table 4 summarizes the applicable logic for the advanced air sealing used in this upgrade, and Table 5 presents the combined logic. Note that stock saturation is the percentage of dwellings in the building stock that this upgrade would apply to.

Table 4. Summarized Apply Logic for Advanced Air Sealing

| Climate Zone   | Infiltration   | Target Infiltration   |
|----------------|----------------|-----------------------|
| Zones 1 - 2    | ≥ 6 ACH50      | 5.0 ACH50             |
| Zones 3 - 5    | ≥ 6 ACH50      | 5.0 ACH50             |
| Zones 6 - 8    | ≥ 6 ACH50      | 5.0 ACH50             |

Table 5. Combined Apply Logic for Advanced Air Sealing

| Climate Zone   | Infiltration   | Target Infiltration   | Stock Saturation   |
|----------------|----------------|-----------------------|--------------------|
| All            | ≥ 6 ACH50      | 5.0 ACH50             | 92.2%              |
