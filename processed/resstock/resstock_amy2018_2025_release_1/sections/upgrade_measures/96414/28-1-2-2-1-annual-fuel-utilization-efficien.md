## 1.2.2.1  Annual Fuel Utilization Efficiency

AFUE is the total annual fuel-generated heat output divided by the total annual fuel input (on a scale of 0% to 100%) over a 12-month period. ANSI/ASHRAE 103-2022 provides a procedure for determining the AFUE of residential central furnaces and boilers, using a test cycle designed to simulate typical heating conditions throughout the year-including seasons (ASHRAE 2022).

A higher AFUE rating indicates greater efficiency in converting fuel into usable heat. Energy lost through exhaust gases, radiation, and other components reduces the furnace's efficiency.

In this dual fuel heating system measure upgrade, a higher efficiency furnace with 95% AFUE is used in colder climates (IECC climate zones 5, 6, and 7) while a slightly lower efficiency furnace with 92% AFUE is used in warmer climates (IECC climate zones 1, 2, 3, and 4).
