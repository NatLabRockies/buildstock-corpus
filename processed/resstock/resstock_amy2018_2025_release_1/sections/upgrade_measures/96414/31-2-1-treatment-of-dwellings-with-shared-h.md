## 2.1  Treatment of Dwellings With Shared HVAC Systems

Because there are no reported dwellings in the building stock that have only shared cooling and a personal fuel furnace that also have evidence of an outdoor space for a condensing unit (required for the ducted ASHP portion of the dual fuel system), no dwellings with shared systems will be applied with this measure upgrade.
