## 4.1  Single Dwelling Testing

To confirm that the cold climate upgrade measure model performs as expected, two dwelling test models are analyzed in this section. Key characteristics are provided in Table 6. Both dwellings are single-family detached households-the most common building type under this upgrade's applicability logic. Both have natural gas furnaces and central AC units. Dwelling 1 is in the colder climate zone of 6A while Dwelling 2 is in the hotter climate of 2A.

Table 6. Characteristics of Two Individual Dwelling Test Models

| Dwelling ID           | 1 (Receives 92.5% Dual Fuel HP)   | 2 (Receives 95% Dual Fuel HP)   |
|-----------------------|-----------------------------------|---------------------------------|
| Building Type         | Single-Family Detached            | Single-Family Detached          |
| IECC Climate Zone     | 6A                                | 2A                              |
| State                 | WI                                | TX                              |
| Baseline Heating Type | Natural Gas Furnace (80% AFUE)    | Natural Gas Furnace (80% AFUE)  |
| Baseline Cooling Type | Central AC, SEER2 12.35           | Central AC, SEER2 14.25         |
