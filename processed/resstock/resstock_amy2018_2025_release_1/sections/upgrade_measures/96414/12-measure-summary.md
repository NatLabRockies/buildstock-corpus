## Measure Summary

| Measure Title                 | Dual Fuel Heating System Measure Upgrade                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
|-------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Measure Definition            | This upgrade measure replaces any existing heating and air conditioning system with a dual fuel heating system that includes an air-source heat pump and a natural gas furnace. If the unit does not have air conditioning, it will receive air conditioning through the air-source heat pump. The technology applied in this measure upgrade package is denoted in ResStock as follows: • Dual Fuel Heating System, 92% Annual Fuel Utilization Efficiency (AFUE), Climate Zones (CZ) 1-4 • Dual Fuel Heating System, 95% AFUE, CZ 5-8. |
| Applicability                 | This measure applies to all dwellings with: • Ducted non-shared (in-unit) HVAC with any of the following heating fuels: electricity, natural gas, fuel oil, or propane • Have at least one appliance or an HVAC system fueled by natural gas. With these applicability constraints, the measure upgrade applies to 44.7% of the existing dwelling units.                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Not Applicable                | This measure does not apply to dwelling units with the following: • Shared HVAC • Non-ducted non-shared HVAC • Heating Fuel of None, Other Fuel, or Wood. These constraints exclude 44.3% of the dwelling units from receiving this measure upgrade.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
