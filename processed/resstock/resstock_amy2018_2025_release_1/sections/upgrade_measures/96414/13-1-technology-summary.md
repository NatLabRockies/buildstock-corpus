## 1  Technology Summary

The dual fuel heating system in this measure upgrade consists of an air-source heat pump (ASHP) and a natural gas furnace. In this configuration the natural gas furnace heats the dwelling during the coldest outdoor temperatures of the year, while the ASHP cools the dwelling during the cooling season and heats it at mild outdoor temperatures during the heating season. This section provides an overview of the technology used in this measure upgrade and includes details about specific features of the dual fuel systems.
