## 4.3  Stock Energy Utility Bill Impacts

Figure 9 shows the average energy utility bill costs per dwelling for households that received the dual fuel heating system upgrade (labeled as 'Upgrade 2' in the figure). On average, utility bill costs are slightly lower with the upgrade, with higher electricity utility bills but lower natural gas and other fossil fuel utility bills. Generally, natural gas is cheaper per unit energy than electricity; therefore, the dual fuel heating system results in lower utility bills than the cold climate ASHP, which is also modeled in ResStock 2025 Release 1.
