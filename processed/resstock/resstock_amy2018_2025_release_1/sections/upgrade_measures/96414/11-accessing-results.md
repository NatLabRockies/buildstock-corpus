## Accessing Results

This documentation covers the 'Dual Fuel Heating System' measure upgrade methodology and briefly discusses key results. All results can be accessed on the ResStock™ Open Energy Data Initiative End-Use Load Profiles for the U.S. Building Stock Data Lake and on the data viewer at resstock.nlr.gov/datasets.
