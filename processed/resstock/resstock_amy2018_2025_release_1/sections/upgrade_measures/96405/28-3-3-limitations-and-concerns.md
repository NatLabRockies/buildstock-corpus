## 3.3  Limitations and Concerns

There are two key limitations and concerns for this modeling. First, because the requirements for certification allow a range of performance characteristics, this modeling approach inherently cannot reflect the performance of all possible certified products. As described in the results, the choices of performance characteristics even show negative savings for a tiny segment of the stock. In reality, it is likely possible to identify certified products that achieve positive savings for this segment. It is also possible to select certified products that achieve better or worse performance compared to what was modeled.

The second concern is the use of the simplified window glazing model in OS-HPXML. A detailed investigation of the tradeoffs in that modeling simplification was beyond the scope of this effort because changing OS-HPXML was also beyond the scope. However, this simplification may have ramifications on the results.
