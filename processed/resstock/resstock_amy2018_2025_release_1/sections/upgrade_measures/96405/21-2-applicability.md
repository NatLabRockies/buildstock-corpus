## 2  Applicability

This upgrade is applicable to all dwelling units where the baseline window performance characteristics are lower than the ENERGY STAR V7 characteristics shown in Table 4. None of the baseline windows meet these requirements, therefore the upgrade is applicable to 100% of dwelling units.
