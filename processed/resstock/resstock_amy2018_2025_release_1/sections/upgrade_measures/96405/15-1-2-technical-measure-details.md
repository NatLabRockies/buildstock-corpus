## 1.2  Technical Measure Details

The appropriate choice of windows for a building depends on many factors. Some of the major energy-related factors include the climate, the orientation of the windows relative to the sun, and the architectural or landscape features surrounding the window. Non-energy-related factors such as material durability, aesthetics, and physical operation (opening and closing) are also important to consider.
