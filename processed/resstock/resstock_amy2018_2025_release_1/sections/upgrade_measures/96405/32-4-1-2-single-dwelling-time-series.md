## 4.1.2 Single Dwelling Time Series

The time series of the individual dwellings shows how the upgrade impacts the load shape. Figure 3 and Figure 4 respectively show the impacts of the upgrade on heating energy in January and cooling energy in July for Dwelling 2. As expected, heating energy is reduced most significantly during the cold nighttime hours, and cooling energy is reduced most significantly during the hot afternoon hours.

Figure 5 confirms that the observed infiltration rate is reduced by roughly the 30% input into the model. Because of the formulation of the infiltration model, the output is not exactly a one-toone match with the input single value number.
