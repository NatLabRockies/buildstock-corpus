## 4.3  Stock Utility Bill Impacts

Figure 12 shows a comparison of modeled utility bills with this measure applied. Stock-wide total bill savings are 7%, with a reduction of 5% in electricity bills and 7% in natural gas bills. This is similar to the magnitude of the overall stock level site energy savings. Propane and fuel oil bills also decrease, but these make up a small fraction of the total building stock utility bills.

Figure 12. Comparison of projected annual utility bills between the ResStock baseline and the ENERGY STAR V7 Windows upgrade scenario. Utility bills are categorized by fuel type.
