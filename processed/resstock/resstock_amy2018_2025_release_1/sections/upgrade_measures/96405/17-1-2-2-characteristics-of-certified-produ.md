## 1.2.2 Characteristics of Certified Products

As shown in Table 1, the certification requirements establish a range of performance values rather than a singular value for each climate zone. For this report, NLR performed an assessment to understand the range of performance characteristics available in the market as of the time of writing.

The EPA Product Finder is a database of certified products. As of the date of publication, there were more than 1,000 certified window product lines, with hundreds or thousands of individual products within each line (U.S. Environmental Protection Agency, n.d.). Because of the limitations of the Product Finder, it was not possible to extract detailed information for every product in every line. Instead, a sample of products was reviewed. According to the websites of several of the largest window manufacturers in the U.S., double hung windows are the most common type of windows in the residential market. NLR's review of four lines of double hung windows covering 50,000 individual window configurations from four of the ten largest U.S. window manufacturers was performed using data available through the NFRC Directory Search linked from the EPA Product Finder (National Fenestration Rating Council, n.d.).

As shown in Table 2, the performance characteristics of just a fraction of all available products show that their performance characteristics span the range of values that allow a product to achieve certification, rather than all products being at one extreme of the performance band.
