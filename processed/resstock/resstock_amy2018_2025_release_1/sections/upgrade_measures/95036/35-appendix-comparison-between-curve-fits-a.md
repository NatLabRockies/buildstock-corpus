Figure A-1: three stacked colour-scaled data grids of full-load heating EIR, titled Manufacturer Data, Curvefit Data and Difference. Rows are air temperature (50 to 80 °F, 10.00 to 26.67 °C) and columns are water temperature (30 to 90 °F, -1.11 to 32.22 °C). EIR values run from about 0.13 to 0.31 in both grids; the differences lie between -0.003 and 0.016, largest in the 4.44 °C water column.
