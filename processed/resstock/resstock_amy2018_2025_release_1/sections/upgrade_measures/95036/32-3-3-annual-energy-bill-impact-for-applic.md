## 3.3  Annual Energy Bill Impact for Applicable Dwelling Units Only

Energy bills for applicable dwelling units only are shown in the following figures. On average, energy bills decreased by 24% for the variable-speed GHP. This is a greater percent savings than the single-stage GHP, at 3% savings, and it is double the two-stage GHP, which showed an 11% savings. The average energy bill for the variable-speed GHP is shown in Figure 32. All fuel costs decreased on average.

Figure 32. Average annual energy bill categorized by fuel type for dwelling units that received the variable-speed GHP
