## 3.3  Annual Energy Bill Impact for Applicable Dwelling Units Only

Energy bills for applicable dwelling units only are shown in the following figures. On average, energy bills decreased by 24% for the variable-speed GHP. This is a greater percent savings than the single-stage GHP, at 3% savings, and it is double the two-stage GHP, which showed an 11% savings. The average energy bill for the variable-speed GHP is shown in Figure 32. All fuel costs decreased on average.

Figure 32. Average annual energy bill categorized by fuel type for dwelling units that received the variable-speed GHP

![Image](95036_images/image_000028_3584d0502c5a83331f10e25b73191c5fdbcb53d72828b77b1e948da7905ad067.png)

Figure 33 shows that the variable-speed GHP average energy bill is 22% less than the singlestage GHP energy bill and 11% less than the two-stage GHP energy bill.

Figure 33. Average annual energy bill comparison between the single-stage, two-stage, and variable-speed GHP categorized by fuel type for applicable dwelling units only

![Image](95036_images/image_000029_6b44ca98ad4ea94ac5a558b850ea5d6ba1e51a7f87e00d8552a086aad8a1e243.png)

For the variable-speed GHP, Figure 34 shows that the average energy bill decreased across all Building America climate zones.
