## Accessing Results

This documentation covers the ' Residential Geothermal Variable-Speed Heat Pump (4.4 Coefficient of Performance [COP], 30.9 Energy Efficiency Ratio [EER]) ' measure upgrade methodology and briefly discusses key results. All results can be accessed on the ResStock ™ Open Energy Data Initiative ' End-Use Load Profiles for the U.S. Building Stock ' data lake and on the Data Viewer at https://resstock.nlr.gov/.
