## 2.2.1.2  Summary of Different Inputs Than the Single-Stage and Two-Stage GHP Measures

Although some key inputs and their values are discussed in the preceding section, Table 3 summarizes the inputs for the variable-speed GHP measure in ResStock and OpenStudio-HPXML.

Table 3. Summary of the Different Variable-Speed GHP Measure Inputs From the Single- and Two-Stage GHP Measures

| Input                             | Value          | Source                               |
|-----------------------------------|----------------|--------------------------------------|
| Heat pump cooling compressor type | Variable speed | Measure definition                   |
| Heat pump cooling efficiency type | 30.9           | ENERGY STAR Most Efficient 2025 list |
| Heat pump heating efficiency      | 4.4            | ENERGY STAR Most Efficient 2025 list |

6  The rated heating COP is defined by the conditions in ARI Standard 325. The entering water temperature: 32°F; entering air: 68°F dry bulb, and 59°F wet bulb.

7  The rated cooling EER is defined by the conditions in ARI Standard 325. The entering water temperature: 77°F; entering air: 80.6°F dry bulb, and 66.2°F wet bulb.
