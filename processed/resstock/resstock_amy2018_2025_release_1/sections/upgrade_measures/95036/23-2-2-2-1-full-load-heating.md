Figure 1: colour-scaled data grid (heat map) of full-load heating energy input ratio. Rows are air temperature from 10 to 27.5 °C and columns are water temperature from 0 to 30 °C, both in 2.5 °C steps; each cell holds an EIR. EIR rises with air temperature and falls with water temperature, from 0.131 (10 °C air, 30 °C water) to 0.312 (27.5 °C air, 0 °C water).
