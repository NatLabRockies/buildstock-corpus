## Measure Summary

| Measure Title                 | Residential Variable-Speed Geothermal Heat Pump (4.4 COP, 30.9 EER)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
|-------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Measure Definition            | This upgrade measure replaces the heating, ventilation, and air-conditioning (HVAC) system in any existing dwelling unit in a building that is less than eight stories with ducted heating or cooling to a variable-speed, water-to-air, vertical ground heat exchanger GHP with a variable-speed fan.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Applicability                 | This measure applies to all dwellings with: • Non-shared (in-unit) HVAC and any of the following heating fuels: electricity, natural gas, fuel oil, or propane • Shared (across multiple units) HVAC and a building height of less than eight stories and any of the following heating fuels: electricity, natural gas, fuel oil, or propane • Dwelling units with air ducts • Dwelling units with soil thermal conductivity greater than 0.8 Btu/hr-ft-°F. With these applicability constraints, the measure upgrade applies to 71% of the existing residential building stock. The 6.4% of the residential units that receive the GHP upgrade lack existing space-cooling systems in the baseline. |
| Not Applicable                | This measure does not apply to dwelling units with: • Shared HVAC (across multiple units) in high-rise (greater than eight floors) buildings (e.g., multifamily residential buildings) • Dwelling units without air ducts. These constraints exclude 18% of the dwelling units from receiving this measure upgrade. An additional 4% of the dwelling units do not receive this upgrade because of their heating fuel type.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
