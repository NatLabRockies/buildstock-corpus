## 4.1  Single Model Results

This section analyzes three example dwellings that received upgrades. The upgrades had the following building characteristics shown in Table 9.

Table 9. Single Model Types

| Building Geometry         | IECC Climate Zone, 2004   | State   | Heating Fuel   | HVAC Heating Efficiency   | HVAC Cooling Efficiency   |
|---------------------------|---------------------------|---------|----------------|---------------------------|---------------------------|
| Single-Family Detached    | 5A                        | MA      | Fuel Oil       | Fuel Boiler, 76% AFUE     | AC, SEER 15               |
| Multifamily With 5+ Units | 3A                        | GA      | Electricity    | ASHP, SEER 13, 7.7 HSPF   | ASHP, SEER 13, 7.7 HSPF   |
| Single-Family Detached    | 4A                        | IL      | Natural Gas    | Fuel Furnace, 60% AFUE    | AC, SEER 10               |

This section focuses on the percentage energy reduction achieved by the upgrade. The annual energy consumption of the dwellings before and after the upgrades are shown below.

Figure 1. Annual energy consumption for an example unit in Massachusetts before and after the upgrade

![Image](96636_images/image_000002_1428a0e11423f2857129471b60674e8342823d610bc684f3a04854fbc6ad1c63.png)

In Figure 1, the fuel boiler is upgraded to the existing standard, showing annual energy savings of 6.5 MBtu, with these energy savings sourced from the fuel oil heating end use. The other end uses remain unchanged from the upgrade.

Figure 2. Annual energy consumption for an example unit in Georgia before and after the upgrade

![Image](96636_images/image_000003_4add8fa61c2d014bbf2a03548724fbd7000e9f0c34b3779d7e97680aec6e23f9.png)

Like the prior example, Figure 2 shows slight energy savings in both heating and cooling because of energy efficiency improvements in the air-source heat pump.

Figure 3. Annual energy consumption for an example unit in Illinois before and after the upgrade

![Image](96636_images/image_000004_50bb88ef8aee8346705921c36632747ab0b229391595017988c68a510096700b.png)

After the upgrade, the example dwelling in Illinois changes from 122.8 MBtu in the baseline to 98.9 MBtu, where the upgrade reduced the heating loads with a more efficient furnace.
