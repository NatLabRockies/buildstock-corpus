## 4.3  Stock Energy Impacts

Out of 550,000 model building simulation (i.e., models), 413,611 models are applicable for the reference space heating and air conditioning upgrade (75.2%). For the 413,611 models, the total baseline consumption is 2.87 trillion kWh annually, and after the upgrade is 2.80 trillion kWh, a 2% savings. Figure 6 shows the total site energy consumption between the baseline and upgrade.

Figure 6. Annual total site energy consumption for housing stock

![Image](96636_images/image_000007_364a491fc6506078058f88e1f374db2b5c739884bfc74818a0914c13e178f38e.png)

Figure 7. Annual energy consumption by HVAC heating type and fuel end use

![Image](96636_images/image_000008_c04c8c01ea88059b7267c2b82a1fdca1d3b42cde14a169f2080af53a3d168ada.png)

Cooling from electricity in ducted heating systems accounts for 42,000 GWh of energy reduction, the greatest energy reduction after the upgrade; in comparison, heating from natural gas in ducted heating systems was reduced by 6,000 GWh. Cooling fans and pumps (i.e., air handler fan energy) also yielded 6,400 GWh in energy savings, whereas heating fans and pumps yielded 1,300 GWh in energy savings. Energy savings from fans and pumps are primarily a result of more advanced motors and controls.

Given that this is a reference case and relatively few HVAC systems are below minimum efficiency, only modest energy savings are expected: 4% for electricity-heated homes, 2% for natural gas, 3% for fuel oil, and 1% for propane.

Figure 8 shows average dwelling annual energy consumption by HVAC cooling efficiency, fuels, and end uses.

Figure 8. Average annual energy consumption per dwelling by HVAC cooling efficiency

![Image](96636_images/image_000009_709b301bf1a003d6cfeb91b8bba67e32331dc5a16fc3e7c04c86db8566c5ec6e.png)

On average, dwellings upgraded from an ' AC, SEER 8 ' cooling system to the minimum efficiency resulted in 2,900 kWh in energy savings, the largest average savings when segmented by HVAC cooling efficiency. In contrast, ' AC, SEER 15 ' shows 107 kWh in average perdwelling annual energy savings -all sourced from heating end uses and indicating efficiency gains in heating systems rather than cooling systems. Please note that the central AC cooling efficiencies are measured in SEER1 as the baseline condition. Conversion to SEER2 in the baseline condition may be a feature in future versions of ResStock outputs.

Figure 9 shows average annual energy consumption per dwelling by HVAC heating efficiency.

Figure 9. Average annual energy consumption per dwelling by HVAC heating efficiency

![Image](96636_images/image_000010_00f3f6dae6a0dbadd20234f11d600a8d164b1191dddf2f37f03f0ee11ded316c.png)

Similar to the baseline AC systems being measured in SEER1, note that the central heating efficiencies are measured in HSPF1 as the baseline condition. Conversion to HSPF2 in the baseline condition may be a feature in future versions of ResStock outputs. Reexamining the distribution of energy consumption by heating fuel provides a clearer view of reduction across the housing stock for the upgrade, shown in Figure 10.

Figure 10. Annual site energy consumption savings per dwelling by heating fuel

![Image](96636_images/image_000011_1877ce4b1fe9b1ac5073829ead0c647abf37728d59159910fd36d126c08bd810.png)

Figure 10 shows the distribution of annual energy consumption savings by heating fuel, where fuel oil heated dwellings average 1,134 kWh in savings, and electricity-heated dwellings average 790 kWh in savings.

Upgrading dwellings from a lower to a higher efficiency air conditioning system can be effective for reducing energy consumption, as shown in Figure 11. For example, ' AC, SEER 8 ' and ' AC, SEER 10 ' can result in average annual savings of 2,892 kWh and 1,749 kWh, respectively.

Figure 11. Average annual energy savings by HVAC cooling efficiency per year

![Image](96636_images/image_000012_03a3edc1fb69a3d65d536955e3d34340366b27b13ec615865afefb8b5ac959f6.png)

Because this is a reference case and only a fraction of HVAC systems have below minimum efficiency ratings, only modest energy savings are expected.
