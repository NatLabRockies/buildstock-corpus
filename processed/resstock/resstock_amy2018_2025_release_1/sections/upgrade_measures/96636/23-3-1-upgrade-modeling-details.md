## 3.1  Upgrade Modeling Details

In ResStock, building characteristics are translated to OpenStudio-HPXML parameters. Measure upgrades, such as installing natural gas furnaces and air conditioning, are modeled as changes to baseline building characteristics. This section outlines the technical details of how the federal minimum heating and cooling upgrade is implemented in the ResStock workflow.
