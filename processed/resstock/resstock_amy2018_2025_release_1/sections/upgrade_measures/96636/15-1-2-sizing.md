## 1.2  Sizing

HVAC sizing is automatic across all HVAC system types and sizes following the ACCA Manual J/S methodology. ACCA Manual J (ACCA, 2024) provides guidelines for calculating the design heating and cooling loads in a building, and ACCA Manual S (ACCA, 2014) provides a guide for selecting the size of residential HVAC systems to meet those loads. For a full description of sizing and system selection for ASHPs, reference the Dual Fuel Heat Pump System documentation (White et al. 2025).
