## 1 Technology Summary

This work evaluates a reference case where existing space heating and air conditioning equipment in homes with existing ductwork is upgraded to the minimum efficiency available (circa 2025) ⎯ or like-for-like replacements for above-minimum equipment -across the U.S. residential building stock. The purpose of this reference case is to provide a comparison point for other measures and packages that simulate heating and cooling equipment replacements at the end of the equipment 's useful life (also called 'at wear out'). This enables estimates of incremental energy savings and incremental upfront cost of measures compared to an assumed alternative.
