## Upgrade Summary

| Measure Title      | Reference Space Heating and Air Conditioning Upgrade Circa 2025                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
|--------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Measure Definition | This upgrade replaces space heating and cooling equipment in homes with existing ductwork with units that meet minimum efficiency standards reflective of circa-2025 requirements. It includes: • Replacement of ducted fuel furnaces with a minimum of 80% - 83% AFUE (92.5% AFUE for like-for-like replacements at higher efficiency). • Replacement of air-source heat pumps with equipment rated at 14.3 SEER2 and 7.5 HSPF2. • Replacement of central air conditioners (ACs) with units rated at 14.3 SEER2 in the South region and 13.4 SEER2 in the North region, with higher efficiency replacements (e.g., SEER2 15) applied where applicable. • Replacement of ducted electric resistance furnaces with baseline 100% efficiency electric options for costing consistency. In homes that have existing ductwork that is used for air conditioning but not heating, the non-ducted heating systems are also replaced: • Replacement of fuel boilers with a minimum of 80% - 83% AFUE (90% AFUE for like-for-like replacements at higher efficiency). • Replacement of non-ducted electric resistance heating systems (boiler, baseboard, wall furnace) with baseline 100% efficiency electric options for costing consistency. In homes that have existing ductwork that is used for heating but not central air conditioning, the non-ducted air conditioning systems are also replaced: • Replacement of room ACs with models meeting an EER of 12.0. |
| Applicability      | This upgrade applies to all dwelling units that have existing ductwork and a heating fuel that is natural gas, electricity, fuel oil, or propane. For consistency with other upgrades, it excludes any homes that do not have existing ductwork, do not use heating, or that heat with wood. It applies to about 5% of homes that have non-ducted heating (electric resistance baseboard, boilers, or wall/floor furnaces) but have ducts used for air conditioning. This includes a small number (<1%) of multifamily dwelling units that have a whole-building shared heating system but that have individual ducted air conditioning systems serving each unit. It covers a wide range of heating technologies (furnaces, boilers, electric resistance) and cooling technologies (central AC, heat pumps, room ACs). With these applicability constraints, the measure upgrade applies to 75.2% of the existing dwelling units.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |

| Not Applicable             | This upgrade does not apply to: • Homes that do not have existing ductwork, including homes heated with electric resistance baseboard, boilers/radiators, ductless heat pumps, and wall/floor furnaces that do not have ducted air conditioning. • Heating systems sourced from wood and homes without heating (i.e., 'n one ' ). • Systems already meeting or exceeding the specified minimum efficiency thresholds (modeled as like-for-like replacements). These constraints exclude 24.8% of the dwelling units from receiving this measure upgrade. |
