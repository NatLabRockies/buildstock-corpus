## 4.4  Stock Utility Bill Impacts

After the upgrade, the housing stock shows a reduction in energy bill costs, as shown in Figure 10, where dwellings heated with fuel oil show a 4.7% average percentage savings and dwellings heated with propane show a 7.4% average percentage savings.

Figure 10. Annual energy bills by heating fuel in baseline and upgrade

Figure 11 shows the average annual bill savings by HVAC heating efficiency and the energy source of the bill.

Figure 11. Average annual bill savings by HVAC heating efficiency

![Image](96635_images/image_000012_0fa7f9e75eda956a4a11774c15d688f309d989b45bb5ce4522413917e7419103.png)

Aligning with increasing energy performance shown in the previous section, lower efficiency furnaces show the greatest average annual bill savings after the upgrade. Dwellings receiving the upgrade and heated with fuel oil yield an average annual bill savings of $248, and dwellings heated with propane yield $293. Improving furnaces ' efficiency saves on both energy and bills.
