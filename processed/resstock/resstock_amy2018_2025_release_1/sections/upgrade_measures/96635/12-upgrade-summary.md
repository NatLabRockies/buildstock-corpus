**Upgrade Summary, part 1 of 2**

| Measure Title | Propane Furnace 95% AFUE and Fuel Oil Furnace 88% AFUE |
|---|---|
| Measure Definition | Upgrades existing ducted fuel furnaces to high-efficiency units while retaining the current fuel: • If the dwelling heats with propane , replace with 95% AFUE fuel furnace • If the dwelling heats with fuel oil , replace with 88% AFUE fuel furnace. |
| Applicability | Applies to dwellings that: • Have ducts, and • Do not use wood, 'other fuel,' or have no primary heating fuel, and • Are not serviced by disqualifying shared central systems, and • For propane upgrade: Currently have a fuel furnace with efficiency less than 95% AFUE • For fuel oil upgrade: Currently have a fuel furnace with efficiency less than 88% AFUE. With these applicability constraints, the measure upgrade applies to 5.8% of the existing dwelling units. |
| Not Applicable | This upgrade does not apply to: • Dwellings without ducts • Dwellings with primary heating electricity, natural gas, fuel wood, other fuel, or none • Dwellings served by shared central systems • Dwelling whose existing fuel furnace efficiency falls outside the sets noted in 'Applicability' . These constraints exclude 94.4% of the dwelling units from receiving this measure upgrade. |
