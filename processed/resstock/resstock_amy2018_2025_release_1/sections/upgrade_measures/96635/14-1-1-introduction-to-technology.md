## 1.1  Introduction to Technology

Fuel-fired furnaces -including propane and fuel oil -are used to heat indoor environments. Fuel ignites in the burner, and the flame heats a metal heat exchanger. A blower fan moves air over the heat exchangers, which warms the air. Most furnaces use ducts to distribute heat, while combustion exhaust is vented outside. In comparison, gravity furnaces do exist but are older and not as common; as such in this study, we only model forced air furnaces.
