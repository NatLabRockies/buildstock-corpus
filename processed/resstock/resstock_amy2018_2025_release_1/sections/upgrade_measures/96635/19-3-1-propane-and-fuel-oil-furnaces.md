## 3.1  Propane and Fuel Oil Furnaces

In ResStock, building characteristics are translated to OpenStudio-HPXML parameters. Measure upgrades, such as converting or replacing furnaces with higher-efficiency propane or fuel oil systems, are modeled as changes to baseline building characteristics. This section outlines the technical details of how propane and fuel oil furnace upgrades are implemented in the ResStock workflow.
