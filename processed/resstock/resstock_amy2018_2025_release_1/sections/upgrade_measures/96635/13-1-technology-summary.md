## 1 Technology Summary

This work examines the technical potential for replacing existing fuel oil and propane furnaces across the U.S. residential building stock -but only in homes where these types of systems are already installed. Though less widespread than natural gas systems, oil and propane furnaces remain common in areas without pipeline gas service, particularly in older and colder climate dwellings. Oil is most common in older or rural buildings in New England and the mid-Atlantic.
