## 4.1  Single Model Results

This section analyzes two example dwellings that received upgrades, both of which are modeled with an 80% AFUE fuel furnace in the baseline. Figure 1 shows the annual energy consumption before and after a fuel oil furnace upgrade in a dwelling in Alaska, and Figure 2 shows the annual energy consumption before and after propane furnace upgrade in a dwelling in Montana.

Figure 1. Annual energy consumption for an example dwelling in Alaska before and after the upgrade

![Image](96635_images/image_000002_d47bd62a76126eaf9a628ae98556e9c974d40ee7b1a94516094c99573b2e0539.png)

Figure 2. Annual energy consumption for an example dwelling in Montana before and after the upgrade

![Image](96635_images/image_000003_cc847472d85ab4147944b439f571a83215cf0d479fcb161281da3c72a3a3bc8b.png)

Figure 1 demonstrates energy savings are sourced from fuel oil heating end uses, where this example dwelling resulted in 7.4 MBtu of energy savings. Figure 2 also demonstrates 27 MBtu total annual energy savings, primarily sourced from propane heating end uses and electricity consumed from heating fans and pumps.
