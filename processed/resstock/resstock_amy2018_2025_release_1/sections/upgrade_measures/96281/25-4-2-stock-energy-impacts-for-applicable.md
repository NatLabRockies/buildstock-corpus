## 4.2  Stock Energy Impacts for Applicable Dwelling Units Only

This section presents results for the full ResStock 2025 Release 1, which includes 550,000 modeled samples. Advanced air sealing with drill-and-fill wall insulation was applicable to 92% of dwelling units. In theory, advanced air-sealing was applicable to 92.2% of baseline dwelling units, drill-and-fill wall insulation to 31% of baseline dwelling units, and about 30% of units could receive both measures as shown in Table 3.
