## 4.1  Individual Model Results

This section analyzes three dwelling unit models that received both advanced air sealing and drill-and-fill wall insulation. Information about each of the three models is shown in Table 6:

Table 6. Baseline Individual Model Characteristics

|                                              | Colorado model            | North Carolina model      | Arizona model             |
|----------------------------------------------|---------------------------|---------------------------|---------------------------|
| Location                                     | Denver County, CO         | Lincoln County, NC        | Maricopa County, AZ       |
| Building America Climate Zone Classification | Cold                      | Mixed-humid               | Hot-dry                   |
| Building Type                                | Single-family attached    | Single-family detached    | Single-family detached    |
| Heating System and Fuel                      | Natural gas furnace       | Ducted heat pump          | Electric furnace          |
| Air Conditioning System                      | Central air conditioning  | Ducted heat pump          | Central air conditioning  |
| Starting Infiltration Rate                   | 15 ACH50                  | 50 ACH50                  | 15 ACH50                  |
| Wall Type and Insulation Level               | Wood stud and uninsulated | Wood stud and uninsulated | Wood stud and uninsulated |

These models differ by location, building system, heating and air conditioning systems, and initial infiltration rates. Additionally, these modeled dwelling units vary in square footage, heating and cooling thermostat setpoints, and other factors. None of these dwelling units had backup HVAC systems. Due to all these model differences, their simulated results will vary.

The individual model results are discussed below. Using fewer samples leads to greater uncertainty. One model is not sufficient to make assumptions or generalizations. The single model results shown here are demonstrations to confirm that the package is being applied correctly and that individual model results align with expectations. More information about the recommended number of samples for analysis, and how to calculate uncertainty for any sample size, is available on the ResStock user resource website. 7
