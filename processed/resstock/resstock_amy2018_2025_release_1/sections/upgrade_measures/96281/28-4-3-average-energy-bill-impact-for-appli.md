## 4.3  Average Energy Bill Impact for Applicable Dwelling Units Only

Energy bills for applicable units generally decreased after advanced air sealing with drill-and-fill wall insulation, as shown in Figure 13. Applicable dwelling units saw an average decrease in energy bills of 15%. The largest reduction in energy bills was for fuel oil, with an average savings of 38%, followed by natural gas at 26%, propane at 30%, and electricity at 9%.
