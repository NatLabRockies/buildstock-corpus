## 4.2.2  Aggregated Power Time Series in Select States During the Peak Day

Colorado was analyzed for the time-series results. Of the 550,000 dwelling units in the national run, 549,971 of them completed simulation. 9,374 of the completed dwelling units in the national run were in Colorado. The power time-series for Colorado is shown in Figure 11. February 20 is the peak power day.

Figure 11. Baseline site daily aggregated average energy consumption time series for Colorado

![Image](96281_images/image_000012_c85e04ec85ddd60ac0aa0d6e751d57ed63cdbca0fc428d77d6bdfbadc8fa53e3.png)

The peak day, February 20, is shown in more detail in Figure 12, with 15-minute power data.

Figure 12. Time series for peak day in Colorado before and after upgrade

![Image](96281_images/image_000013_4ab1b7f951180dd941402c341fa761194a5236b369c5b9c94ac2175fcdb36ed1.png)

The baseline peak power was 12.9 kW per dwelling unit. After advanced air sealing with drilland-fill wall insulation was applied to the dwelling units, the peak power decreased to 10.1 kW per dwelling unit, representing a 15.5% reduction. Notable savings occurred throughout the entire day, including early morning and late at night. The peaks and valleys generally maintained the same shape.
