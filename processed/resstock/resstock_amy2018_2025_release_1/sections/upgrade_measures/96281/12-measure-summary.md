## Measure Summary

| Measure Title                 | Advanced Air Sealing with Drill-and-Fill Wall Insulation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
|-------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Measure Definition            | This upgrade measure applies R-13 drill-and-fill insulation to exterior wood-stud walls and is modeled with advanced air sealing. Advanced air sealing details are available in the measure documentation (Phoung, Lou and White 2025).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Applicability                 | The insulation portion of this measure applies to all dwellings with: • Uninsulated above-grade wood stud walls separating conditioned from unconditioned space. With these applicability constraints, this portion of the measure upgrade applies to about 30% of existing dwelling units. The advanced air sealing portion of the measure applies to dwelling units with infiltration rates of ≥6 ACH50. With these applicability constraints, this portion of the measure applies to about 92% of existing dwelling units. In total, portions or the full package apply to 92% of dwelling units. |
| Not Applicable                | The insulation portion of this measure does not apply to dwelling units with: • Wood stud walls with existing insulation (any amount) • Non-wood stud walls. These constraints exclude about 70% of dwelling units from receiving wall insulation upgrades. The advanced air sealing portion of this measure does not apply to dwelling units with infiltration rates below 6 ACH50. This excludes about 8% of all dwelling units.                                                                                                                                                                   |
