## 4.1  Single Building Example

This section examines a modeled dwelling unit in Colorado (climate zone 5B) that underwent the envelope package upgrade, which includes light air sealing, attic floor insulation, and duct sealing and insulation. The dwelling unit is in Denver County, and it has a Single-Family Attached geometry. It has a central air conditioning system that is SEER 13 and an 80% AFUE natural gas furnace. Table 3 presents the housing characteristics of the unit before and after the upgrade.

Table 3. Housing Characteristics of the Example Dwelling Unit Before and After the Envelope Package Upgrades (Light Air Sealing + Attic Insulation + Duct Sealing and Insulation)

| Housing Characteristics     | Before Envelope Package Upgrades    | After Envelope Package Upgrades   |
|-----------------------------|-------------------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| Infiltration                | 30 ACH50                            | 22.5 ACH50                        |
| Attic Insulation            | R-30                                | R-60                              |
| Duct Leakage and Insulation | 30% Leakage to Outside, Uninsulated | 5% Leakage to Outside, R-8        |
