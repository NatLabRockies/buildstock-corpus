## 4.1.1 Individual Building Annual Results

Table 4 shows the modeled annual energy consumption of the example dwelling unit before and after the upgrade.

Table 4. Individual Dwelling Unit Modeled Annual Results

|                                    |   Before |   After | Percent Decrease   |
|------------------------------------|----------|---------|--------------------|
| Site Energy for All Fuels [kWh]    |   34,457 |  24,165 | 29.9%              |
| Cooling Energy Consumption [kWh]   |    1,393 |   1,025 | 26.4%              |
| Heating Energy Consumption [kWh]   |   21,909 |  12,302 | 43.8%              |
| Cooling Hourly Peak Power [kBtu/h] |     21.0 |    20.7 | 1.4%               |
| Heating Hourly Peak Power [kBtu/h] |     59.5 |    34.4 | 42.2%              |

The dwelling unit shows a 29.9% annual site energy decrease, driven by the natural gas heating energy decrease of 43.8%. Cooling energy consumption decreases moderately as well at 26.4%. In terms of peak power impacts, the heating peak power decreases by 42.2% and the cooling peak power decreases by 1.4%.
