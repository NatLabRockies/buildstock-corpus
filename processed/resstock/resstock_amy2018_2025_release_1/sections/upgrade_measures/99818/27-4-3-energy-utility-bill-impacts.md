## 4.3  Energy Utility Bill Impacts

This section shows modeled energy utility bill impacts as a result of this upgrade. Figure 9 shows a comparison of energy bill savings broken out by fuel for applicable dwelling units. Overall, most bill savings occur in electricity, with a total of $19.2B saved across the residential stock. Stock natural gas bill savings are $10.7B, while stock fuel oil and propane bill savings are dwelling units $3.0B and $2.6B, respectively.

Figure 9. Residential building stock modeled energy bill savings by fuel type

![Image](99818_images/image_000010_2ec6ff900dc5ed99d7712da4713df36c8a8fecb7d38d289c335007fb77bcdfcd.png)

Figure 10 shows the average annual energy bill percent savings broken out by fuel type. Average total bill savings are 10%, with an 8% reduction in electricity bills and a 15% reduction in natural gas bills. Fuel oil bills and propane bills see the largest percent reductions at 19% and 18%, respectively, however bills from these fuels make up a small portion of the total stock.
