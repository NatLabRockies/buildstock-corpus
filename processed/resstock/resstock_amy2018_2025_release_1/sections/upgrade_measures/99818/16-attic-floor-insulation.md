## Attic Floor Insulation

The attic floor insulation methodology in this measure package is also reused from an existing measure, and the background information is restated here (Phoung, Lou and White 2026). Insulating the attic floor reduces heat transfer between the living space and the unconditioned attic. In colder climates, this keeps heat inside during winter. In warmer climates, it helps keep the living space cooler. The effectiveness of insulation is measured by its R-value; a higher R-value indicates better resistance to heat flow. Installing insulation in existing homes typically involves blowing loose-fill insulation over the attic floor, especially in unfinished attics. The optimal insulation level depends on the climate zone and the home's existing insulation. Refer to the attic insulation documentation for guidance on recommended R-values (as established by International Energy Conservation Code) and installation approaches (Phoung, Lou and White 2026).
