## 5  Results

This section provides an overview of results from the 'EV Adoption With L1 Charging' measure package and the baseline scenario. The results are presented to illustrate the measure's performance and ensure confidence in its implementation, not to reflect a realistic scenario on its own. The measure is designed to offer the users flexibility when combined with the baseline or other measures. As such, results presented here should be interpreted with caution.

Although this section presents the energy and unmet driving hour impacts of the measure, the full set of outputs available in the dataset includes annual and time series charging energy, charging emissions, utility bills, and EV schedules. Section 5.1 shows the overall stock energy consumption, Section 5.2 displays the unmet driving hours, and Section 5.3 shows samples of time series data.
