## Accessing Results

This documentation covers the 'Electric Vehicle (EV) Adoption With Level 1 (L1) Charging' measure methodology and briefly discusses key results from a national-scale ResStock TM simulation. All results can be accessed on the ResStock Open Energy Data Initiative End-Use Load Profiles for the U.S. Building Stock Data Lake and on the data viewer at resstock.nlr.gov.
