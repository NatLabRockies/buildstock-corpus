## 5.3  Peak Considerations

Figure 7 shows the electricity demand for the state of Colorado over 3 days in the winter (left) and 3 days in the summer (right). The dashed line is the demand from the baseline scenario. This measure results in new electricity peaks coinciding with the average time an occupant returns home and aligns closely with the timing of the baseline daily peaks in both seasons. While L1 charging will have lower peak demand than L2 charging, there is still a significant increase due to the full adoption of EVs in the measure. Note that this measure is not meant to reflect a realistic scenario on its own. Rather, it is designed to offer the users flexibility when combined with the baseline or other measures. As such, results should be interpreted with caution and not assumed to reflect real-world outcomes.

Figure 7. Electricity demand in Colorado for the measure over 3 days in the winter (left) and 3 days in the summer (right).
