## 5.2  Average Unmet Driving Hours

Figure 6 displays the average unmet driving hours for each VMT value and percentage charged at home. Unmet driving hours occur when battery charging is not sufficient for the EV's energy demand and are primarily a result of modeling limitations. The most important factors determining unmet driving hours are charger level, VMT, and percent charged at home. Charger level influences the rate of charging possible, while VMT and percent charged at home determine the amount of charging needed. Figure 6 highlights this trend, where only vehicles with high VMT and a high rate of home charging incur unmet driving hours. The increase in this measure is due to the larger fraction of homes with L1 chargers, which are less likely to meet the vehicle's energy demand. However, vehicles with high VMT are relatively uncommon, and most vehicles in the stock experience no unmet driving hours. Across the entire stock, this measure results in an average of 2.37 unmet driving hours, affecting 6.61% of homes.
