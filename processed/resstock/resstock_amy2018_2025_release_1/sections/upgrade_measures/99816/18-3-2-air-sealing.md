## 3.2  Air Sealing

In ResStock, building characteristics are translated to OS-HPXML parameters using the options\_lookup.tsv file. Measure upgrades, such as infiltration, are modeled as changes to baseline building characteristics. This section outlines the technical details of how infiltration upgrades are implemented in the ResStock workflow.
