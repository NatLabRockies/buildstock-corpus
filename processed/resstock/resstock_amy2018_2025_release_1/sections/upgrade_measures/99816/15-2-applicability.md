## 2  Applicability

Dwelling units that have 10 ACH50 or greater infiltration rates are applicable for this light air sealing measure.

Table 1. Apply Logic for Light Air Sealing

| Climate Zone   | Infiltration   | Target Infiltration   | Stock Saturation   |
|----------------|----------------|-----------------------|--------------------|
| All            | ≥10 ACH50      | 25% reduction         | 77.66%             |

Table 2. Percentage of Dwelling Units by Vintage per Infiltration Rate Group in the Baseline Stock

| Infiltration Rate   | < 1940   | 1940-1979   | 1980-1999   | 2000+   |
|---------------------|----------|-------------|-------------|---------|
| < 5                 | 0.11%    | 0.37%       | 1.42%       | 2.53%   |
| 5 ≤ X < 10          | 0.78%    | 3.37%       | 6.72%       | 7.05%   |
| 10 ≤ X < 20         | 3.58%    | 13.46%      | 11.56%      | 6.92%   |
| 20 ≤ X              | 8.19%    | 23.90%      | 7.76%       | 2.26%   |

In general, the baseline of ResStock 2025 Release 1 shows that dwelling units built in the 19401979 range have the highest levels of applicability, followed by dwelling units built in 19801999.
