| Measure Title | Light Air Sealing |
|---|---|
| Measure Definition | This measure reduces infiltration rates by 25% in existing dwelling units with baseline infiltration levels of 10 air changes per hour at 50 pascals (ACH50) or higher. |
| Applicability | Dwelling units with infiltration rates of ≥10 ACH50 are eligible for an upgrade. With these applicability constraints, the measure upgrade applies to 77.66% of the existing dwelling units. |
| Not Applicable | This measure does not apply to existing dwelling units with infiltration rates below 10 ACH50. These constraints exclude 22.34% of dwelling units from receiving this measure upgrade. |
