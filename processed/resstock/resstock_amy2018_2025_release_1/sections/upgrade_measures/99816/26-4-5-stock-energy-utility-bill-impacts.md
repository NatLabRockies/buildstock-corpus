## 4.5  Stock Energy Utility Bill Impacts

Figure 13 shows the modeled energy utility bills savings across each fuel bill with the light air sealing upgrade applied. For the applicable units, the average energy bill savings are 108 USD/unit/year (4%), with a reduction of 45 USD/unit/year (2%) in electricity bills, 39 USD/unit/year (7%) in natural gas bills, 13 USD/unit/year (10%) in fuel oil bills, and 10 USD/unit/year (9%) in propane bills.

Figure 13. Modeled average annual energy utility bill savings by fuel type comparison for light air sealing upgrade

Figure 14 shows the modeled average annual energy utility bill savings by baseline infiltration rate. As with site energy savings, higher average savings are seen in buildings with higher baseline infiltration rates. Buildings starting with 50 ACH50 demonstrate nearly $100 more in annual bill savings compared with buildings starting with 10 ACH50.
