## 4.1  Single Model Results

This section analyzes three dwelling unit models that received the light air sealing measure. Information about each of the three models is shown in Table 4.

Table 4. Baseline Individual Dwelling Unit Characteristics

|                                              | Connecticut Model          | Florida Model                 | Texas Model                 |
|----------------------------------------------|----------------------------|-------------------------------|-----------------------------|
| Location                                     | New Haven County, CT       | Duval County, FL              | Travis County, TX           |
| Building America Climate Zone Classification | Cold                       | Hot-humid                     | Hot-humid                   |
| Building Type                                | Multifamily with 2-4 units | Single-family detached        | Multi-family with 5+ units  |
| Heating System and Fuel                      | Natural gas shared heating | Electric furnace              | Electric furnace            |
| Air Conditioning System                      | Room air conditioning      | Central air conditioning      | Central air conditioning    |
| Starting Infiltration Rate                   | 25 ACH50                   | 40 ACH50                      | 40 ACH50                    |
| Wall Type and Insulation Level               | Wood stud and uninsulated  | CMU, 6-in Hollow, Uninsulated | Brick, 12-in, 3-wythe, R-19 |

These models differ by location, building type, initial infiltration rate, and wall type/insulation level. Due to these and other model differences, their simulated results will vary.
