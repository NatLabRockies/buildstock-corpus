## 1.1  Technical Measure Details

Only dwelling units that have an infiltration rate of 10 ACH50 or greater are assumed to be applicable for this 'Light Air Sealing' measure. That is because this ResStock measure does not add mechanical ventilation to dwelling units with a low infiltration rate. Whole-home mechanical ventilation should be considered when the infiltration rate is 5 ACH50 or less according to the 2015 International Residential Code (ICC Digital Codes 2024). This 10 ACH50 cutoff is due to natural ventilation requirements that homes generally receive at least 0.35 air changes per hour (ASHRAE 2016, International Code Council 2021). 1 A 25% reduction in infiltration of a 10 ACH50 dwelling will reduce its ACH50 value to 7.5 ACH50. The 'LBL factor' (developed by Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory) is a factor based on climate region and other characteristics, and is used to convert estimated air changes in a building by natural means without a fan to the air changes per hour at 50 Pa (ACH50). Using an LBL factor of 20, the lowest allowable infiltration level without forcing natural ventilation into the house is 7 ACH50 (ENERGY STAR 2001).

1 At least 0.35 air changes per hour, but not less than 15 cubic feet of air per minute per person (International Code Council 2021). See ASHRAE 2016 for the specific requirements and equations for calculating required ventilation.
