## Measure Motivation

Utilities, auto manufacturers, charging providers, and EV supply equipment manufacturers are working together to develop technologies (such as smart chargers) and services (such as time-of use rates) that enable efficient EV-grid integration. With potential continued EV adoption (over 10% of sales in the United States in 2024 [IEA 2025] and 25.3% of sales in California [California Energy Commission 2025] in 2024), coordination between the residential energy sector, transportation sector, and electric grid will play a key role in maintaining affordability and reliability. Without such coordination, increases in peak demand may require costly grid upgrades, increase electricity prices, and increase the risk of outages or degraded service.

This ResStock ™ (Reyna et al. 2025) measure applies one EV with a 15% efficiency improvement and an L2 charger to each occupied dwelling unit model in the baseline scenario and enables DF for all the chargers so that they suspend charging during peak periods.  The selected efficiency improvement reflects the average of expected fuel economy changes over the next 5 to 10 years for 300-mile and 400-mile range compact, small SUV, and midsize SUV vehicles in the 2024 Annual Technology Baseline data 1 . All existing units with level 1 (L1) chargers are upgraded to L2.  By modeling widespread adoption of EVs, chargers, and DF, ResStock users can combine models from the baseline, relevant measures without DF, and this measure to create their own unique EV and DF saturation scenarios. This allows nuanced scenarios in which only segments of households adopt EVs or shift their charging load. Such a method can be used to model EV and charger adoption scenarios and examine the resulting energy and power impacts on the residential building sector. The DF implementation in this measure helps quantify the benefit of a DF program and can contribute to alleviating the potential stress that a local electric grid may incur due to the charging of EVs at peak hours.

[1 For more information, visit https://atb.nlr.gov/transportation/2024/battery\_electric](https://atb.nlr.gov/transportation/2024/battery_electric)
