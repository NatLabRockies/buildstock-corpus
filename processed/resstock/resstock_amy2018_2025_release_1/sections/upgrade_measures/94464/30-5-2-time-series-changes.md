## 5.2  Time Series Changes

Figure 10 shows the electricity demand for the state of Colorado over 3 days in the winter (left) and 3 days in the summer (right). The dashed line is the demand from the baseline scenario. The full adoption of efficient EVs with L2 chargers increases the demand, however, during the peak hours, the demand is reduced below the baseline value as EV charging is suspended.
