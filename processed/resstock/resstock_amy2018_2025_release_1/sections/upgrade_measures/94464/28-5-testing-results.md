## 5  Testing Results

This section reports results from the national-scale ResStock run, demonstrating the functionality of the measure and verifying its correct operation. The analysis is presented to illustrate the measure's performance and ensure confidence in its implementation, not to reflect a realistic scenario on its own. The measure is designed to offer the users flexibility when combined with the baseline or other measures. As such, results presented here should be interpreted with caution.

We analyzed this measure and three other scenarios as points of comparison, as summarized in Table 5. The baseline represents the ResStock baseline, which includes a low saturation of EVs, whereas the 'EV + L2' scenario models standard efficiency EVs and excludes demand flexibility, and the 'Efficient EV + L2' scenario excludes demand flexibility only. These comparison points help to highlight the impacts of more efficient EVs and the flexible charging schedules. Although this section presents the energy and unmet driving hour impacts of the measure, the full set of outputs available in the dataset includes annual and time series charging energy, charging emissions, utility bills, and EV schedules.

Table 5. Summary of the Three Scenarios Modeled To Analyze the Results of This Measure

| Scenario                              | EV Assigned to All   | Charger Assigned to All   | Efficient EV   | DF Enabled   |
|---------------------------------------|----------------------|---------------------------|----------------|--------------|
| Baseline                              | No                   | No                        | No             | No           |
| EV + L2                               | Yes                  | L2                        | No             | No           |
| Efficient EV + L2                     | Yes                  | L2                        | Yes            | No           |
| Efficient EV + L2 + DF (this measure) | Yes                  | L2                        | Yes            | Yes          |
