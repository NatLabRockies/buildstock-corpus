## Accessing Results

This documentation covers the 'attic floor insulation for unfinished attics' measure upgrade methodology and briefly discusses key results. All results can be accessed on the ResStock Open Energy Data Initiative 'End-Use Load Profiles for the U.S. Building Stock' data lake and on the data viewer at resstock.nlr.gov.
