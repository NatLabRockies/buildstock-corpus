## 1  Technology Summary

Attic floor insulation for unfinished attics is designed to reduce heat transfer between the unconditioned attic space and the conditioned living areas below. This thermal separation helps improve energy efficiency, lower heating and cooling costs, enhance indoor comfort, and manage moisture within the home. This section provides an overview of the insulation materials commonly used for attic floors and outlines the recommended R-values based on U.S. climate zones, as established by the International Energy Conservation Code (IECC). These specifications inform the insulation levels included in this measure upgrade.
