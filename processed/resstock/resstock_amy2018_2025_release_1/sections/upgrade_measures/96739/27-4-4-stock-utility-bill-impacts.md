## 4.4  Stock Utility Bill Impacts

Figure 18 shows a comparison of utility bills with the attic floor insulation upgrade applied. For the applicable units, the total bill savings are 44 USD/unit/year (1.7%), with a reduction of 23 USD/unit/year (1.3%) in electricity bills and 13 USD/unit/year (2.5%) in natural gas bills. This is similar to the magnitude in overall stock level site energy savings.

Figure 18. Average annual utility bills by fuel type comparison for baseline compared to upgrade

Figure 19 shows the average utility bill savings by baseline insulation level. As with site energy savings, there is a wide distribution of bill savings, with higher average savings from buildings with lowest baseline insulation level.
