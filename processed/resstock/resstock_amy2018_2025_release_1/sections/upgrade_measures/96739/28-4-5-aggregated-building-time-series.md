## 4.5  Aggregated Building Time Series

Colorado is selected as a case study to analyze the aggregated time-series data. Out of all national dwelling units, 1.7% are in Colorado. Figure 20 presents the aggregated average daily baseline energy consumption in Colorado, with Feb. 20 identified as the day with the highest energy use. To further explore this peak, Figure 21 examines the aggregated average power on Feb. 20 in Colorado, comparing conditions before and after the attic floor insulation for unfinished attic upgrade.

Consumption is highest and most variable in winter, peaking near 780 kBtu in February, and flat at about 110 to 130 kBtu from June to September.

Figure 21 shows that the attic floor insulation upgrade scenario consistently results in lower power demand throughout the day compared to the baseline, particularly during peak heating hours. The peak demand decreases from 12.9 kW per unit to 12.7 kW per unit, representing a 2% reduction.
