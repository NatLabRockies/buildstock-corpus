The L1 methods use a single pulse per season (heating/cooling) and have been described in (Bose 1985)-which also describes the L1 tools used by the International Ground Source Heat Pump Association (IGSHPA) method (Oklahoma State University 1988)-and (S. P. Kavanaugh 1991) (S. Kavanaugh 1995). As such, the L1 methods also apply the concept of design days and apply the 'worst case scenario' conditions for designing the ground heat exchanger. More sophisticated methods than the L1 methods are now available (Ahmadfard 2019).
