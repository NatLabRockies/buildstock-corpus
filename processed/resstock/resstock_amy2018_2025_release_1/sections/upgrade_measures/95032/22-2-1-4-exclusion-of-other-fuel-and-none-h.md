## 2.1.4 Exclusion of 'Other Fuel' and 'None' Heating Fuels

Dwellings with 'Other Fuel' or 'None' heating fuels do not receive this measure upgrade because retrofits to these dwellings can lead to confusing results (e.g., false negative savings compared to baseline or installing heating in regions that do not need it) and the number of dwellings this affects is relatively small. 'Other Fuel' includes fuels like wood and coal, which are not currently represented in ResStock, so there is not an accurate baseline heating energy consumption to compare to. Additionally, there is not a large impact at scale since dwellings with 'Other Fuel' or 'None' heating fuels comprise approximately 2.9% of the total building stock. The 'Other Fuel' in ResStock is largely composed of solar heating systems. ResStock does not model solar heating systems.
