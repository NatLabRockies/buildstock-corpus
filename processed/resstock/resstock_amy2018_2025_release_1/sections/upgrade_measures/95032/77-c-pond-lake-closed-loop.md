## c. Pond/Lake Closed Loop

If the installation site has adequate access to a body of water, such as a pond or lake, a surface water system may be the most suitable option. A supply line pipe is run underground from the building to the water and coiled into circles at least 8 ft under the surface to prevent freezing. There is no specific way or orientation of looping for this type of system, but the coils should only be placed in water source that meets minimum volume, depth, and quality requirements (U.S. DOE. 2023). Rivers are not ideal for this application due to their unpredictable behaviors, including flooding and droughts that can damage loop systems, as well as hazards due to moving debris (Omer 2008), (Ma 2004).
