## Presence of Residential GHP Equipment Types in the Residential Building Stock

A review of GHP deployments was performed in 2013 as part of a market analysis (Battocletti &amp; Glassley, 2013). The study surveyed manufacturers, geothermal loop installers, and mechanical equipment installers. The survey of residential mechanical equipment installers resulted in the distribution of equipment types seen in Figure A-6. Water-to-air heat pumps made up the largest fraction of residential GHP mechanical equipment installations at 77%. If the combined systems are also included, as they have a water-to-air component, this fraction increases to 87%. Waterto-water and direct geo-exchange systems made up a lower fraction of installations at 8% and 5%, respectively.
