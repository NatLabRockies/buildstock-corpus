## 1. Open-Loop Systems

Open-loop systems use a well or a surface body water (like a local pond or lake) directly as heat exchange fluid that circulates through the GHP system. Feasibility of the use of open-loop systems, however, depends on location, local codes, and regulations (U.S. DOE. 2023).
