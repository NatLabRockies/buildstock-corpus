## Residential Building Stock Characterization of GHP Installations

Characterizing the GHP installations in the U.S. residential building stock helps identify segments of the stock that have GHPs and segments of the stock that have not been the focus of the GHP market. This characterization focuses on the number of GHP installations, where they are located, in what type of buildings, when the buildings were built, and some recent trends in GHP installations.
