## Number of Residential GHP Installations and Their Locations

There are approximately 139 million residential housing units in the U.S. (U.S. Census 2021). Of these housing units, 124 million housing units are occupied. There are approximately 1.28 -2 million housing units with GHPs as of 2015 -2016 (Table A-1), approximately 1 -1.6% of occupied residential housing units. Recently, the U.S. DOE released a report, GeoVision (DOE, 2019), that discusses the untapped potential of geothermal energy both from the generation side and for GHPs. The GeoVision report similarly estimates that GHPs represent about 1% of the U.S. HVAC market (DOE, 2019).

Table A-1. An Estimate of the Number of GHP Installations in the U.S.

| Data source                                                                        | Number of Housing Units with GHPs   | Fraction of Occupied Residential Stock   |
|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| Energy Information Administration (EIA) 2016 Annual Energy Outlook (U.S. EIA 2016) | 1.28 million                        | 1%                                       |
| J. W. Lund and T. L. Boyd, 2016 (Lund and Boyd 2016)                               | 2 million                           | 1.6%                                     |
| ENERGY STAR Shipment Data, 2003- 2015 (ENERGY STAR 2022)                           | 1.66 million                        | 1.3%                                     |
| EIA's 2020 Residential Energy Consumption Survey (RECS) (U.S. EIA 2023)            | 1.27 million                        | 1%                                       |

According to combined data from ENERGY STAR (ENERGY STAR 2022) and J. Jaewan et al. (Jaewan, et al. 2021), GHP shipments increased steadily from 2003 to 2015 (Figure A-1). The number of GHP shipments appears to have leveled out since 2015. There is some inconsistency between the estimates in Table A-1 and the ENERGY STAR shipment and J. Jaewan et al. sources. The total number of reported ENERGY STAR GHP shipments between 2003 and 2015 is 1.66 million units, whereas J. Jaewan et al. estimate shipments of 2.6 million units between 2003 and 2021. The 1.66 million units between 2003 and 2015 does not include GHP units installed before 2003, while the largest estimate from Table A-2 is 2 million.

According to data from EIA 2020 RECS (U.S. EIA 2023), the majority of the residential GHP installations are in the eastern and southern portions of the U.S. (Figure A-2). The largest number of residential GHPs are in Florida, Tennessee, North Carolina, Alabama, and Texas (Figure A-3). These states make up almost 40% of the residential GHP installations. Around 95% of the GHPs are in the IECC 2012 moist climate zones, 2A-5A. Climate zone 4A has the most GHP installations, with about 30% of the total residential GHP installations.
