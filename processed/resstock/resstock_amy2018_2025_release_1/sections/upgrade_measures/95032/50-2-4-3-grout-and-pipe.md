## 2.4.3 Grout and Pipe

For both the grout type and pipe type, HPXML provides inputs as 'standard' or 'thermally enhanced.' OpenStudio-HPXML uses grout conductivity of 0.75 Btu/h-ft-°F for standard grout (IGSPHA 2014) and 1.2 Btu/h-ft-°F 26 for thermally enhanced grout (Liu, et al. 2021). OpenStudio-HPXML uses pipe conductivity of 0.23 Btu/h-ft-°F for standard pipe and 0.4 Btu/hft-°F for thermally enhanced pipe (Liu, et al. 2021). Per conversations with industry, systems being installed today typically use thermally enhanced grout and pipes. The GHP measures use 'thermally enhanced' grout and pipe conductivities.
