## 2.4.4 Single-Stage GHP Heating and Cooling Efficiency

The compressor type significantly impacts the EER and COP of a GHP by affecting how efficiently the system transfers heat and matches the load. Two-stage and variable-speed compressors can fine-tune this process, reducing energy losses and improving heat exchange efficiency, especially under partial load conditions. Single-stage compressors use more energy through frequent on-off cycles. These extra cycles require more energy to start up and cause inefficient operation, either by overworking or underworking, leading to temperature fluctuations and unnecessary energy use.

ENERGY STAR ®  provides a list of ENERGY STAR qualified products for GHPs. The research team downloaded the 2025 Most Efficient GHP list. 27 From the dataset, only the closed-loop water-to-air heat pump systems were selected. Model duplicates and systems with no EER or COP values were removed from the dataset before processing. After the filtering there were 694 systems in the dataset, and 195 were single-stage systems. The manufacturer websites were used to identify if each of the models listed in the ENERGY STAR 2025 most efficient list had a single-stage, two-stage, or variable-speed compressor. Among the products in the database, the range of EER and COP were calculated based on the compressor type, shown in Table 9 and Table 10.

Using the data from ENERGY STAR 2025 Most Efficient GHP list and the processed metrics in Table 9 and Table 10, the median values were chosen for the single-stage upgrade GHP measure inputs: heating efficiency=3.8 COP, cooling efficiency=18.6 EER.
