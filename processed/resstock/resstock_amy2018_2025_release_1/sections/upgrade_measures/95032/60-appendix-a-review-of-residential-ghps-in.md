## Appendix A: Review of Residential GHPs in the U.S. Building Stock

A review of GHPs in the U.S. residential building stock was performed as part of this work. The review covered how GHPs have been installed in the U.S. residential building stock. The outcome of the review helps target the modeling improvements and helps answer the following questions:

1. How many GHP systems exist in the residential building stock, where are they located, and in what type of buildings have the systems been installed?
2. What is the most common ground heat exchanger configuration?
3. How common are water-to-water, water-to-air, and direct geo-exchange systems?
4. How have ENERGY STAR GHPs improved in recent years and how many units have been shipped?
