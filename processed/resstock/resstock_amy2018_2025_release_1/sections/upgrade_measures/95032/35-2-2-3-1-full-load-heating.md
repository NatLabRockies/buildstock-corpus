Figure 4: colour-scaled grid of full load heating capacity. Rows are return air temperature (Tair), 10 to 27.5°C, and columns ground exit water temperature (Twater), 0 to 30°C, in 2.5°C steps; no unit is printed. Capacity rises with water temperature and falls slightly with air temperature: 5650 at 10°C air and 0°C water, 10073 at 10°C air and 30°C water, and 4918 at 27.5°C air and 0°C water.
