## 2.1.2  Exclusion of High-Rise Shared HVAC Dwellings

High-rise (8+ stories) dwellings with shared HVAC are excluded from this upgrade measure because the outdoor units would have to be mounted directly outside the unit in the high-rise building, creating a uniquely complicated maintenance environment. 3 Due to the current unit-based multifamilty modeling approach in ResStock, these buildings are also complicated to accurately model the application of a GHP upgrade. Because these dwellings only comprise 1% of the total building stock, this housing segment was not upgraded.

3 Multifamily units without shared HVAC systems are included in the upgrade regardless of the number of stories in the building.
