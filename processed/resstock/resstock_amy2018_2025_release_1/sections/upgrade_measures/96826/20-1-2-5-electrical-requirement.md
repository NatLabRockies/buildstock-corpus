## 1.2.5 Electrical Requirement

The HPWH options considered in this measure require a 208/240V circuit protected by a doublepole breaker, which typically requires two breaker spaces. The equipment power rating is driven by the size of the backup elements, which are typically 4,500 W and require a 30A breaker (AOSmith, 2026). This information is used in ResStock to assess whether the home's electrical panel would be constrained in terms of space or capacity due to this upgrade.
