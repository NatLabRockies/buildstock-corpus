## Where:

Q\_ft : Heating output capacity modifier based on temperature

COP\_fT : Nominal coefficient of performance modifier based on temperature.

Tair : Wet bulb temperature of air entering the heat pump

Twater : Tank water temperature adjacent to the condenser

a-j : Curve coefficients

Curve coefficients were derived based on detailed laboratory testing that captured performance under a wide range of conditions. Values used here are:

a : 1.1332

b : 0.063

c : -0.0000979

d : -0.00972

e : -0.0000214

f : -0.00069

g: 0.563

h: 0.0437

i: 0.000039

j: -0.00015

k: -0.00015
