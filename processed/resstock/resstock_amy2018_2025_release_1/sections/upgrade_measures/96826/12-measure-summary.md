| Measure Title | Heat Pump Water Heater |
|---|---|
| Measure Definition | This measure installs a 240V HPWH to all dwelling units that are not currently using a HPWH or a solar thermal water heater. |
| Applicability | This measure applies to all dwellings with one of the following water heaters: • Electric resistance storage or tankless • Fuel storage or tankless. With these constraints, the measure applies to 99.5% of existing dwelling units. |
| Not Applicable | This measure does not apply to dwelling units with one of the following water heaters: • Heat pump • Solar thermal. These constraints exclude 0.5% of dwelling units from receiving this measure upgrade. |
