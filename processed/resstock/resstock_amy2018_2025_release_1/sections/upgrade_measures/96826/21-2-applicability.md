## 2  Applicability

This measure replaces existing water heaters with 240V HPWHs for dwelling units without a HPWH or solar thermal. HPWH tank size is determined according to the number of bedrooms and number of occupants, as shown in Table 3. The HPWH upgrade is done in place without a location change. Based on the above assumption, 99.5% of housing stock receives the HPWH upgrade, as shown in Table 4. Note, 'other fuel' and 'wood' water heaters are mapped to coal and wood cord energy consumption in the output, respectively. These fuel types are excluded from the published energy results due to low confidence in the estimates, meaning they appear in the housing characteristics but not in the reported energy consumption. As a result, homes with water heaters of these fuel types will appear to show an increase in site energy consumption that does not reflect real energy changes from the upgrade.
