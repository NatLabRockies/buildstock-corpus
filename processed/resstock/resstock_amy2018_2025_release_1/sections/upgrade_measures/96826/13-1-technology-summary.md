## 1  Technology Summary

This measure models installing a high-efficiency 240V HPWH into all U.S. homes that are not already using a heat pump or solar thermal for water heating. The equipment has a uniform energy factor (UEF) no less than 3.3 and a first hour rating (FHR) no less than 45 gal/h.
