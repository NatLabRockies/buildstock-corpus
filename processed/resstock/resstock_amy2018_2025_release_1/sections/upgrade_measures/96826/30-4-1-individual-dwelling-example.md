## 4.1  Individual Dwelling Example

This section analyzes three dwelling units that upgraded their electric storage water heater to a HPWH. Table 7 provides key housing characteristics of the dwelling unit samples. The samples selected are in Hawai'i (HI, Climate Zone 1A), California (CA, Climate Zone 3B), and Alaska (AK, Climate Zone 7), representing a range of climate conditions. To control for major factors that influence hot water demand and water heater sizing, all three dwellings are occupied by four occupants across four bedrooms with an average usage of hot water fixtures, clothes washer, and dishwasher. As a result of the bedroom and bathroom counts, they have a 50-gal electric storage water heater in the baseline and received an 80-gal HPWH for upgrade. Note the water heater location is selected to show the extent of HPWH backup usage and controlled for comparison purposes. It is rare to have a water heater, especially a HPWH, installed in the garage in AK.
