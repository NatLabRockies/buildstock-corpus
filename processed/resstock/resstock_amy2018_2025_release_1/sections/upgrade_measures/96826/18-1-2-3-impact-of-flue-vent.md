## 1.2.3 Impact of Flue Vent

All fuel-based water heaters need to pull in air for combustion and require a way to safely vent the exhaust. This additional vent can add to the overall infiltration of the house and needs to be balanced to prevent dangerous back-drafting of the flue gas (Moffatt, 1991). Replacing fuelbased water heaters with HPWHs can improve the airtightness of the house and reduce space heating and cooling demand when their flue vent is eliminated. ResStock assumes that the water heater and the space heating system share a flue vent when both systems are in the conditioned space and require ventilation, as indicated by the parameter 'Has Flue or Chimney In Conditioned Space' in

Table 6 in Section 3.1. Therefore, the measure can remove the fuel water heater flue vent and reduce infiltration only when the flue vent is standalone and the existing space heating system does not require ventilation.
