**Measure Summary, part 1 of 2**

| Measure Title | Natural Gas Water Heater |
|---|---|
| Measure Definition | This measure installs a high-efficiency condensing natural gas tankless water heater to all dwelling units that are not currently using solar thermal, heat pump, or gas tankless water heaters and have access to gas. |
| Applicability | This measure applies to all dwellings that have natural gas space heating, water heating, cooking, clothes drying, pool heating, or spa heating, and one of the following water heaters: • Electric resistance storage or tankless water heater • Natural gas storage water heater • Non-natural gas fuel storage or tankless water heater. With these constraints, the measure applies to 55.7% of existing dwelling units. Note: Natural gas lighting, fireplaces, and grills exist in ResStock, but they are random and were not used to determine gas access. |
| Not Applicable | This measure does not apply to dwelling units with one of the following characteristics: • Heat pump water heater • Solar thermal water heater • Natural gas tankless water heater • Does not have access to natural gas based on space heating, water heating, cooking, a dryer, a pool heater, or a spa heater all using other fuels. These constraints exclude 44.3% of dwelling units from receiving this measure upgrade. |
