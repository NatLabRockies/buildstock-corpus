## 2  Applicability

This measure installs a high-efficiency condensing natural gas tankless water heater to all homes that are not currently using a solar thermal, heat pump, or gas tankless water heater and have access to gas. This includes homes already using natural gas for water heating, space heating, cooking, clothes drying, pool heating, or hot tub/spa heating. Although ResStock models gas fireplaces, grills, and lighting, their presence is infrequent and random and thus not included in the gas access evaluation. Based on this assumption, 55.7% of the housing stock receives a high-efficiency condensing natural gas tankless water heater upgrade, as shown in Table 3. Note that 'other fuel' and 'wood' water heaters are mapped to coal and wood cord energy consumption in the output, respectively. These fuel types are excluded from the published energy results due to low confidence in the estimates, meaning they appear in the housing characteristics but not in the reported energy consumption. As a result, homes with water heaters of these fuel types will appear to show an increase in site energy consumption that does not reflect real energy changes from the upgrade.
