## 4.1  Single Building Example

This section analyzes three dwelling units that upgrade the natural gas storage water heaters to condensing natural gas tankless water heaters with a UEF of 0.98. Table 6 provides key housing characteristics of the dwelling unit samples. The samples selected are in Hawai'i (HI; Climate Zone 1A), California (CA; Climate Zone 3B), and Alaska (AK; Climate Zone 7AK), representing a range of climate conditions. The samples also vary in terms of attributes that influence the hot water demand, including the number of occupants, the hot water fixture usage (100% represents average), and the hot water appliances. Note that the water heater location is not controlled because the interaction between a tankless water heater and the heating, ventilation, and air conditioning is limited. Although it is possible to have a water heater installed in the garage in Alaska, it is rare.
