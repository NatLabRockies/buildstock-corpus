## 4.2.2  Stock Aggregated Time-Series Impact

To illustrate the impact of the natural gas tankless water heater measure on the aggregated load profile, Figure 9 presents the modeled total residential stock electricity profile before and after the measure for Hawai'i, California, and Alaska during their respective peak days in 2018. The measure only slightly reduced the system electric peak across the three states because it replaces some electric resistance water heating with gas. For Hawai'i, the upgrade reduced the electric peak on Sept. 30, 2018, 16:30:00 local time, from 0.78 GW by 2.0%. For California, the upgrade reduced the electric peak on July 7, 2018, 17:00:00 local time, from 32.17 GW by 1.5%. For Alaska, the upgrade reduced the electric peak on Feb. 3, 2018, 19:45:00 local time, from 0.43 GW by 0.7%. This measure does not significantly impact the electric system load shape because on average only one in three homes with an electric resistance water heater has access to natural gas, and they are fuel switched in the measure, as shown in Table 3.
